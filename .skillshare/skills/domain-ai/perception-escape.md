# AI · Perception · The escape pool (`escape_pool.gd`, `escape_search.gd`)

Side file of `perception-grenades.md` (the plan, the zone, the safe spot). Spec: `docs/grenades.md` G16.

One escape search cost 2 to 8.5 ms (a snap about 22 us, a verdict about 28, a route about 125, the candidates round a burst 300 to 600), the frames of a grenade in flight in the opening's tail. A search is now a job (`EscapeSearch`: the candidates in their order, the best so far, resumable) against `EscapePool.per_frame` **80 units** a physics frame across every NPC (about 2 ms; a snap 1, a verdict 1, a route 5, the candidates round a burst 16 once: counts of work done, never a clock). `AgentGrenades._begin` makes the search and registers it; `_landed` is its plan once finished. A search is served on every ask, the frame after it began, in this order (a rule, `EscapePool._before`): a plan with none yet before a replan, the grenade that goes off first, the NPC nearest the burst, the search begun first. A search that cannot finish continues next frame; the NPC carries on as it was (no plan yet, or its old one). Its plan is from where the NPC stood and the fuse left when the search began (`from`, `began_fuse` in the plan). A frame spends the pool and at most one candidate more (7 units) and the around of a new burst. A search unasked for `STALE_FRAMES` (30) is dropped; outside a physics frame it runs whole. Latency, zone entry to plan: median 0 / p90 1 / max 3 frames before, median 1 / p90 6 / max 10 after (the grenade tests); against the shortest fuse (a stun, 1.5 s = 90 frames) the max is 11 %. `has_escape` is not pooled; a stack member whose slot is its cover (`Entry.expects_burst`) still plans. Pins: `escape_pool_spreads_plans_test.gd`, `escape_spot_matches_plain_test.gd` (sliced = whole = plain), tests that need a plan the frame it begins set `EscapePool.per_frame` huge (`evade_wave_spots_match_own_plan`).
