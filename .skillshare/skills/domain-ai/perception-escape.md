# AI · Perception · The escape pool (`escape_pool.gd`, `escape_search.gd`)

Side file of `perception-grenades.md` (the plan, the zone, the safe spot). Spec: `docs/grenades.md` G16.

One escape search cost 2 to 8.5 ms (a snap about 22 us, a verdict about 28, a route about 125, the candidates round a burst 300 to 600), the frames of a grenade in flight in the opening's tail. A search is now a job (`EscapeSearch`: the candidates in their order, the best so far, resumable) against `EscapePool.per_frame` **80 units** a physics frame across every NPC (about 2 ms; a snap 1, a verdict 1, a route 5, the candidates round a burst 16 once: counts of work done, never a clock). `AgentGrenades._begin` makes the search and registers it; `_landed` is its plan once finished. A search is served on every ask, the frame after it began, in this order (a rule, `EscapePool._before`): first an **urgent** search, the one begun first of them (its wave stays in the order below); then a plan with none yet before a replan, the grenade that goes off first, the NPC nearest the burst, the search begun first. A search is urgent when it began `AGE_FRAMES` (12) ago or more, or its grenade goes off within `ARRIVE_MARGIN` and `AGE_FRAMES` (36 frames: `EscapePool.urgent`). **The bound on a wait**: a search lands within `AGE_FRAMES` plus the work of every search begun before it or on its frame, over the pool, whatever arrives after (nearer, or with a sooner fuse: an arrival after it is behind it once it is urgent); `escape_pool_bounds_wait` pins it (20 NPCs on two grenades, 34 round one, and a stream of 2 searches a frame the pool cannot keep up with: the far search waits 12 frames, not 130). The pool is work-conserving, and while any search is urgent a frame holds `EscapePool.urgent_extra` (80) more units, twice the rate (a frame of urgent work spends up to 167 units, about 4 ms): a wave of N searches of about 110 units (the mean of a new plan over the ai domain's tests: median 65, p90 232, dearest 620) lands within 12 frames at the pool's rate and then at twice it, N x 0.7 frames, so its last NPC is served before the shortest fuse (a stun, 1.5 s = 90 frames) less the margin (66) up to about 80 NPCs at once. A search that cannot finish continues next frame; the NPC carries on as it was (no plan yet, or its old one). Its plan is from where the NPC stood and the fuse left when the search began (`from`, `began_fuse` in the plan): it lands `w` frames later than it was made for, and `ARRIVE_MARGIN` (0.4 s, 24 frames) is what absorbs that. Thirty defenders and four more round one frag (`escape_pool_spreads_plans`, real searches) waited up to 19 frames at the pool alone, and one of them (a 300 px run planned to arrive 0.4 s before the burst, begun 19 frames late) was caught; with the urgent extra the most is 15 frames and none is. (Planning for the fuse left at landing, by shrinking the run allowed with the search's age, was tried and dropped: the shortest route is the winner whatever the allowance, so it only turned a late runner into one with no spot, who crouches where the burst finds it: 10 of the 34 caught.) A frame serves the pool and at most one candidate more (7 units), and a new burst's candidates cost their 16 units when the search is begun, whatever the pool has left (`EscapePool.peak_served` is the first, `peak_spent` both). A search unasked for `STALE_FRAMES` (30) is dropped, and is not served again if asked about (`serving`: its NPC begins a new one from where it stands with the fuse left); one whose NPC has died or been freed is cancelled (`EscapeSearch.alive`, set by `AgentGrenades._begin`) and spends none of the pool; outside a physics frame a search runs whole. Latency, search begun to plan, over the 90 grenade tests with the 34-NPC wave (272 pooled searches): median 3 / p90 19 / max 21 frames (fresh plans: 2 / 14 / 16); the 34 plan within 15 of knowing the frag; before the pool a plan landed the frame it began. Against the shortest fuse (90 frames) the max is 23 %. `has_escape` is not pooled; a stack member whose slot is its cover (`Entry.expects_burst`) still plans. Pins: `escape_pool_spreads_plans_test.gd`, `escape_pool_bounds_wait_test.gd`, `escape_spot_matches_plain_test.gd` (sliced = whole = plain), `escape_search_owner_test.gd` (a stale search is begun again, a dead NPC's is dropped), and the ones that compare a spot with the plain search carry the plan's `from` and `began_fuse` (`evade_wave_spots_match_own_plan`, at the shipped pool).
