# AI · The beat: an NPC senses and acts one frame in two (`npc_pace.gd`, held as `_reflexes.pace`, #194 perf-cadence)

Side file of `orchestrator.md`. The AI control floor (`movement.pull`, 9.6 to 11 ms a frame over 20 NPCs: sense 4.35, act 4.5) was the cost left in the match guards, and no exact cut reached it (`decision-latency.md` cut the decision, not this). The user chose a per-NPC cadence.

**The rule.** Each NPC runs its SENSE and ACT (`_sense`, `_act` in `goal_controller.gd`) on a **beat**: one frame in `NpcPace.BEAT` (2), on the phase `(WorldRng.frame_of(world) + WorldRng.order_of(NPC)) % 2`. The key is the world's own frame and the NPC's spawn order, never a process count (the replfix rule): a match plays the same three times in a process, and a squad's NPCs fall half on each phase, so the frame's work is even. A frame off the beat is a **coast**: the body goes on (`movement.walk`, `move_and_slide`, facing, the anatomy, the weapon's own clock) with the intent the last beat wrote (`move_input`, `aim_point`, `run_input` stand until written again), and the seconds of the coast are carried (`owed`) into the next beat's `delta`, so every timer a beat counts down (commit, tactic, fire, back-off, guard clock) still counts real time.

**On the beat (the cost cut):** the events' effect on perception beyond the urgent ones aside, `observe` (the sight pass, memory, `house.take_in`, `learn_by_sight`, `grenades.live.observe`), `follow_squad`, `react_to_grenades` (always on the beat: only the evade it starts, `grenades.evading()`, runs every frame), `update_known` and `_check_salient`, then `Behavior.apply` (`set_up_walk` and `walk_bodies`, the primitive: entries, `apply_follow` / `aim_move` / `Watch.travel_point`, `apply_move`, engage, defend), `Watch.apply` and the order's progress. The sight pass counts beats (`PerceptionSight.beat` = `Vector2i(NpcPace.BEAT, NpcPace.SIGHT_EVERY_BEATS)`: 2 frames a beat, 2 beats between two tests): a seen character is held 4 frames old at most (3 before) and a character entering the cone is tested on the NPC's next beat, at most a frame late.

**Every frame (why):** a burst owing a round (`Behavior.coast`: the shot is decided, the gun's own rate sets it); the decision job (`_think`: the pool of work units is a per-frame budget and a forced decision must not wait a beat); the queued events' intake (`_comms.process_events`, so a hit, an order, a callout and a grenade are read the frame they come); `prime` (an ambusher's priority); `service_interaction` and `_take_resolved` (a resolved task forces its decision at once); the body's motion, steering and `move_and_slide`, which read the intent every frame (a direction held one frame stale is 2 to 3 px of walk).

**Wake at once (off the beat, this frame, the whole beat):** the NPC was hit or a hit near it was relevant (`_comms.urgent`: `process_hit`, victim itself), a `grenade` or `explosion` event reached it (a grenade thrown, landing or bursting: the frame the event comes), an `order` reached it, it is flash-blind (`needs_every_frame`) or was last beat (its sight returning forces a decision, G15: `Reflexes.blind_pending`), or `Behavior.needs_every_frame()`: a throw (every stage), a tactical entry (the primitive `entry`: the stack, the pie, the crossing, `committed`; its approach only within `ENTRY_FAR` 200 px of the doorway, or as a guard's detour), a door being worked or detoured, a grenade evade, a primed ambusher (its trigger is read the frame it is due, D37). The phase never moves: a woken NPC is back on its own frames the next one.

**Wakes tried and dropped (measured, 700 frames from first contact of the seed 23 10v10, `control()` timed: 9.1 ms a frame on the old build, 8.3 with the first wake list, 6.2 with this one; 23 % of NPC frames coasted, then 40 %):** (1) the forced-decision flag (`_cadence.forced`): it stays up from a forced decision until the job lands it, tens of frames in a busy pool, and `_think` runs every frame whatever; the events that force a decision on contact (a hit, an order, a grenade) wake through `urgent`, and a belief or task change a beat made is acted on at the next beat; (2) a live grenade merely KNOWN (`grenades.live.has_known()`): a fuse is seconds long and was 1500 NPC-frames of 12600; the grenade's landing, its burst and the throw's events wake the NPC the frame they come, a grenade seen in flight is read on the next beat (at most a frame), and the evade it starts runs every frame (`grenades.evading()`); (3) the entry approach beyond 200 px of its doorway (a long walk a beat carries as well as any: the guard's reads a beat apart move a covering by a few pixels; inside 200 px, and in a detour, the guard and the team's stack timings, `entry_guard_secures_passed_doorway`, `entry_team_flows_in`, `order_stack_waits_for_all`, need every frame). Kept: the primed ambusher (about one NPC of 20).

**What it costs in behaviour (accepted by the user):** a reaction lags up to one frame (17 ms): the first aim at a new sighting, the first shot of a fight (a gun's fire interval rounds up to the NPC's beat when its cooldown is an odd number of frames), the first step of a new walk, a door seen shut. The measured reaction latency and the A/B (perf_ab, the fixed-scenario reaction frames) are in `docs/adr/0019-the-npc-beat.md` (its measured and fixed-scenario paragraphs). The match recordings (`von_recordings`) and the frame pins of the AI tests moved with it; each was re-recorded with this cause.
