# AI · The beat: an NPC senses and acts one frame in two (`npc_pace.gd`, held as `_reflexes.pace`)

Side file of `orchestrator.md`. Sense and act are the largest part of an NPC's control cost in a match (`movement.pull`), so they run on a per-NPC cadence. Why, and the measurements: `docs/adr/0019-the-npc-beat.md`.

**The rule.** Each NPC runs its SENSE and ACT (`_sense`, `_act` in `goal_controller.gd`) on a **beat**: one frame in `NpcPace.BEAT` (2), on the phase `(WorldRng.frame_of(world) + WorldRng.order_of(NPC)) % 2`. The key is the world's own frame and the NPC's spawn order, never a process count (the replfix rule): a match plays the same three times in a process, and a squad's NPCs fall half on each phase, so the frame's work is even. A frame off the beat is a **coast**: the body goes on (`movement.walk`, `move_and_slide`, facing, the anatomy, the weapon's own clock) with the intent the last beat wrote (`move_input`, `aim_point`, `run_input` stand until written again), and the seconds of the coast are carried (`owed`) into the next beat's `delta`, so every timer a beat counts down (commit, tactic, fire, back-off, guard clock) still counts real time.

**On the beat (the cost cut):** the events' effect on perception beyond the urgent ones aside, `observe` (the sight pass, memory, `house.take_in`, `learn_by_sight`, `grenades.live.observe`), `follow_squad`, `react_to_grenades` (always on the beat: only the evade it starts, `grenades.evading()`, runs every frame), `update_known` and `_check_salient`, then `Behavior.apply` (`set_up_walk` and `walk_bodies`, the primitive: entries, `apply_follow` / `aim_move` / `Watch.travel_point`, `apply_move`, engage, defend), `Watch.apply` and the order's progress. The sight pass counts beats (`PerceptionSight.beat` = `Vector2i(NpcPace.BEAT, NpcPace.SIGHT_EVERY_BEATS)`: 2 frames a beat, 2 beats between two tests): a seen character is held 4 frames old at most and a character entering the cone is tested on the NPC's next beat, at most a frame late.

**Every frame (why):** a burst owing a round (`Behavior.coast`: the shot is decided, the gun's own rate sets it); the decision job (`_think`: the pool of work units is a per-frame budget and a forced decision must not wait a beat); the queued events' intake (`_comms.process_events`, so a hit, an order, a callout and a grenade are read the frame they come); `prime` (an ambusher's priority); `service_interaction` and `_take_resolved` (a resolved task forces its decision at once); the body's motion, steering and `move_and_slide`, which read the intent every frame (a direction held one frame stale is 2 to 3 px of walk).

**Wake at once (off the beat, this frame, the whole beat):** the NPC was hit or a hit near it was relevant (`_comms.urgent`: `process_hit`, victim itself), a `grenade` or `explosion` event reached it (a grenade thrown, landing or bursting: the frame the event comes), an `order` reached it, it is flash-blind (`needs_every_frame`) or was last beat (its sight returning forces a decision, G15: `Reflexes.blind_pending`), or `Behavior.needs_every_frame()`: a throw (every stage), a tactical entry (the primitive `entry`: the stack, the pie, the crossing, `committed`; its approach only within `ENTRY_FAR` 200 px of the doorway, or as a guard's detour), a door being worked or detoured, a grenade evade, a primed ambusher (its trigger is read the frame it is due, D37). The phase never moves: a woken NPC is back on its own frames the next one.

**Not wakes** (the primed ambusher, about one NPC of 20, is one): the forced-decision flag (`_cadence.forced`: it stays up from a forced decision until the job lands it, tens of frames in a busy pool; `_think` runs every frame whatever, the events that force a decision on contact wake through `urgent`, and a belief or task change a beat made is acted on at the next); a live grenade merely known (`grenades.live.has_known()`: a fuse is seconds long; its landing, burst and throw events wake the NPC the frame they come, one seen in flight is read on the next beat, and the evade it starts runs every frame, `grenades.evading()`); an entry's approach beyond 200 px of its doorway (a long walk a beat carries as well as any; inside 200 px, and in a detour, the guard and the stack's timings need every frame: `entry_guard_secures_passed_doorway`, `entry_team_flows_in`, `order_stack_waits_for_all`).

**What it costs in behaviour (accepted by the user):** a reaction lags up to one frame (17 ms): the first aim at a new sighting, the first shot of a fight (a gun's fire interval rounds up to the NPC's beat when its cooldown is an odd number of frames), the first step of a new walk, a door seen shut. The measured reaction latency: ADR 0019.
