# AI behaviour policies, orders and reflexes

Policies the executor applies after a primitive steers: where it may act, running, posture, order handling, the grenade evade reflex and the blind NPC. Entered from `behavior.md`.

- **Gaze and fire** are the watch stage (`watch.md`): after the primitive steers, a threat takes the eyes and the gun whatever the primitive is.
- **Where it may act** is perception's `confinement` (`perception.md`), refreshed each tick by the controller's reflexes and asked live: `fight.hold_ground` is set while the contact's spot is somewhere it may not act; Combat asks `allows` itself; `follow` faces away from `leash_center`; the errand walk's keep-out reads `confinement.keep_out`. The planner's COMBAT filter reads the snapshot's frozen copy (`decision.md`); the leaf carries no confinement. `orders.walk_pending()` (a `move` or `engage` not arrived, a `throw` in `walk`) is what the controller's leash break reads, beside `in_combat()` and `committed()`.
- **Running** (`gait.should_run()`) is policy after the watch stage picked its threat: only while TRAVELLING (a `move` that is not a `look_around`, or an `engage`, `defend`, `ambush` or `follow`, not arrived; a `melee` closing in; never an arrived walk, interaction, hold or entry), when the leaf has `run: true` or the NPC is under fire, the destination is farther than `run_min_distance` and a run can start (`run_start_fraction` of stamina, or already running). A `follow` not arrived runs once it is more than `SquadFollowConfig.step` from its live spot and keeps `gait.catching_up` until within `caught_up`; a tick with no step (give-way, keep-off, held) keeps the catch-up. A running character cannot shoot or reload, so it is false while `Combat.can_fire_at(threat)` or `reload_due()`, unless `run_through_fire` (RETREAT). Tunables: `tuning.pace` (`run_enabled`, `run_min_distance`, `run_start_fraction`). Tests: `tests/ai/movement/ai_runs_to_destination_test.gd`, `retreat_keeps_running_test.gd`.
- **Posture** (`gait.apply_posture`, every tick, through `movement.set_posture`; spec `docs/cover-and-stance.md` A1 to A3): travelling, outside a fight and running stand (only a standing body runs); fighting in place crouches. An `ambush` at its post (LD15) and a member of a frag entry holding its slot (`Entry.holds_crouched()`, LD14) crouch outside a fight. A crouch is held `posture_dwell`; standing up is never delayed. Not a decision-tree level, so it works with the server down. Tunables: `tuning.pace` (`posture_enabled`, `posture_dwell`). Tests: `tests/ai/posture/`.
- **Orders** (`docs/leadership.md`). A leaf carrying `order_id` radios `moving` when adopted and `in_position` on arrival (`orders.take_steps()` hands `{ order_id, step }` to the controller, LD7). `orders.primed()` is the ambusher at its post, which the reflexes prime every tick (D37). `debug_state()["order"]` is the order leaf being carried out (`paused` behind an entry detour). **An order stops a walk, not a fight (LD6):** from the frame an order is taken (`orders.await_next(true)`) until the decision made knowing it lands, `apply` zeroes `move_input` after the primitive steers while `_walking()` and not `committed()`: a `move` not chosen under COMBAT, an `entry` in `approach`, a `defend` / `ambush` / `follow` not arrived, a `throw` in `walk`, an `interact` still walking. Fights (`engage`, `melee`, `reload`, `hold`, a `move` chosen under COMBAT), the rest of an entry, an evade and a committed step are never touched. Tests `ai/decision/order_stops_walk`, `order_fight_goes_on`, `order_retreat_goes_on`. The ordered stack (LD9) and the join (LD24, LD25) are an `entry` leaf with the order's params: `Orders.ordered_entry(params)` (an `order_id` and no `join`) gates `ordered_stack()` and the held-back way reading; `StackClaim.is_join(params)` marks a join, which keeps its `order_id` so `orders.same`, `_live` and the `moving` radio treat it as the member's order but is not an ordered stack. A join over while the entry is in `approach` or `stack` resolves the task ("join over", `orders.join_over()`), so the member follows again. A frag entry's thrower radios `frag_out` through `grenades.take_throw_calls` (`grenades.poll_throw` takes `Entry.take_thrown()`). The rest is `entry.md`.

## Evade reflex (G16) and the blind NPC (G15)

- **Evade** (`grenades.set_evade(plan)`, fed every tick from the perception's `grenades.evade_plan`; `docs/grenades.md` G16) runs outside Von and over every primitive, an entry and an interaction included. The first tick of a plan drops the detour, abandons the entry (the stack is told) and replaces the order with `evade` (path `["evade"]`); no decision is started or adopted while it runs (`_should_decide`, `_on_decided`, `set_leaf`). An entry in `bang` is left to its own hold when `Entry.expects_burst(plan)` says the grenade is the bang's. `apply_evade` runs into `plan.spot` (`Locomotion.move_to` within `EVADE_REACH`: a spot is out of the burst's line with little room to spare), then holds it facing away, crouched when the spot is cover. The watch stage does not run: safe first, then the fight; once arrived (or caught by a frag with no spot) `Watch.fight_threat` runs after the facing away. When the plan ends `task.resolved` forces a decision. A thrower in its own wind-up (`throw.stage` `windup`) evades the tick the grenade leaves, `grenades.poll_throw` running first. Read-outs: `grenades.evading()`, `evade_spot()`, `debug_state()["evade"]`. Tests: `tests/ai/grenade/evade_*`.
- **A blinded NPC** (`Character.stun.is_blind()`): no new decision starts (`_should_decide`) and one runs the tick sight returns; its order goes on (it stumbles); the perception keeps its contacts and sees no new one, so the watch stage fires at the last-known spot with aim sway (G13). Whether it fires blind is one roll as it goes blind, `tuning.grenades.blind_fire_chance`, from the reflexes' own RNG seeded at bind (`npc_reflexes.gd`); `grenades.set_blind_fire(on)` hands it to `Combat.blind_fire`. A deafened NPC hears no gunfire, hit, footstep or callout (the orchestrator drops them). Tests: `ai/grenade/stunned_defender_blind`, `stunned_defender_holds_fire`.
