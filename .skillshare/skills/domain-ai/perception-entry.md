# AI · Perception · Entry geometry (`agent_entry.gd`, `entry/`)

The doorway geometry a tactical entry (`docs/tactical-entry.md`; the phase machine is `entry.md`) and the team read. Built from the house's openings and rooms (`set_house`, fed from `WorldGen.get_openings`), cached per doorway on a baked nav map. The claims and every slot rule that reads them are `perception-stack.md`; the doorway views and the walk that avoids them are `perception-views.md`. Tunables: `tuning.entry_geometry`.

## Files

- `agent_entry.gd` (`AgentEntry`) — `entries_from(room_key)` (the doorways out of the NPC's area), `watch_doors(room_key, exclude_id, is_cleared)` (what the team covers after clearing a room, E8), `geometry(id, into, map, space)` (`out`, `along`, `wall`, `side`, `slots`, `pie`, `lookouts`, `capacity`, the four corners `near` / `side` / `hard`), `stack_side_from`, `cuts_view`, `slot_reach`, `room_doorways`, static `normal_into`. `lookouts` are the slots and pie points before snapping: what an ambush post is hidden from.
- `entry/entry_slots.gd` (`EntrySlots`, part `slots`): lays the stack slots. `entry/entry_layouts.gd` (`EntryLayouts`, part `layouts`): the caches. `entry/entry_posts.gd` (`EntryPosts`, part `posts`): defend and ambush posts. `entry/entry_landings.gd` (`EntryLandings`, static): where an entry's grenade lands. Each part holds nothing of its entry; the entry is handed in at the call.
- An `AgentEntry` built outside the perception (`PlayerStack`, `Squad`) makes its own `AgentVision` and `AgentDoorViews`. The team's published seam into it: `set_house`, `geometry`, `stack_side_from`, `slots.on_stack_side`, `opening_id`, `normal_into`.

## Gotchas

- `stack_spacing` keeps a slot outside the hole a body at rest cuts in the steering map, so a slot is never a destination inside a teammate. `capacity` is 2 in a short room (E13).
- **A split stack (E19).** `stack_side_from` is the team's side unless the plain route from `from` crosses the doorway's view (`cuts_view`; a walk that starts in the view or goes round it does not), then the member's own side.
- **Where a stack stands (E20 to E22, `slots.lay`).** Layout `kind`: `wall` (files against the wall beside the door, out of every other doorway's view and funnel), `views` (every wall spot is in another doorway's view), `open_floor`, `dead_end` (no wall spot and no other doorway in the stack room: the member is refused). Overflow runs open floor, then the tail in the room beyond the nearest other doorway; a capped layout takes no tail. A point is kept only on the navmesh, inside the stack room, on the stack side, reachable without going through the doorway, and out of other doorways' funnels (`StackClaim.in_funnel`, the one funnel test). A navmesh carved round solid furniture skips a spot (E22).
- **Shared and laid ahead.** Layouts are cached per instance and in a static map keyed by navigation-map RID and `layouts.key(entry)` (the house and the tunables `_build` reads), dropped when the map's planning region leaves the tree. `warm_geometry` lays at most one missing way in per physics frame among all NPCs (`EntryLayouts.warmed_frame`), so a bake's doorways are laid in the frames after it, not inside a decision. Once every way in is laid it returns at once until the shared dictionary or the `tuning_inputs` array changes (cheap compare). Laying one way in is expensive (tens of ms), which is why the perf guards measure only after the house is laid (`docs/leadership.md`, Performance).
- **Cleared rooms (E9).** `cleared_room` (keyed per room, ttl `tuning.memory.search_ttl`) is not `visited_room`: standing in a room visits it, but only `entry.mark_cleared(key)` clears it. Callers: an entry finishing (`Entry.hold.finish`), a search covering its room by sight, and `hearing.process_callout` on an ally's `cleared_room`. The snapshot's `entries` group skips only cleared rooms; search ranking still uses `visited_room`.
- `entry.slot_reach(to, bodies)`: whether any way reaches `to` with a body standing at each point of `bodies`, on an `avoid_map` of its own. 1 reaches, 0 sealed, -1 still baking (asking starts it), 1 again with no planning region. The entry asks it before it reads a hold as a sealed slot (E2).

## Posts (`posts`; `docs/leadership.md` LD8 D2, LD15 D17)

- `defend_spot(entry, room_key, place, map, space)`: member `nth` of `count` ordered to defend a room takes doorway `nth` mod their number (extras double up), a spot back from its wall and past its jamb, scored out of every fatal funnel first, then with a sight line to its doorway, then over low cover. A lone member of a room with several doorways takes the spot that sees the most (`door` "", `door_pos` INF: it turns among them). The snapshot's `order` group calls it. `place` is one `Vector2i`: x `nth`, y `count`.
- `ambush_spot(entry, g, place, map, space)` → `{ spot, door_pos, seen }`, {} for an unknown doorway or room or no post. Candidates are ranked once per doorway and room side, **shared by every NPC on the map** (`EntryLayouts.per_map`), since a ranking reads only tunables and looks through bodies; a geometry with no NPC keeps its own. The score is lexicographic in effect: `seen` (how many lookouts see the post; 0 hidden) outweighs frag landings, which outweigh stun landings, funnel, no cover on the line to the threshold, then distance from the ideal depth; `ambush_spot` adds `RANK_SIDE` for a post off the member's side above all. Landings (what an entry's grenade does to a body crouched at the post, judged with `Explosion.shields`, interface 3, and `GrenadeConfig.stats_of(kind)`, interface 6) are counted only where they can decide a pick (`_best_post`). Pins: `ai/combat/ambush_posts_capture_terrace` / `_house` (a capture of the full ranking), `ambush_post_cover_through_body`, `perf/ambush_order_stress`.
- `EntryLandings.grid / aim / lands / spots`: the one rule the stack's thrower aims by (`behavior/entry/entry_grenade.gd`, `entry.md`) and an ambush post is shielded by (`docs/grenades.md` G18). `lands` keeps a landing inside the room, clear of its walls and at least a minimum depth from the doorway.
