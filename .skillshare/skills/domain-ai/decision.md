# AI · Decision (`scenes/ai/decision/`)

THINK: the decision policy as data, and the only place the AI talks to the outside model. Von is a stateless single-shot ranker that scores every question independently, so a drill-down is **sequential requests, one per level**, each conditioned on the path chosen so far. The knowledge a walk decides over is built by `snapshot.md`; the chosen leaf is run by `behavior.md`.

## Files

- `decision_tree.gd` — `const NODES`, data only: the node fields, the shape, each node's `desc` / `question` / `context`.
- `decision_planner.gd` — the walk (a `Node`): gating, entry rules, per-level state, Von calls; `driven`, each level a piece (`step`). Its leaf `decision_walk.gd` holds the walk in flight.
- `decision_job.gd` (the decision job) and `decision_budget.gd` (`DecisionBudget`); the Von files are `decision-von.md`'s.

## The tree

Broad mode → tactic → option → primitive: ORDER, COMBAT (engage, flank, grenade, advance, melee, reload, retreat, locate), FOLLOW, RELOAD_QUIET, INVESTIGATE (+ STAY), SEARCH (+ ENTER, STUN ENTRY), IDLE (use, go, wait). A mode or tactic is a node, plus a snapshot option group (`snapshot.md`) for new places.

- `options` names snapshot option groups, looked up under the bound target first, then globally. A node whose groups end up empty (every flank side dropped) has no leaf and is not offered. `drop_tags` drops options carrying those tags. `requires` gates on facts (`!` negates; a bound name counts as true): `engage` and `flank` require `gun_loaded`; `advance`, `melee` and `retreat` stay so an empty NPC can still close in, punch or fall back.
- `params` merge under each option's own. `run: true` lets the behaviour sprint (Von never sees it); `run_through_fire: true` on retreat lets it sprint with a shot available (others drop to a walk to take it, `docs/gaze-and-fire.md` W6); `entry_guard: false` on retreat exempts it from the entry guard that sends every other walk into an uncleared room through a tactical entry first.
- A level with one option is taken without asking Von (ORDER, FOLLOW, `reload`), except INVESTIGATE (`ask`): whether a lead is worth going to is Von's call.
- **Staying in the house is Von's call, guided by the prompt, not a rule.** A territorial goal ("hold your ground inside the house; do not chase anyone outside") is in every state, with the leads saying where the gunfire is; INVESTIGATE's `stay` child (offered while a lead lies outside and the NPC is inside: fact `lead_outside`) is Von's way to decline. Its desc states the situation in the state's words and echoes the goal's sentence, else it loses to the lead under fire and invaders hold too. After rewording re-run `tools/von_probe.py` (`investigate_outside_lead*`) and `tests/ai/investigate/`. `tuning.decision.defend_territory` confines COMBAT only.
- **ENTER** offers the doorways into rooms unseen or unsearched (`entries`); the question asks for "the shortest route". Von chooses whether and which doorway; the rest is the `entry` primitive (`entry.md`), which asks Von nothing on the cadence until over. A forced decision (a hit, a belief change) still replaces it, or with Von down aborts it.
- **GRENADE** (`docs/grenades.md` G17): requires `grenade_ready` only, so the unbound level offers it too. Its desc is "{situation} and you can throw a grenade onto their spot ({summary})"; `_fill` takes `{situation}` from the summary group. Wording: `snapshot-wording.md`; runs `throw` (`combat.md`).
- **STUN ENTRY** (G18): requires `stack_has_stun`; the same `entries` group and `entry` primitive as ENTER with `bang: true`. Its desc and the suspect-room phrases are measured: two reasons in a desc tie ENTER, every reason bangs plain rooms.
- **How a `desc` must read.** It states the SITUATION in which the branch is right, in the phrases the snapshot writes into the state, plus specifics in parentheses (ENGAGE: "You have a clear shot at {target}, and you are not badly wounded ({summary})"). No action labels (they pull toward the goal's verbs), no negated attributes, no option repeating a dominated choice (drop it). Fields: `{gun}`, `{summary}`, `{situation}`, `{blinded}`.

## The planner (`decision_planner.gd`)

- Config pushed in by the controller: `disabled_nodes`, `entry_rules` (ordered fact → start node), `skip_single_option`; re-pushed each decision, `node_overrides` aside (baked in at `setup()`). `pursue_hostiles` maps to `idle` disabled + `hostile_known → combat`. The rule `ordered → order` sits after `under_fire` / `engaged`, before `hostile_known`. A follower disables `investigate`, `search`, `idle` and adds `follows → follow` after `hostile_known` (LD20).
- `begin(snapshot, continuing)` starts a walk at the first entry rule whose fact holds and whose node has a leaf, else `root` (an empty entry falls back to `root`; an empty root fails). When `continuing` (the last choice still runs) its option reads "(your current plan)", never a finished one, or Von re-picks a completed tactic. A bind level with nothing to bind falls through to the node's unbound children (RETREAT / LOCATE). The state is the goal + the node's `context` sections (the bound target's own first) + "Decided so far: COMBAT → Intruder → FLANK."
- **The confinement filter.** Where the NPC may act is no config of the planner's: it reads `snapshot["confinement"]`, perception's confinement frozen as the snapshot was built (`perception.md`), so a hand-made one drives a walk. Inside a `confine` subtree (COMBAT) `_allowed` drops every option `allows_option` refuses, judged where it acts (`params.from`, else `params.point`), (a defender may throw from inside onto a spot outside). Behaviour asks the live confinement, not the leaf's params. `summary_inside` fills `{summary}` while `inside_only` holds in a confined subtree. An order refused as the walk begins (LD6) leaves a copy with no room: the job's refusal step replaces it. Test: `tests/ai/decision/combat_options_within_confinement_test.gd` (hand-made snapshot with `DecisionStub`).
- **ORDER** (LD6): its one option is the snapshot's `order` group, taken unasked; the option names its own `primitive` (`_finish` takes an option's `primitive` over the node's). Its `order` section follows the goal at every level.
- `signal decided(leaf)`: `{ path, labels, primitive, params (node params ⊕ option params + target_id) }`. `signal failed()`: transport failure or nothing to choose; wired to the behaviour's `fallback()`.
- `entry_for(facts)` / `current_entry()` let the controller interrupt a walk when the situation now calls for a different start. `debug_state()` carries per level what replays it offline (state, question, offered, probabilities, pick) and the `confinement` view decided over.

## The budget and the job (`decision_budget.gd`, `decision_job.gd`)

`DecisionBudget` is a **pool** of `per_frame` work units (default 1) a physics frame, shared by every NPC, counted never timed. A **decision job** runs **pieces**: the build's START, HOSTILEs and FINISH (`snapshot-staged.md`), the order-refusal step, one planner level each. Each has a static `cost_*` (`cost_start` 1, the rest 0: one decision is one unit); `take(cost)` spends it when it fits, and a frame whose pool is untouched runs the next piece even if dearer (a job never stalls). Tests use `set_static`; `frame_source` replaces the engine frame in a native suite.

The job holds no owner (ADR 0006). `request(forced)` claims the START: refused, nothing changes and the controller's wish (`forced`, its reason, the elapsed timer) stands for next frame; claimed, any job in flight is dropped without a signal (a cadence wish never claims over one). `advance()` runs the pieces the pool allows; the planner is `driven`: a Von-answered level runs at once if its price fits (`piece_ready`), else on a later `advance()`. The refusal step ends the held order the build offered no option for (`ordered` false, the confinement refreshed and copied over the snapshot's). `last_facts`, `last_sections`, `last_demand`, `last_analysed` change only as a build completes. Tests: `decision_job_suite`, `decisions_spread_over_frames`.
