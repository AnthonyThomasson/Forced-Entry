# AI · Perception · Grenades (`agent_grenades.gd`, `agent_throw.gd`, `perception_grenades.gd`)

What the NPC knows of live grenades and the plan to get clear of them. Spec: `docs/grenades.md` G15, G16. The behaviour half (running the plan, the throw) is `behavior.md` (the evade reflex) and `combat.md`; the GRENADE and STUN ENTRY options are `snapshot-wording.md`. Tunables: `tuning.grenades` (`enabled`, `hear_grenade`, `evade_margin`, `blind_fire_chance`, `throw_spot_walk`).

## What it knows (`grenades.known()`)

A live grenade is a node of the group `live_grenades` (Items: `kind`, `fuse_left()`, `throw_stats()`, `thrower`). The NPC knows one by **sight** (`observe`, only while the group is non-empty: view cone and clear sight line), by **hearing** it come down (`hear`, a `&"grenade"` bounce or rest within `hear_grenade`, not deaf), by an ally's **callout** (`called`), or because it threw it (**own**: always known, blind or not). A grenade keeps the source it was first known by. It is forgotten at the burst (`&"explosion"`, or the node leaving the group). `process_explosion` keeps `grenades.last_burst` (`{ kind, position, frame, team }`: a bang entry waits for its stun, and an entry goes in on its own team's burst, E17) and marks the hostiles a stun caught as `flashed` (memory topic `flashed`), which the contact view reads as `blind`.

Its own grenade (`_note_own`) is known at once; until a bounce its spot is where it was aimed (`Character.weapon.aim_target()`, clamped to the kind's range), not the hand it left.

## The zone and the safe spot (`agent_throw.gd`)

- The cover rule is the blast's own, asked of the Projectile System so the AI keeps no copy: callers use `Explosion.shields` and `Explosion.first_stop` (interface 3). The line is the real 3D one (#194 D10): from `GrenadeThreat.point()` (the burst plan point lifted to the grenade's height, `altitude()` plus `Explosion.BURST_CENTRE`) to each point of the body at its posture's heights (`BodyHeights.sight_points`); a wall, door, vehicle or piece of furniture stops it, a body is shielded only when every point is cut off (a ducked body behind a couch, or a standing one far behind a low piece, in its shadow). A stun's furniture shields only within `flash_cover_reach` of the body. `AgentThrow._judge` gives SAFE_STANDING, SAFE_CROUCHED or NOT_SAFE; `AgentGrenades.plan` reads the NPC's posture with `SightRay.posture_of`.
- `escape(space, map, threat: GrenadeThreat, runner: GrenadeRunner, exclude)` (the whole search; `begin_escape` the same as a job, `EscapeSearch`) returns `{ spot, crouch, route }`. Candidates: the NPC's own position first (already shielded: no move), the circle just outside the zone, the shadows behind what rays from the burst meet, rings round the NPC, and a probe against the far face of each stopper. Each is snapped to the navmesh and judged (outside, shielded standing, or shielded only crouched = `crouch`) as the lazy search reaches it, a survivor routed (`NavBuilder.route`: a spot behind a wall can be a house away), and the shortest route within `(fuse_left - margin) × run speed` wins; `spot` is `Vector2.INF` when none is in time. A route ending short of its spot (a ring past a closed arena's wall) drops it. Run speed accounts for stamina, a stun and a heavy outfit.
- **Lazy, pruned search.** `_nearest_in_time` takes the points nearest the runner first, snaps, judges and routes each, and stops past the best route and the run allowed (a route is never shorter than its straight line less a snap, an off-mesh start, an end short): a far point is never snapped or judged, and a snapped spot whose own straight line is past that is neither judged nor routed. The answer is the one every point gives (`escape_pruned_matches_full_test`).
- `has_escape(...)` is the cornered / `no_safe_spot` question only: it runs the same candidates, snapping, safety verdicts, route endpoint and time rules as `escape`, deduplicates routes by snapped point and returns at the first reachable in-time safe spot. Its boolean equals whether the full `escape` found a spot; the evade plan still uses full `escape` for the shortest route. `escape_route_queries` observes the early exit.
- **Shared on a physics frame.** What depends on the burst alone is the same for every NPC fleeing it, and an evade wave is 4 to 8 NPCs asking on one frame, so `escape` shares the candidates round the burst, each spot's verdict and navmesh spot in static dictionaries keyed by space, burst, kind, margins, heights and excluded bodies, cleared when a new physics frame begins. Outside a physics frame nothing is kept. Only `[from]`, the ring round it and the routes are the NPC's own. Pins: `escape_spot_matches_plain_test`, `evade_wave_spots_match_own_plan_test`; plain search: `tests/_ai/grenade_ai_probe.gd`.
- `can_lob` is the throw test of the GRENADE tactic, the throw's re-check and the bang: within range and `throw_clear`, which asks the shared arc planner (`GrenadeArc.clear`, interface 6) for an arc from the hand at the posture's release height that clears the twin space with `LOB_MARGIN` to spare; the throw flies the same planner (`throw_planner_agrees_with_flight`). `throw_clear_on_arrival` tests the spot a thrower will walk to, with its arrival slack. `reach_of(kind)` is the range less a slack: how far from its spot a thrower lets go.
- **What it knows of a grenade it carries.** No copy of the tuning: `learn(stats)` takes `throw_stats` from `Character.inventory.throw_stats(slot)` of every carried grenade (each `observe`) and from each live grenade (interface 6/7), keeps it by kind so it outlives the last thrown, and answers `range_of`, `radius_of`, `fuse_of`, `max_blind_of`, `cover_reach` (0 or INF for a kind never met). `PerceptionGrenades.carried(character)` lists the kinds with a count, a frag first (`ItemRegistry.FRAG_SLOT` / `STUN_SLOT`).

## The plan (`evade_plan()`)

For the known grenade that goes off first among those the NPC is in the zone of: `{ id, point, kind, radius, spot, crouch, fuse_left, landed, lies }`. Made on first entering a zone, again when the grenade moved (in flight, a bounce) or any distance that leaves the spot in its line (`_still_safe`, test `evade_replans_when_spot_in_line`), at most every `REPLAN_EVERY` s, and kept while the grenade lives, so the NPC holds the spot until the burst. A frag entry's stack reads `point` and `lies` (`Entry.expects_burst`, LD14). The callout waits until the grenade has come down (`announcements()`) and repeats once if it rests far from there; an ally's call or the NPC's own throw is not radioed again.

**The escape pool** (`escape_pool.gd`, `escape_search.gd`): the searches share 80 units a physics frame and a plan can land frames after its NPC enters a zone: `perception-escape.md`.

## Wrappers for the behaviour (`perception_grenades.gd`)

`can_lob(at, to, kind, walked := false)`, `throw_clear`, `reach`, `fuse`, `in_zone(kind, burst, p)` (every character's body excluded: the frag entry's slot check, `docs/leadership.md` D16), `evade_plan`, `known`, `announcements`. `AgentThrow` is also the geometry `SnapshotThrows.grenade_group` uses.

## Stunned and deaf (G15)

Blind: no new sighting and no new decision (it re-decides the tick sight returns), its contacts kept. One roll as it goes blind against `tuning.grenades.blind_fire_chance` decides whether it fires at the last-known spot (aim sway applies) or holds fire (`Combat.holds_fire`); the roll comes from the reflexes' own RNG, seeded at bind from the character's name and spawn spot (a re-stun while blind does not reroll). Deaf: the controller drops what it would have heard (`perception.md`).
