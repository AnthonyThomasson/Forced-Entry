# AI entry: the bang

A stun (or, on an order, a frag) thrown through the doorway before the crossing: E17 in `docs/tactical-entry.md`, G18 in `docs/grenades.md`, LD14 in `docs/leadership.md`. Code: `entry/entry_grenade.gd` (`grenade`), `entry_listen.gd` (`listen`), `entry_readiness.gd`, `Combat.throw` (`combat.md`). Back to `entry.md`.

## The phase

- An ENTER or STUN ENTRY leaf with `bang: true` adds the `bang` phase between `stack` and `cross`. Every claim carries `bang`, `bang_off` and the `stuns` / `frags` the member carries. The stack follows the first claimer's mode: one `bang: false` makes it quiet.
- **Thrower:** `DoorStack.thrower(kind, claim.me())` picks the second of the stack (by slot, either side of a split) if it carries the grenade, else the lowest slot that does; the same on every member. Never the human (a `PlayerStack` claim says `stuns: 0`, `frags: 0`).
- **Plan** (`grenade._plan_bang`, when `grenade.due`): a target by `EntryLandings` (perception's `entry/entry_landings.gd`, the rule an ambush post is shielded by too, LD15), pulled back to the farthest point the grenade reaches (`AgentPerception.grenades.reach`), and a throw spot (its slot, or in front of the door, out from the wall) from which the lob is clear of the jamb (`grenades.can_lob`). None means the bang is given up and the stack goes in quietly. Then it radios `bang` and every member that sees the call enters `bang`.
- In `bang` the thrower walks out to the spot (`StackClaim.SLOT_REACH`: the lob was judged from there), throws through `Combat.throw`, walks back. Everyone else holds its slot turned away from the door; the watch stage does not turn it back in `bang`.
- **Over the bang** (`grenade.over`): a burst of the team's own grenade in the room (`grenades.last_burst` with `team`) while a member is in `approach` / `stack` / `pie` (`note_burst`), or one within `BANG_WINDOW` before `begin` (`note_burst_before`). The point then crosses at once as soon as `readiness.stack_ready`: no pie, no top-off, no upgrade; E14's hold is lifted for `BANG_WINDOW` (`grenade.in_bang_window()`).
- **Give-up:** a call not answered by a burst within `BANG_BURST_CAP`, or never made within `BANG_CALL_CAP`, gives the bang up (`grenade.give_up_bang`: `bang` false, `bang_off` true on its claims, a veto), as does a teammate's veto (no lob, or lost its stun). A member in `stack` or `bang` follows at once. A member that began in bang mode and hears a quiet claim made before its own goes quiet without a veto (`grenade._go_quiet`). Tests: `tests/ai/grenade/bang_fallback_*`.
- **The thrower is committed (R16):** `Entry.throwing()` (phase `bang`, `grenade.stage` `go` or `throw`) makes `committed()` true, so a hostile seen on the walk out does not make it leave. When it ends `entries.apply` raises `task.resolved` with "after the bang". Test `ai/grenade/bang_kept_through_fight` (real Von).
- `Entry.expects_burst(plan)` makes `Behavior.grenades.set_evade` leave a stack to the bang's hold: a stun of a stun bang while the phase runs; a frag of a frag bang in flight or landed where this slot is out of its zone (`grenades.in_zone`).

## When the mode turns

- **A suspect room means a bang (R10):** `begin` makes the entry a bang one when its leaf asks (STUN ENTRY) or the stack carries a stun (`grenade.stack_has_stun`) and the room is suspect (`AgentPerception.hearing.room_suspicion(into)` not empty), whatever brought the NPC to the door. A pass-by (`secure`) never is. A bang its leaf did not ask for sets `readiness.turned` (`take_turned()`), so the controller re-adopts the ENTER as `search/stun_entry/<door>` (`npc_comms.gd` `on_entry_turned`); a guard's entry keeps its walk's name. `listen.begin_mode` takes a team's mode when it already claims the doorway (`DoorStack.mode`).
- **A late reason turns a quiet entry (R11):** `listen.maybe_upgrade`, each tick in `approach` / `stack` / `pie` while nobody crosses and the stack carries a stun: a suspicion reason that came up (or that this member knew on joining a quiet stack), or a teammate seen quiet now saying bang, sets `listen.upgraded` and `listen.turn_bang` (a point in `pie` goes back to `stack`). The upgraded member waits for the rest (`grenade.banging` counts its own turn) and gives the bang up if someone crosses first. A claim without `bang` (the human's) reads as bang, so it never holds a stack back or turns one.
- **A quiet stack listens first (R17):** the point holds in its slot `tuning.entry.listen_time` from `readiness.ready_at` before `pie.start_pie` while a late reason could still turn it (`listen._may_turn`). `state.hostile_in_view` or `AgentPerception.under_fire()` ends the hold (`listen._listen_over`).
- Tests: `tests/ai/grenade/quiet_entry_*`, `stun_entry_footsteps_heard`, `burst_then_cross`, `ai/entry/entry_guard_bang_suspect_room`, `entry_guard_quiet_unsuspected_room`.

## A frag on an order (LD14)

A stack ordered with mode `frag`, or a rally whose go named frag (`grenade._bang_kind()` from `DoorStack.mode`), runs the bang with `grenade.kind` set to frag: the thrower is picked among the frag carriers; `_stack_has` stands for the stun check (D1, D15: no frag in the stack, the bang is given up quietly); `_plan_bang` reaches with the frag's reach and skips every target whose burst would put a claimed slot in the danger zone (`grenades.in_zone`, D16); `tick_bang` waits `FRAG_BURST_CAP` and throws `ItemRegistry.FRAG_SLOT`; on the release it keeps `{ kind, point }` for `take_thrown()`, which `Behavior.grenades.poll_throw` hands to the controller to radio `frag_out`. Every member holds its slot crouched meanwhile (`holds_crouched()`, read by `gait`'s `_wanted_posture`). Tests: `ai/grenade/order_frag_entry`, `order_frag_held_then_go`, `order_frag_no_frag_goes_quiet`.
