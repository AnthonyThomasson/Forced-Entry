# AI · How long a decision takes, who is served first (`decision_roster.gd`)

Side file of `decision-job.md` and `decision-prices.md` (split out to keep them under the 8192-byte cap): the order the pool serves jobs in, what the "cheaper decisions" steps cut (#194 perf-decide2) and what they measured.

**Order and ageing.** The pool is served by reason rank (`DecisionRoster.rank_of`): attacked and hostile belief changed (also `lost the engaged contact`, and `sight returned`: the NPC's own blindness ending, not a hostile seen again, rank 0 because an NPC just unblinded must decide again at once), ordered (rank 1), task resolved, follows its leader, way blocked, any other reason and cadence. A younger better-ranked claim is served before an older worse-ranked job: cross-rank overtaking is the rule. A rank **ages in frames** (never a clock): effective rank = rank less one for every 30 frames since the claim (`AGE_FRAMES`, never below 0), judged in `owed()`. So a job reaches rank 0 at the worst 150 frames (`OTHER_RANK` x `AGE_FRAMES`) after its claim, ranks with the urgent ones, begun before them is served first, and its own build follows (`decision_pool_suite` pins 150 to 180). A job the roster forgot (not served for 3 frames) is registered again as a new claim and starts its age over. Eight decisions at once land on frames 3, 5, 8, 10, 13, 15, 18 and 20 (`decisions_spread_over_frames`).

**The cheaper decisions.** (1) An NPC that carries no grenade makes no throw search (`SnapshotDemand.throw_search`, `throw_search_skipped_without_grenade`). (2) Reason ranks with ageing, above. (3) One analysed hostile unless engaged (`AgentSnapshot._analysed_ids`): with an engaged hostile among the contacts the cap is `min(max_analysed_targets, max_options_per_level)` (the engaged one and the nearest other); with none, one: the nearest in sight now, else the nearest by its last known spot (a remembered hostile behind a wall does not take the slot from a farther one that is shooting the NPC). The others are listed with their line only. `analyses_one_when_not_engaged` pins it, and that the engaged id read after the start moves nothing. (4) A hostile's analysis reused for the NPC's next decision was built and probed, 0 of 43 reused in the seed 23 opening (decisions 15 to 210 frames apart; the NPC moved 0 to 99 px, the other hostiles and the nav iterations differ): not made.

**Why claims drop** (never a wish re-claiming over its own job). A job in flight is dropped by: an order taken since the build began (`overtaken_by_order`); a forced decision at another node (`stale`); the NPC's death (`_on_died` calls `_job.cancel()`, the roster is left at once); its freeing (`release()`, nothing lands). Tally, the same probe run as the table (five seeds, from first contact to frame 700, whole run: the windows above are parts of it): pool 4 25 of 428 claims (ordered 9, belief changed 8, attacked 3, died 3, task resolved 2); pool 6 44 of 519 (ordered 19, belief changed 9, attacked 8, died 6, join over 2); none freed. The earlier a56fd2d2 run: 26 of 517. A longer flight is exposed to more of them: the cure is a shorter build.
