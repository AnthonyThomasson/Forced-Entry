# AI · How long a decision takes, who is served first (`decision_roster.gd`)

Side file of `decision-job.md` and `decision-prices.md` (split out to keep them under the 8192-byte cap): the order the pool serves jobs in, what the "cheaper decisions" steps cut (#194 perf-decide2) and what they measured.

**Order and ageing.** The pool is served by reason rank (`DecisionRoster.rank_of`): attacked and hostile belief changed (also `lost the engaged contact`, and `sight returned`: the NPC's own blindness ending, not a hostile seen again, rank 0 because an NPC just unblinded must decide again at once), ordered (rank 1), task resolved, follows its leader, way blocked, any other reason and cadence. A younger better-ranked claim is served before an older worse-ranked job: cross-rank overtaking is the rule. A rank **ages in frames** (never a clock): effective rank = rank less one for every 30 frames since the claim (`AGE_FRAMES`, never below 0), judged in `owed()`. So a job reaches rank 0 at the worst 150 frames (`OTHER_RANK` x `AGE_FRAMES`) after its claim, ranks with the urgent ones, begun before them is served first, and its own build follows (`decision_pool_suite` pins 150 to 180). A job the roster forgot (not served for 3 frames) is registered again as a new claim and starts its age over. Eight decisions at once land on frames 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 23, 27 and 31 (`decisions_spread_over_frames`).

**The cheaper decisions.** (1) An NPC that carries no grenade makes no throw search (`SnapshotDemand.throw_search`, `throw_search_skipped_without_grenade`). (2) Reason ranks with ageing, above. (3) One analysed hostile unless engaged (`AgentSnapshot._analysed_ids`): with an engaged hostile among the contacts the cap is `min(max_analysed_targets, max_options_per_level)` (the engaged one and the nearest other); with none, one: the nearest in sight now, else the nearest by its last known spot (a remembered hostile behind a wall does not take the slot from a farther one that is shooting the NPC). The others are listed with their line only. `analyses_one_when_not_engaged` pins it, and that the engaged id read after the start moves nothing. (4) A hostile's analysis reused for the NPC's next decision was built and probed, 0 of 43 reused in the seed 23 opening (decisions 15 to 210 frames apart; the NPC moved 0 to 99 px, the other hostiles and the nav iterations differ): not made.

**Latency: the cut did NOT meet its goal** (decisions as quick to land as before). Baseline a56fd2d2 (the ticket tip: pool 3, coarse pieces); "branch" = this branch with the visible-first slot (c5e04417 plus F1). The tail is the price of keeping the pool at 4 (the frame cost is the cut's goal); a follow-up ticket takes it.

**Stress, 0.47 to 0.24 decisions a second on seed 23 at c5e04417: not an earlier end of the fight** (alive NPCs a frame 16.8 against 17.0, engaged 3.2 against 3.0, hostile contacts 3.7 against 3.2; per engaged NPC 2.45 to 1.35 a second). The pool was not the limit there (1.1 of 4 units a frame, wish to land 4.5): the claims it lacks are events that fight did not make (hostile belief changed 7 to 2, sight returned 4 to 0, ordered 4 to 0). Fights diverge once a landing moves; five seeds pooled the rate is 0.43, then 0.39 (-9 %), then 0.45 with F1.

**Why claims drop** (never a wish re-claiming over its own job). A job in flight is dropped by: an order taken since the build began (`overtaken_by_order`); a forced decision at another node (`stale`); the NPC's death (`_on_died` calls `_job.cancel()`, the roster is left at once); its freeing (`release()`, nothing lands). Tally, five seeds, 700 frames each: a56fd2d2 26 of 517 claims (ordered 10, attacked 8, belief changed 5, join over 2, task resolved 1); c5e04417 30 of 458 (ordered 13, belief changed 10, task resolved 3, died 2, attacked 2); branch 28 of 470 (ordered 12, belief changed 7, attacked 4, died 3, way blocked 1, task resolved 1); none freed. A longer flight is exposed to more of them: the cure is a shorter build.

**Pool size** (measured at c5e04417, before F1; five seeds pooled, wish to land median / p95, decisions a second, units per decision): pool 4: opening 7 / 107, 0.58, 15; stress 7 / 203, 0.36, 16. Pool 5: 5 / 143, 0.56, 20; 9.5 / 170, 0.35, 31, no better than 4. Pool 6: 7 / 52, 0.63, 20; 16 / 156, 0.43, 36: it improves the stress p95, decisions a second and the opening p95, but the stress median doubles and a frame spends 3 ms on decisions instead of 2, which is the cut's own goal; that is the reason `per_frame` stays 4. That 19 recordings stop matching with pool 6 is a re-record cost, not a reason.
