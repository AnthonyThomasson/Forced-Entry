# The domestic furniture meshes (`scenes/objects/art/*_mesh.gd`, `furniture_*.gd`, `object_mesh_*_config.gd`)

The pattern is the table's (`art.md`, `domain-art/meshes.md`); this is what the other kinds add.

The domestic kinds (sofa, chair, cabinet, shelf, counter, appliance, bed, office chair, toilet, bath, rug, plant, lamp, coat rack, recliner, fixture, hotplate) follow it: each `<kind>_mesh.gd` reads a `FurnitureSpec` (`furniture_spec.gd`: the footprint in the painter's canonical frame, the drawn colour, `art_opts`, the quarter turns to the facing; the mesh is built canonical and turned by `spec.kit()`, as the 2D art is painted canonical and turned), takes its sizes from the painter's own `ObjectArt*Config` rows and options and its heights from a holder of its topic (`ObjectMeshSeatConfig`, `ObjectMeshStorageConfig`, `ObjectMeshKitchenConfig`, `ObjectMeshRoomConfig`, `ObjectMeshChairConfig`, `ObjectMeshDecorConfig`; a piece's `art_opts.height` wins), and fetches its `Mesh` through `FurnitureMesh.fetch` (the key is the id, footprint, colour, turns and every option and holder value the shape reads, so a retuned holder is another mesh; `tests/objects/furniture_mesh_suite.gd` retunes every holder field and checks it). `FurnitureMesh.show` wraps it in a `FurnitureRig` (no `mirror_update`, no `_process`: a resting piece costs nothing per frame): the body, the damage tint (written when the piece's `Deformable` records an impact, as the 2D art darkens), and glow parts (`add_glow`: a lamp's bulb, a hot ring, a lit screen, a neon trim; `set_lit`, written only on change; no real light, which is the lighting job's). A bed's `FurnitureBedRig` is the one rig with a `mirror_update`: every few frames it checks whether a character in an interaction lies on the bed and lays the blanket flat, writing only on change. Tests: `tests/objects/furniture_mesh_test.gd`, `furniture_mesh_suite.gd`, `furniture_mesh_windowed_test.gd` (`FURNITURE_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves a picture of each room and a close-up of each kind).
