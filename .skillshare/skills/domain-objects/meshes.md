# The furniture meshes (`scenes/objects/art/*_mesh.gd`, `furniture_*.gd`, `object_mesh_*_config.gd`)

The pattern is the table's (`art.md`, `domain-art/meshes.md`); this is what the other kinds add.

The domestic kinds (sofa, chair, cabinet, shelf, counter, appliance, bed, office chair, toilet, bath, rug, plant, lamp, coat rack, recliner, fixture, hotplate) follow it: each `<kind>_mesh.gd` reads a `FurnitureSpec` (`furniture_spec.gd`: the footprint in the painter's canonical frame, the drawn colour, `art_opts`, the quarter turns to the facing; the mesh is built canonical and turned by `spec.kit()`, as the 2D art is painted canonical and turned), takes its sizes from the painter's own `ObjectArt*Config` rows and options and its heights from a holder of its topic (`ObjectMeshSeatConfig`, `ObjectMeshStorageConfig`, `ObjectMeshKitchenConfig`, `ObjectMeshRoomConfig`, `ObjectMeshChairConfig`, `ObjectMeshDecorConfig`, `ObjectMeshClinicConfig`, `ObjectMeshStateConfig`; a piece's `art_opts.height` wins), and fetches its `Mesh` through `FurnitureMesh.fetch` (the key is the id, footprint, colour, turns and every option and holder value the shape reads, so a retuned holder is another mesh; `tests/objects/furniture_mesh_suite.gd` retunes every holder field and checks it). `FurnitureMesh.show` wraps it in a `FurnitureRig` (no `mirror_update`, no `_process`: a resting piece costs nothing per frame): the body, the damage tint (written when the piece's `Deformable` records an impact, as the 2D art darkens), and glow parts (`add_glow(mesh, color, strength)`: a lamp's bulb, a hot ring, a lit screen, a neon trim, each its own strength from `ObjectMeshStateConfig` that `set_lit` restores; written only on change, no production caller until phase 3; no real light, which is the lighting job's). The damage tint reads the piece's `Deformable` through its `changed` signal. A bed's `FurnitureBedRig` is the one rig with a `mirror_update`: every `occupied_every` frames, staggered by instance id so beds do not poll together, it asks one shared per-frame walk of the `characters` group whether a character's `use.active_interaction()` is this piece's `lie` (interface 8: hiding under a bed does not count) and lays the blanket flat, writing only on change. A rug's medallion is one flat mesh at the field's height; the recliner's `surgical` kind is a tilted back, seat and leg rest; the shower's glass is lower than its rod and head. Tests: `tests/objects/furniture_mesh_test.gd`, `furniture_mesh_suite.gd`, `furniture_mesh_windowed_test.gd` (`FURNITURE_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves a picture of each room and a close-up of each kind).

## The commercial, shell and street kinds

Registered the same way, one script per art id: `tech` (monitor wall, server rack), `clinic` (tool cart, cyberware shelf), `security` (barricade, scanner post, lockers), `shop` (counter, aisle, fridge, snack machine), `noodle` (bar, stool, booth, menu board), `stock` (the safe: crates and pallet are loose items now, `item_stock_mesh.gd`), `street_prop` (vending machine, bench, bin, grate, litter), `podium`, `shell_decor` (rebar, rubble, scaffold, cement bags, wall stain), `decor` (trash, cables, cable nest, cardboard, puddle, neon tube), `streetlight` (cobra head, street sign), `awning` (awning, hanging sign) and `car` (the garage's prop, not the Vehicles domain's). Heights come from per-topic holders: `ObjectMeshTechConfig` (tech, clinic, security), `ObjectMeshShopConfig` (shop, stock), `ObjectMeshNoodleConfig`, `ObjectMeshStreetConfig` (street props, podium), `ObjectMeshOverheadConfig` (post, awning, hanging sign), `ObjectMeshShellConfig` (shell decor, clutter) and `ObjectMeshCarConfig`; their fields and the painters' own `ObjectArt*Config` colours are all in the cache key (`furniture_mesh_suite.gd` retunes each). A painter that takes its colours as given (podium, decor, shell decor, awning stripes) is built in them, not the piece's.

- **Overhang.** A piece with an overhead part (streetlight arm and head, awning, hanging sign) gets it in `FurnitureSpec.overhang`: the piece's `look.overhang_rect()` (the definition's `overhang` `{ size, reach }`, a plain field `ObjectFactory` sets before it builds the `Overhang` child, so the mesh needs no 2D node) turned back to the canonical frame, `reach` px out toward the front, and in the cache key. The mesh stands in the footprint merged with it (the post at the footprint, the arm over the street).
- **Heights.** Wall-hung pieces sit at their wall height: an awning slopes down from the wall top (96) and a hanging sign is a vertical blade on a rod, its 2D width its height; a menu board and a wall stain hang thin on the wall at the back of the footprint, a stain's streaks falling from `stain_top`. A streetlight is a post 130 tall; a sign head is a board standing in the x-height plane.
- **Glow, no light.** A lit part is a glow node with its own strength (`ObjectMeshStateConfig`: `screen`, `led`, `window`, `neon`, `lens`, `bulb`; a glow takes one colour, so differently coloured parts are separate nodes, as a rack's three LED colours and a menu's two lines): monitor and till screens, rack LEDs, scanner emitter, fridge, snack and vending windows, menu board, neon tube, awning edge, streetlight sign and lens, hanging-sign characters. No real light: phase 3.
- **Furniture wears its 2D art** (the painter's flat render on the rig's solids, prewarmed): `furniture-art.md`.
- **Hung pieces fade with their wall.** A menu board, a wall stain and the mirror (`FurnitureRig.hangs_on_wall`) are looked after by the wall fade as a hung unit that fades with the wall behind it (`domain-art/wall-fade.md`), so they do not float when the wall fades. An awning and a hanging sign are plain tall units: they fade as any piece between the camera and a character (`overhead_fade_test.gd`).
- **Static.** No kind has a `mirror_update`; loose scatter (rubble, litter, trash, cement bags, cables) is a stream seeded by the look (`spec.rng(salt, OPTIONS)`: the footprint, colour and the options the shape reads), so pieces alike share one mesh. Parts stay inside the footprint but for a glow's `PROUD` on a face.
- **Decals.** A tower's shadow is the sun's, cast by the 3D walls. `wear` and the decals are `wear-meshes.md`; `floor`, `street`, `wall` and `door` are `StructureMeshes`'.
- **Stencils are decals.** `DecalFactory.stencil` puts its sprite in `STENCIL_GROUP`; `ObjectMeshes.track(follower)` (Art's `MeshFollower`) (Main's `_register_meshes`) shows each by `StencilMesh`, a decal of the sprite's own texture flat on the floor slab (`floor_lift`, plus the decal lift). The mesh does no work of its own each frame; the mirror compares the sprite's transform as it does for any tracked node. The stencil is on the shared decal layers: `MeshNodes.decal`'s `layer` is `ObjectWearMeshConfig.tier_stencil`, over every wear mark. A road's lane glyphs are decals of the wear (`wear-meshes.md`).

Tests: `tests/objects/furniture_commercial_mesh_test.gd` (every catalogue row: footprint, height, colour, sharing, glows, facings, stencil, half scale, Main), `furniture_mesh_suite.gd` (cache keys), `overhead_fade_test.gd` and `wall_hung_fade_test.gd` (the fades), `furniture_commercial_mesh_windowed_test.gd` (`COMMERCIAL_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves the downtown district's shop front, noodle stand, clinic, security post, street, lamps and podium at zoom 1 and 3 and a close-up of each kind from a gallery).

Real heights (every piece's top and parts, `PieceHeights`): `heights.md`.
- **Emptied pieces' meshes** (`docs/stacked-items.md` SI13). The shelf mesh builds bare boards when `art_opts.loose` (its parts are `ShelfMesh.hollow_parts`); the stock mesh no longer draws crates or a pallet (`crate_stack`, `wrapped_pallet` are bare places); the hotplate is the crate with a `hob` item (the hot ring is the item's glow part) and a `pan` on it. What they held is item meshes (`item_stock_mesh.gd`, art id `item`, `kind` pallet, wrapped_box, book_stood, shoes, hob, pan, pot; heights in `ObjectMeshStockItemConfig`, painted flat by `item_stock_painter.gd`). Showroom: `sandboxes/showroom/emptied_pieces_sandbox.gd`.
