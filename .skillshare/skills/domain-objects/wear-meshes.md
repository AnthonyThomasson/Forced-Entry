# Wear and decals in 3D (`wear_mesh.gd`, `wall_wear_mesh.gd`, `ObjectWearMeshConfig`)

Side file of `meshes.md` (the stencils and the kit's rules are there and in `domain-art/meshes.md`).

What the 2D draws as wear is a decal (`MeshNodes.decal`, a flat textured quad, `decal_lift` 0.15 over its surface) from the **very textures, rects, kinds and seeds** of the 2D marks (`RoomWear.floor_marks()`, `wall_marks()`, `StreetMarks.decals()`; `DecalLayer.add` takes the mark's kind), so the 3D matches the 2D. `WearFactory.spawn` tells the room's `Floor` and `Wall` (the ones at the room's corner) their `wear`. Nothing is tracked and nothing runs per frame: a decal is a child of the mesh of its surface, written once; a quad is shared by size and a material by texture (a downtown district: about 200 decals). **Two decals of one surface never share a depth:** each lies on a `layer` of `MeshNodes.decal` (`decal_layer_step` 0.25 px a layer), its kind's tier on the street (`ObjectWearMeshConfig`: slab 0, chip 1, other mark 2, lane glyph 3, floor stencil 4, as the 2D layer draws them) plus `order_fraction` (0.9) of a tier by its place in the draw order (`WearMesh.layer_of`), so the later lies over the earlier and a tier never reaches the next; a wall's marks take the fraction alone. Any decal on these surfaces takes a named tier.

- **Floor wear** lies on the room floor's slab, `floor_lift` (0.1) + the decal lift, each mark a step of draw order over the last (`order_layer`: no tier, under 0.48 in all, below a rug's top 0.6, as the 2D draws wear under rugs): `FloorMesh.build` calls `WearMesh.attach_floor`.
- **Wall wear** (`WallWearMesh`) stands on the face of the wall segment it lies on, the face toward the room (`RoomWear.wall_marks()`: `at`, `dir`, `normal`, `length`), painted by the wear painter on a canvas as long as the mark and `wall_mark_height[kind]` tall, its middle at `wall_mark_at[kind]` (a wall is 96). It is a **child of its segment's instance**, so it turns with the wall, is one of the segment's fade units (`FadeBook` fades a segment's `GeometryInstance3D` children with it) and `WallMesh` hands it to a rebuilt segment (`WallWearMesh.dress`; a dent does not cut it). Marks beside a doorway are cut short of it in 2D, so they stand on one segment.
- **The street** (`WearMesh.attach_street`, from `StreetMesh.build`): marks lie at the pavement's height (0) and on the road a kerb lower (`-ObjectStreetMeshConfig.kerb`, 3), not at the ground; overlapping marks keep their draw order by the layers above. The lane's dashes and the crossings are the street mesh's own (`street_drawn`: not laid twice); the lane glyphs, cracks, patches, stains, gum, weeds, slab shades and litter are decals. The kerb is the mesh's.
- **Stencils** are `StencilMesh`'s (`meshes.md`): its decal lies on `tier_stencil`, over every mark of the floor or street under it and never at the height of one; drawn once. A tower's `DecalFactory.shadow` is not drawn: the 3D sun casts a real one (phase 3 owns the light).

Tests: `tests/objects/wear_mesh_test.gd`, `wear_mesh_windowed_test.gd` (`WEAR_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves the worn room, the road's glyph and a lobby stencil at zoom 1 and 3).
