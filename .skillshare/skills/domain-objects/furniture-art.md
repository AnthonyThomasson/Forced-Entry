# Furniture wears its 2D art (#193 phase 2b)

(`furniture_art.gd`, `FurnitureArt.of(spec)`, `FurnitureRig`). The rig textures its solids (the body and each `add_part`, a bed's blanket) through `MeshNodes.textured` (`domain-art/textured-toon.md`) with the painter's flat render (`ObjectArt.flat`: `PixelArt.render_flat` of the piece's `art`, its `art_opts` and `object_material`, at the canonical footprint, in the piece's raw colour: `FurnitureSpec.paint`, since `ObjectArt` grades the colour itself). The mesh is built already turned to the facing, so the art is turned the same quarter turns (the image `rotate_90`, its rect turned with it: the texture is `rect` = the turned footprint) and cached per look (`FurnitureArt` holds the dressed texture; `count()`), never made per frame. A piece with an `overhang` (streetlight, awning) wears one canvas over the footprint's art and the overhang's (`part: "overhang"`), `rect` the union. Glow parts stay their own untextured glow nodes. The damage tint is the same `base_color` write. A piece with no art keeps the shared toon material. The district pre-warm paints these: `DistrictPrewarm.furniture_requests` (every catalogued piece with an art, both orientations of its size, its colour and each room-palette tone of a wood/fabric piece, each neon trim it can take; ~940 requests) joins `WorldGen.prewarm`. Tests: `tests/objects/furniture_textured_test.gd`, `furniture_prewarm_test.gd`; `furniture_mesh_windowed_test.gd` takes the shots (`FURNITURE_PITCH`, `FURNITURE_ZOOM`).
