# Objects: real heights (`scenes/objects/art/piece_heights.gd`)

Side file of `domain-objects`; interfaces 11 and 12. The meshes (`meshes.md`) are built to these.

## Real heights

The meshes' sizes are real: 1 px a 3D unit, 42.3 px a metre (`BodyHeights`, `docs/research/3d-cover-heights.md`), so every `ObjectMesh*Config` height is a real height and no piece is drawn out of proportion with a 74 px person: a kitchen counter 38 (0.90 m) with a 6 px backsplash, a dresser, cabinet and tool chest 38, a nightstand 26, a filing cabinet 56 (its `art_opts.height`), a table 31, a coffee table 18, a bench 19, a workbench 38 (their `art_opts.height`), a bookshelf 77, a fridge 74, a stove 38, a floor lamp 62, a coat rack 72, a shower's glass 80, a bar counter 40 with its stool 27 and a booth's back 46, a drinks fridge 80, a snack machine 76, a vending machine 78, a locker 77, a wrapped pallet 45, a street bench 19, a bin 40, a car 60 (roof). An awning's valance (80) and a hanging sign's blade (78 to 98) clear a standing head (74); the wall is 96 and a door leaf 84 as before.

`PieceHeights` (interfaces 11 and 12) is the read of them: `top_of(definition)` / `base_of(definition)` / `parts_of(definition)` for a catalogue row, `top_of_spec` / `parts(spec)` for a `FurnitureSpec`. It asks each mesh script's own `height_of(spec)` (table, cabinet, shelf, appliance, counter, noodle, clinic, security, shop, stock, street prop) or the holder's value, so the mesh and its height are one number; a multi-part piece lists its parts (`{ base, top, rect }`, `rect` the share of the footprint in the canonical frame): a sofa's seat, two arms and back; a chair's seat and back; a bed's mattress and headboard; a counter's top and backsplash; a noodle booth's seats, backs and table; a hung piece (mirror, towel rack, menu board) starts at its base. The top is the piece's mass: a prop on it (a bowl on a bar, a coat rack's hat, a till, a surgical chair's lamp arm) is left out. The catalogue's 0 to 100 `coverage` column still decides shots until the real-cover job replaces it. Test: `tests/objects/piece_heights_test.gd` (every catalogue kind has a top inside the real metres of its kind; its built mesh agrees, props allowed).
