# General: the twin 3D space (`scenes/general/`)

Side file of `domain-general`; the contract is architecture interface 29, the why is `docs/adr/0014-twin-staging-and-plan-space.md`.

`TwinSpace` (`twin_space.gd`, a never-drawn `SubViewport` named `Twins`, built by `MatchBuilder.stage` under the match's world) holds a 3D twin of each solid sim body in a 3D physics space of its own (Jolt). No 2D physics is left: the sim's roots are plain `Node2D` plan handles, and the twins are the truth of where a free piece, a character or a vehicle stands, what a bullet, an aim (`Pointer.aim_hit`), a sight line and the nav's bake meet
(`TwinSpace.space_of(node)`, `owner_of(collider)` back to the plan handle for `get_surface()`); ADR 0014.

- **Why that world.** The picture's `SubViewport` has a world of its own and a headless test world has no picture: tying the twins to the picture would make "the twin space exists" false in every test, and phase 2 reads it headless. The 3D view draws this world: once told of a world (`View3D.mirror_world`) its picture's viewport takes the world's
  `TwinSpace.of_world(world)` `World3D`, so the lights and meshes stand where the bodies are (a world with no twin space gets a world of the view's own; each staged world has its own, a test's second one never drawn through the first's camera). Test: `tests/general/view_shares_the_twins_world_test.gd`, `twin_carried_picture_windowed_test.gd` (carried meshes: same pixels as ones placed by hand).
- **The hook.** `builders: Array[Callable]`, `Callable(node: Node2D) -> Node3D`, first node wins, null passes. Each domain registers its own (`ObjectTwins.register(space)`, `VehicleTwins.register`, `CharacterTwin.register`, called by `MatchBuilder._twin_space`).
  The node is a `CollisionObject3D` in the frame of the 2D body's origin, shapes built in code (`TwinShapes`), on a `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_*` layer, mask 0; any `CollisionObject3D` is written when its body moves (Objects' are `StaticBody3D`s; a vehicle's an `AnimatableBody3D` with `sync_to_physics` off, a character's a `CharacterBody3D` and a free piece's a `PieceBody`: a write is in the space at once, which a ray reads).
  A node with `twin_update(body)` gets it each frame it follows. A reader with no `TwinSpace` finds a body's twin with `TwinSpace.twin_for(body)` (a meta on the body) and, holding the `World2D` the sim stands in, the 3D space beside it with `TwinSpace.space_in(world)` (its `World3D`: `TwinWorlds.world_in(world)`); `TwinShapes.top_of(twin)` is a twin's top (not a character's: its shapes change). A floor has no physics body: `track_group(&"floors", Floor)` (called by `ObjectTwins.register`; the kind script keeps `node_added` from queueing every `Node2D`). So has a wall, a plain `Node2D` plan handle in `TwinSpace.BODIES` (twinned like a body), `WALLS` (found by a bake, a blast, the wall fade) and `HARD` (walls, doors, vehicles). So has a piece: in `BODIES` and `PIECES`.
- **The update rule:** read `global_transform`, write only when changed, park a sleeping rigid body (once more as it falls asleep; a slow look, `PARK_SPREAD` frames round, catches a script that moves it asleep), write a never-moving body once (an anchored piece too, looked at slowly), retire a freed one (`tree_exiting` frees its twin and takes it off its layer at once). It runs before every body's script
  (`PRIORITY` -100): a reader finds a rigid body exactly where the step left it, a door or a character (a script moves them, after the sync) a frame behind; a write reaches Jolt at once (`force_update_transform`), so a ray after it meets the new pose. A twin is on its layer only while its body's `collision_layer` is not 0.
- **Carried meshes.** Art's `MeshCarrier` (interface 27) is fed by this space: `twinned(body, twin)` is said once a body's twin stands in the space with its row made and its 2D body taken over, and the carrier builds the mesh of a wall, floor, piece, door, vehicle or character as a child of that twin (a character's mesh holds the 2D body's turn: the cylinder never turns); `bodies()` lists those twinned already; `untwinned(body)` / `bodies_without_twin()` are the same for a body no builder twinned (a non-solid piece: its mesh is placed once). `is_waiting(body)` is true from a body entering the tree until the next update twins it. `retwin` (a door knocked down, a piece twinned again) emits `retwinned(body, old)`, the new twin in place and the old freed at frame's end: the carrier moves each mesh over in that call. The picture follows the twin: a car's handle is written from the pose its mesh stands at, in the same physics frame, and its rider placed from it, so every render reader (camera, `PlayerView`, `SeenArea`, `WallFade`) finds handle, seat and mesh one place (only a `physics_frame` read sees a step). A rider's twin trails the car's by a step but nothing reads it (cylinder off while riding, mesh hidden). Nothing writes a carried mesh. Tests: `tests/art/twin_carries_*_test.gd`, `tests/general/twin_*_picture_windowed_test.gd`, `tests/general/camera_follows_drawn_car_test.gd`, `tests/vision/view_eye_is_drawn_seat_test.gd`, `tests/perf/mesh_carrier*_cost_test.gd` and `mesh_follower_cost_test.gd` (ratio to a fixed loop, at ~4x). Whole frame: cost-neutral to +1.5 % (child transforms move to the twin side); perf3d owns the A/B.
- **`SmallFlight`** (`general/small_flight.gd`) is the one stepper for a small thing let go in a twin space (a grenade, a dropped weapon, a casing, a chip): gravity, a sphere sweep, bounces, a skid on the floor or on a piece's top. Items' `GrenadeArc` plans with it; Projectile's `Casing` and Physics' `Debris` fly with it (`PhysicsConfig.twin_mask`). **Cost:** over open floor `step` looks ahead once (`_look_ahead`: one cast of a sphere wide enough for the parabola's sag along the chord, up to `HORIZON` frames to the landing) and moves with no cast while it holds (`_probe.frames`, each frame one overlap query of the stretch for what has moved in: a character, a vehicle, a door; a bounce, `place`, `release`, `settle` or `advance_to` end it; `LOOK_COOLDOWN` frames before it looks again when it found something near). `embedded(space)` says a thing let go inside a solid (a casing or chip starting in a wall settles at once, `settle`); a node finds its space once (`TwinSpace.space_of`) and keeps it. Debris flies at most `PhysicsMeshConfig.flying_room` chips at a time, the rest lie where they left.
- **Cost.** The spike: 600 twins, 0.13 to 0.16 ms a frame with the dirty check (0.32 without). Keep per-frame work to the followed bodies (`followed`, `written` read it); never loop all twins each frame.
- **Not followed yet.** A dent leaves a piece's and a vehicle's twin as built (a wall's is cut again); a vehicle's twin stops at the belt (no glass or roof); a non-solid piece has none.
