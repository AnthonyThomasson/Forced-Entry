# The mesh follower (`mirror/mesh_follower.gd`, `mirror_sight.gd`; interface 27)

`MeshFollower` (`mirror/mesh_follower.gd`, a `Node3D` in the view's viewport, `follow(root)`, `track`, `track_group`, `mesh_of`, `remirror`, `leave_out`, `count`, `followed`, `written`) shows what no twin carries a mesh for, plain `Node2D`s of a tracked group (`MeshCarrier` carries every body's): Objects' floor stencils (`ObjectMeshes.track(follower)`) and Items' lying items (`ItemMeshes.register(follower)`); the carrier's rules for a mesh (written only when the node's transform changed, hidden with the node and where the cone does not reach it, parked while out of the tree, freed with it, a null from a builder not asked again until `remirror`), the hiding rules from `MirrorSight` (shared with the carrier). Physics' `FxLayer` and Projectile's `ShotLayer` are children of the view's viewport, in the twins' world (`install(parent, ...)`). `tests/art/mirror_hook_keys_test.gd` and `mirror_mesh_lifecycle_test.gd` exercise `track` and `track_group` on it. Tests: `tests/art/mesh_follower_test.gd`, `tests/perf/mesh_follower_cost_test.gd`.
