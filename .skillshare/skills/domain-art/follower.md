# The mesh follower (`mirror/mesh_follower.gd`, `mirror_sight.gd`; interface 27)

`MeshFollower` (`mirror/mesh_follower.gd`, a `Node3D` in the view's viewport, `follow(root)`, `track`, `track_group`, `mesh_of`, `remirror`, `leave_out`, `count`, `followed`, `written`) shows what no twin carries a mesh for, plain `Node2D`s of a tracked group: Objects' floor stencils (`ObjectMeshes.track(follower)`) and Items' lying items (`ItemMeshes.register(follower)`); the same rules as a tracked node of the mirror (written only when the node's transform changed, hidden with the node and where the cone does not reach it, parked while out of the tree, freed with it, a null from a builder not asked again until `remirror`), the hiding rules from `MirrorSight` (shared with the mirror). A `WorldMirror` has the same `track` / `track_group` still (the tests). Physics' `FxLayer` and Projectile's `ShotLayer` are children of the view's viewport, in the twins' world, with no mirror (`install(parent, ...)`). Tests: `tests/art/mesh_follower_test.gd`, `tests/perf/mesh_follower_cost_test.gd`.
