# What emptied pieces gave up: loose item meshes and paint (`docs/stacked-items.md` SI13, decision D21)

A shelf's books, boxes and shoes, a crate stack's crates, a pallet's wrapped boxes and the hotplate's plate and pan are not part of their piece's mesh or painter any more: they are loose items (rule 9), set where they were drawn (`PieceCarries`, Objects; `RoomItems.carry`, World-Gen).

- **The piece.** The shelf mesh builds bare boards (sides, back, a slab per board but the top one: `ShelfMesh.hollow_parts`; open on top so the contents are seen from above, the sides and back `ShelfMesh.HULL` thick as bodies) when its `art_opts.loose` is set; `stock_mesh.gd` and `stock_painter.gd` draw only the wall safe (`crate_stack` and `wrapped_pallet` are bare places with no art); the hotplate is the crate's look, the hot ring being a part of the `hob` item.
- **The items** are art id `item`, picked by `art_opts.kind`: `item_mesh.gd` and `item_painter.gd` delegate the new kinds in one line each to `item_stock_mesh.gd` and `item_stock_painter.gd`: `pallet`, `wrapped_box`, `book_stood` (a run of standing books: its spines come from `art_opts.spines`), `shoes`, `paint_tin`, `hob` (its ring glows: `add_glow`), `pan`, `pot`. Heights are `ObjectMeshStockItemConfig`'s, in the cache key (`furniture_mesh_suite` retunes each). The flat art is painted by `kind` alone (`ObjectArt._art_opts`), so the district pre-warm warms every carried item at its size (`DistrictPrewarm`).
- **The home and shop items** are art id `item` as well, delegated by `kind` in one line each: `item_home_mesh.gd` and `item_home_painter.gd` (`HomeItemsCatalog`: keyboard, papers, clock, vase, ...) and `item_shop_mesh.gd` and `item_shop_painter.gd` (`ShopItemsCatalog`: till, jar, pack, bowl, instruments, cyberware, bottles, snacks; a bowl's and a pot's `well` reaches the flat art through `ObjectArt._art_opts`). The shops' look is judged against the old code kept as `tests/_art/old_*.gd` (the oracle: `old_shop_look.gd` for an item, `old_shop_pieces.gd` for a whole piece) by `art/shop_items_lit_windowed` and `art/shop_pieces_lit_windowed`.
