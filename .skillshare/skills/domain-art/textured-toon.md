# The textured toon (`post/toon_tex.gdshader`, `ToonMaterial.textured`, `MeshNodes.textured`)

the 2D painters' art, painted unshaded, on a mesh, shaded by the toon bands. One planar projection straight down in MODEL space, `uv_rect` (an instance uniform, set in units from the px `rect`) the footprint the art covers; a top face (normal up) shows the art, a side face the art's edge texels stretched down and darkened a step (`MeshKitConfig.textured_side_dim`), a clear texel the vertex colour; `opts.tiled` repeats the art (floor, road). A material per texture and tiling (a weak cache like the decal's: instance uniforms cannot be samplers), so draw calls are unchanged (one per node) and what grows is materials: `ToonMaterial.textured_count()`. Give a textured part a white vertex colour (the art is multiplied by it) and tint with `base_color`.
The outline's colour is the surface's own darkened, not one near black: a silhouette takes the nearer side's colour (`outline_darken` 0.15 on the world, `figure_darken` 0.35 on a figure so an outfit reads at zoom 1), a crease its own pixel's; a mesh marked `MeshNodes.figure` (a character's parts: `figure`, an instance uniform the toon material writes as roughness into the normal buffer's alpha) gets the silhouette only, no creases (`figure_creases` brings them back), as a body's faceted limbs, a pixel or two wide, would crease at every facet. Test: `art/pixel_post_outline_windowed` (windowed, `OUTLINE_SHOTS=<dir>` saves the shots).

The light, bands, fade and vision read are `toon_common.gdshaderinc`, shared with `toon.gdshader` and `decal.gdshader`; `toon_surface.gdshaderinc` holds `base_color`, `figure`, `emissive`. Tests: `tests/art/mesh_textured_suite.gd`, `toon_tex_windowed_test.gd` (`TEXTOON_SHOTS=<dir>` saves the shots).
