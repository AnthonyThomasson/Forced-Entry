# Vehicle art

The vehicle is shown only by the 3D mesh (`art/vehicle_mesh*.gd`, `VehicleMeshConfig`, `domain-art/meshes.md`); the 2D `Chassis` / `Roof` draw layers are gone. The brake lights are read off `brake_lights()`, the dents off `hit_marks()`.

## Painters (`art/`)

- `car_painter.gd` / `van_painter.gd` paint a body (`opts.part` `chassis` or `roof`); `door_painter.gd` (`panel` / `glass` / `mirror`), `wheel_painter.gd`, `lamp_painter.gd`. Each is `static func paint(c, base, material, opts)` (interface 12) and reads its geometry from the `VehicleConfig` `kinds` and `looks` rows, wrapped in `VehiclePaint`.
- `vehicle_art.gd` holds the shared helpers (`paint_parts`, cabin `seats` / `dashboard` / `sills`, door frames, the lamps).
- The body painters cut holes where each door's side glass goes (`clear_door_glass`), because the door's own art holds that glass.
- **Body painters call no `cast_shadows`**: the pass walks the whole canvas; bevels shade the body instead. The small door panel does call it.

## Regions and caching

- `vehicle_art.region` builds a `PixelConvexShape` from a vehicle-frame point test, once per geometry key and canvas size (`_regions`), and reuses it for every lighting step. A mask `PixelShape` would test every pixel on each paint; a convex one's fill, bevel and outline cost only its edge. The test must be convex along both canvas axes.
- Every texture is a flat render (`PixelArt.render_flat`) the 3D meshes wear (below); the painters draw it once per look and the cache keeps it.

## The 3D meshes (`art/vehicle_meshes.gd`, `vehicle_mesh.gd`, `vehicle_mesh_rig.gd`, `vehicle_skin.gd`, `vehicle_mesh_config.gd`)

In the 3D render (`domain-art/meshes.md`) a vehicle is shown by a mesh of Art's kit: `VehicleMeshes.register(builders)` (Main's 3D view calls it) puts `VehicleMesh.build(body)` under `MeshKinds.VEHICLE`, so a car and a van share one builder that reads the kind's `kinds` and `looks` rows (the painters' own numbers) and `VehicleArtConfig`'s colours; the heights the painters leave out are `VehicleMeshConfig` (car belt 36 / roof 52, van 46 / 78). It returns a `VehicleMeshRig` (a `Node3D` with `mirror_update`) in the vehicle's frame (x forward, y right):

- **Hull** (one cached mesh per kind, white faces, textured with the body's 2D art, below): the outline cut at every door's span, the dark recess behind each cut, bumpers, head lamps, the roof slab over dark glass (a car's pillars; a van's cab, with its cargo a solid block), a windscreen and rear window, each a low flat step (a ramp facing the sun is lit a band up and its dark glass reads pale).
- **Doors**: a leaf each, textured with its panel's 2D art, in the frame of its hinge, so the rig turns it by `-door.angle()` or rolls a sliding one by `door.offset()`; the front doors carry a wing mirror.
- **Wheels**: a tyre and hub on their sides at the axles (a spoke on the hub shows the turn); the front pivots steer by `wheel_angle()`, every wheel turns by the way rolled (`VehicleMeshConfig.wheel_spin`).
- **Tail lamps**: one mesh, lit by `MeshNodes.glow` while `brake_lights()`.
- **Dents**: a dark patch per `hit_marks()` impact (whole-pixel sizes, so the mesh cache stays bounded), rebuilt only when a dent is added or dropped (count and newest seed, as the 2D keys them); a patch inside a door's span hangs on that door's leaf and swings or rolls with it. The hull is not re-meshed: a dent is at most 3 px deep, below what a hull of this resolution shows.


**The 2D art on the mesh** (#193 phase 2b, `domain-art/textured-toon.md`, `domain-art/flat-paint.md`). `VehicleSkin.body(kind, paint)` is the car or van painter's `PixelArt.render_flat` of the `chassis` and the `roof` blended (the roof over the chassis, the paint under every clear texel so none is see-through; no side glass), turned a quarter back onto the mesh's frame (`Image.rotate_90`: the painters' front +y becomes +x, the mesh samples `x` along the texture's width), with `rect` the body's footprint; `VehicleSkin.glass(kind, def, paint)` is one door's side window: the body's art with the door's flat `glass` blended over it, cropped to `glass_rect` (opaque), which `vehicle_mesh.gd` lays on the hull at the belt as a decal (`Glass_<id>` on the rig) and `VehicleMeshRig._pane_follows` fades by the door's `amount()` over `VehicleArtConfig.glass_fade` (`MeshNodes.fade`, the pane is hidden at 0), as `_draw_door_glass` does in 2D; `VehicleSkin.mirror(kind, paint)` is the wing mirror's flat art, a decal on the top of the mirror's box (`MirrorArt` on the leaf, so it swings with the door); `VehicleSkin.door(kind, def, paint)` is a door's flat `panel` turned by the door's own `turn` about its hinge, `rect` in the hinge's frame (where the leaf's mesh is). `vehicle_mesh.gd` hands them to `MeshNodes.textured`, so the hull's and the leaves' tops show the 2D car (bonnet creases, windscreens, roof ribs, lamps, bumpers) and their sides stretch its edge texels. Skins are made once per kind, paint (and door) and held (`VehicleSkin.count()`), so the textured materials are `1 + doors` per kind and paint (a car 5, a van 6; `ToonMaterial.textured_count()`), plus a decal material per window and per paint's mirror (`ToonMaterial.decal_count()`), never per vehicle or frame. A door's panel keeps the 2D draw's own stretch (a rear door 59.5 px long on 60 texels). Dents stay decals, the tail lamps glow through `MeshNodes.glow` (the lamp painter is for 2D), the wheels keep the kit's colours.
- **Flat read.** `VehiclePaint.flat` (the canvas has no light) with `lit(tone, plain)` and `ring()`: a flat paint draws the base tone where the lit paint picks a hand-made light tone (a car's roof panel, a van's roof panel and its ribs, the seat back, the bonnet's light crease, the door's handle glint) and skips the rings the post's outline replaces (the roof panel's, the van's vent, the seat's headrest, the mirror's). The lit 2D pixels are pinned by `vehicle/vehicle_art_flat`.

Tests: `vehicle/vehicle_mesh` (the shapes, heights, the art on the hull and leaves, wheels, doors, lamps, dents, scale), `vehicle/vehicle_art_flat` (the 2D pixels pinned, the flat read, the skins' frame, cache), windowed `vehicle/vehicle_mesh_windowed` (`VEHICLE_SHOT_DIR=<dir>` saves the pictures; `VEHICLE_ZOOM`, `VEHICLE_PITCH` set the camera).
