# Character body: the 2D art on the 3D body (`body/art/body_textures.gd`, `BodyTextures`)

**The 2D art on the parts** (`body/art/body_textures.gd`, `BodyTextures.apply`, called by `_dress_parts` when a look changes, never per frame): each painter's flat render (`PixelArt.render_flat`, `domain-art/flat-paint.md`: no light of its own, canonical, one texture per part per look, cached by `PixelArt`) is mapped onto the part it is drawn for with `MeshNodes.textured` (`domain-art/textured-toon.md`: the art projected straight down in the part's model space, so it stays put on the part and turns and stretches with it; tinted white, the art shows as painted). The shoulders (`BodyPainter`: collar, sleeve ends, vest top, plates, the pack's straps) go on the trunk and the vest, 1 art px to 1 world px and centred on the trunk's axis (the trunk is narrower than the art, so the sleeve ends past it are the arms' own strips), the pack's rectangle of it on the pack's box; the head (`HeadPainter`) on the head's dome and on the hair's and headwear's over it, the art's disc the dome's equator (the painters centre a disc on the pixel `int(w / 2)`, half an art px past the canvas's centre: `BodyTextures._disc_rect` centres the disc, not the canvas), and the whole canvas (a brim, ear pods, goggles and the hair's fall lie past the disc) on a **cut-out sprite laid flat over the crown** (`BodyMesh.CROWN`, a thin slab one px over the highest dome, `MeshNodes.textured` with `cutout`: a clear texel draws nothing; casts no shadow; upright over a living head, flat over a fallen one), so from above the head is the 2D sprite and the domes keep the silhouette and the shadow; the face's band is lower and the sprite covers it; a fist (`HandPainter`) on its ball, which turns with `pose.hand_angles` (sampled with the joints, `FISTS`); a limb's strip (`LimbPainter`) on the limb's top: **the limb solids lie along +z** (`BodySolids`; the node's y is the top the art lies on, up, or the body's facing for a steep limb: `BodyMesh._limb_frame`, `STEEP`), so the straight-down projection puts the strip's width across the limb and its length along it; the shin is the knee's and the shin's strips stacked into one texture (`BodyTextures._stacked`, kept by the pair of textures); a boot's shaft and the shoe their own strips (the shoe's over its footprint). The neck, the face's visor band and the plates stay plain colour (their `base_color` tint). Where a part's art is clear (a disc's rim, a pack's rounded corners, a strip's ends) it shows the part's **vertex colour**, so every textured part is a solid made in its own colour (`BodySolids.fetch(shape, opts, color)`, `BodyMesh._coat`: skin on the head, hair on the hair, the pack's colour, a sleeve's, ...), never white. A look's textures are about 16 materials (`ToonMaterial.textured_count()`: head, trunk with vest and pack, two fists, and two sides each of upper arm, forearm, thigh, shin, shaft, shoe) that every body of the look shares; a body with its own look makes its own. The painters read the flat canvas (`StyleArt.flat(c)`, `c.light == Vector2.ZERO`) and then draw no `outline()` ring (the post outlines the figure: a ring would be a second outline) and no hand-picked light rim (their base tone instead): the head, the shoulders and their pack, the fist, the shoe and a boot's shaft, the vest's panels and pouches; the 2D pixels are unchanged (lit canvases never take the branch). Tests: `tests/character/character_mesh_test.gd` (the textures reach the parts, the art is made once), `character_body_map_test.gd` (side 0's sleeve end by side 0's shoulder at four facings, a strip's start at its joint, the fist's front, the dome's centre, the vertex colours, the sprite over the crown, an arm's own side), `character_flat_art_test.gd` (a flat rim is lighter than the lit ring; the shoe's, shaft's and vest's rings and rims are not drawn flat); the look is judged in the windowed shots (`CHARMESH_SHOTS=<dir>` saves them; the windowed shots are drawn on demand with `RenderingServer.force_draw`, as a window the desktop covers is not drawn by the loop and a wait for a draw then stalls the test).
