# Character body: the 3D mesh (`body/art/body_mesh.gd`, `body_held.gd`, `character_meshes.gd`, `CharacterMeshConfig`)

In the 3D render (#193) the mirror shows a living character by `BodyMesh`, a low-poly, human-proportioned body of Art's mesh kit (`domain-art/meshes.md`), in place of the proxy capsule; `CharacterMeshes.register(builders)` puts its builder under the `CHARACTER` hook key and Main's `_register_meshes()` calls it. It reads what the painters read, never a copy of it: the **joints** are `anatomy.rig()` (solved once per physics frame and shared with the 2D draw, so the body faces `pose`'s angles, holds what the hands hold, strides as the gait says and folds in a crouch by `CharacterPostureConfig`: P3), the **colours** are `visuals.dress` (shirt, pants, shoes, skin, each arm segment, each glove) and its resolved look (`PixelArt.look_of(dress.head_opts)`: hair, cap or helmet, vest and plate colours, a pack). The parts are shared unit solids (`BodySolids`, through `MeshCache`: shaped, not boxes: a trunk lathe with hips, a waist, a chest and sloping shoulders, an egg-shaped head, limbs tapering by the 2D art's widths, a neck, a shoe with a rounded toe; a ball and a box for the small ones) scaled and placed between the joints by `mirror_update(body)`, written only when a joint moved by more than `BodyMesh.MOVED` (0.2 px, under one pixel of the 3D picture; a direction by `TURNED` 0.02: a breath or a head's spring settling is a write a few times a breath, not every frame; the last pose is one array sized once, copied into); `mirror_update` first calls `dress.refresh(body.wardrobe)` (the 2D visuals refresh only a body inside the 2D cull rect, the 3D camera sees more; the call answers at once when nothing changed, and the visuals' own refresh then returns false: harmless in 3D), and a new look (`dress.look_key`) re-tints and shows or hides the optional parts (vest, plates, pack, hair, headwear, a boot's shaft) in place, no rebuild, and poses again (a hidden part is not placed: `_place` skips it). The rig's heights are the drawing's (a head 100 px up), so they are squeezed to `CharacterMeshConfig.height` (74: three quarters of a wall, upright and human in proportion); the horizontal reach is the 2D body's, so the body's footprint from above is the 2D art's. A knee and an elbow are not just lifted with their ends: `BodyMesh._bent` shrinks their bend with the chord, so a standing leg stays straight when squeezed (a fallen body's joints are the ragdoll's own, untouched). A seated body (shapes disabled) is hidden by the mirror's own rule. Tests: `tests/character/character_mesh_test.gd` (the seams), `character_mesh_windowed_test.gd` (`CHARMESH_SHOTS=<dir>` saves the pictures; windowed only); the held item and the fallen body: below.

**The parts** (24 `MeshInstance3D`s per character plus the held item's, the last child (`HELD`); one set of shared unit solids (`BodySolids`); `BodyMesh`'s index constants; a limb is 8 sides round, the trunk 12):

| Part | Solid | Shadow | Shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TORSO` (shirt), `VEST` | trunk lathe (12 sides; the vest is its slice from `vest_from` up) | casts | the vest when the outfit has one |
| `PLATE` x2 (front, back) | box | none | a plate in its carrier |
| `PACK` | box | casts | the style's look has a pack |
| `HEAD` | egg lathe | casts | always |
| `HAIR`, `HEADWEAR` (cap or helmet) | ball | none | hair when bare-headed and not bald; headwear when a cap or helmet is worn |
| `FACE` | box, a wide visor band across the front, near black on a light skin and near white on a dark one | none | always: how the head's facing reads |
| `UPPER`, `FORE`, `THIGH`, `SHIN` x2 | tapered limb lathe (the widths of `CharacterArtConfig.body_arm_widths` / `body_leg_widths`) | casts | always |
| `NECK` | tapered limb, chest joint to head | none | always |
| `HAND`, `FOOT` x2 | ball, extruded shoe outline (`SHOE_LENGTH` 0.8 of the 2D foot) | none | always |
| `BOOT` x2 (a boot's shaft) | straight limb lathe | none | boots worn |

Twelve parts cast a shadow (the trunk, vest, pack, head and the eight limb segments), twelve do not (the small ones, the neck among them, skin-tinted: a shadow of a plate, a fist or a shoe adds draw cost the pixel post's resolution does not show). The head's size (`head_scale` 1) and the limbs' (`limb_scale` 1.1) are the 2D art's own; at the game's default zoom the outline pass dominates a 30 px figure and the face band is lost, which only the outline weight (`PixelPostConfig`, Art) would change. The windowed test's pictures: standing, facing away and crouched, at zoom 3 and zoom 1.

Every part is a **figure** for the pixel post (`MeshNodes.figure`, set in `_make`): the outline draws its silhouette only, no crease on a facet, and in its outfit's darkened colour (`domain-art`, the pixel post), so the outfit and the face band read at zoom 1.

## The held item (`body_held.gd`, `BodyHeld`; `body.held()` is its node)

What the character holds (`inventory.current_item()`) is one more figure part, the mesh its art gives (`item.art.held_mesh()`: Items' `GunMesh` / `GrenadeMesh`, `domain-items/weapon-looks.md`: the 2D art stood up, shared per kind and look, null for fists, an empty hand and a key), wearing that art (`held_texture()`, `held_rect()`: `MeshNodes.textured`). `BodyHeld.sample` swaps the node's mesh and texture only when the item's instance or style changed, and returns the item's aim (`held_aim`: the weapon's facing, turned by a punch) and its pitch (`held_lift`: a long gun raised off a wall points up, as the 2D art shows its barrel shortened); the body samples it with the joints, so the node is placed (`place`: at the weapon hand's joint, `weapon_hand()`) only when one of them moved by `MOVED` / `TURNED`. It follows every hold the rig gives: aim, lowered, reload and a grenade's wind-up and throw move the hand and the item with it. It casts no shadow, and it is hidden with a seated body.

## The fallen body

When the character dies the mirror keeps the body (`on_body_died` answers true when the ragdoll exists) and it follows `anatomy.ragdoll`, whose joints have the rig's indices and reads (`point`, `toes`, `face`), through the same `_pose`: written only when a joint moved, nothing at all once `ragdoll.is_settled()` (the mirror's own per-row read is all that remains), and again from the frame a blast wakes it. A joint's height is its 2D px squeezed to the body's height, but never under `CharacterMeshConfig.lying_clear` (eased in over `LIE_FRAMES` after the death), so a body lies on the floor on its limbs' thickness, not in it; a fallen head's up is its neck's, not the world's. A fallen body casts no shadow (its parts' shadows are turned off at death), as the 2D one casts none; it counts as at rest only once the ragdoll sleeps and `LIE_FRAMES` have passed. The held item is gone from the body (the 2D drops a gun as a `DroppedItem`, which the items job mirrors; a grenade is never drawn on a fallen body). What lies on the side it lies on (the pack, the hair, a back plate) sinks into the floor. Tests: `tests/character/character_held_mesh_test.gd`, `character_ragdoll_mesh_test.gd`; windowed shots (`HELD_SHOTS=<dir>`) `character_held_mesh_windowed_test.gd`.
