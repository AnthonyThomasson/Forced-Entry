# Doors: Space and the scroll (interfaces 7 and 24, `docs/doors.md` DR6, DR7)

`use.doors` (`CharacterDoors`, a `RefCounted` held by `use`, not by the body, which is at its 15 variables, ADR 0004) is the character's use of an Objects `Door`, found duck-typed by the group `doors`: `door_in_reach()` (the standing door nearest `movement.aim_point` whose leaf, a segment from `leaf_pose(angle())` and `leaf_size()`, has its nearest point within `CharacterDoorConfig.reach` of the body and ahead of it; none when dead), `gap_to(door, point)` (the leaf's nearest point to `point`, the mouse), `use()`, `open_fully()` and `turn(steps)`, each taking an optional held door (acted on while `reaches(door)`) and false with none in reach. It reads no input event: the puppet's `use()` (Space) / `turn_door()` and the AI's locomotion (`open_fully`) call it as the player's controller does.

`use.interact_target()` is what Space acts on, `{ kind, object }`: the reachable thing nearest the mouse among the objects advertising interactions (`&"object"`), a door in reach (`&"door"`, `doors.gap_to(door, aim)`) and a vehicle with a door it can board by (`&"vehicle"`, `CharacterVehicleUse.nearest_door_distance(aim)`); each is the gap from the mouse to its nearest point, so a candidate that cannot act (a blocked door) is skipped for the next nearest; an interaction in progress is `&"end"` and a ride is `&"vehicle"` (seated, Space gets out). `use.try_interact()` acts on it at once. `player_controller.gd` is the input side: on the press of `Keybinds.INTERACT` it reads `interact_target()`; everything but a door is acted on at once, a door is remembered (`_held_door`). Its `_input` takes a scroll (`Keybinds.scroll_notches(event)`) while Space is held on a door, turns that door by it (`doors.turn(notches, _held_door)`; the camera, which reads `_unhandled_input`, never sees the event) and marks it scrolled (`_use_scrolled`); with no door held the event goes on to the camera. On the release an unscrolled hold toggles the held door. Tests: `tests/character/door_use_key_test.gd`, `door_scroll_open_test.gd`, `tests/interaction/space_picks_nearest_to_mouse_test.gd`, `tests/general/space_vehicle_key_test.gd`.

A door in the way is also a pushable for `_apply_pushes` (`SKILL.md`, "Walking into furniture"): the slow-down by `get_mass` is the door's `weight`, and a walking push (a shove far under a kick's) never counts against a latch. A punch and a kick strike it through `body_strike.gd`'s `apply_impulse` then `take_blow(kind, amount, impulse)`, the strike naming its kind (`MeleeBlow.kind`; `docs/doors.md` DR10).
