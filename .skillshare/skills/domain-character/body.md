# Character body (`scenes/character/body/`)

Everything that gives the character a body. `character.gd` (`SKILL.md`) owns state and physics and steps these through its `anatomy`; nothing here decides what the character *does*. The body's own tuning holders are in `body/` (`CharacterStrideConfig`, `CharacterMeleeConfig`, `CharacterPoseTurnConfig`, `CharacterPoseHandConfig`, `CharacterPoseHeadConfig`, `CharacterRagdollConfig`, `CharacterRagdollJointConfig`); it shares `CharacterBodyConfig`, `CharacterPostureConfig`, `CharacterStanceConfig`, `CharacterStunConfig`, `CharacterArtConfig`, `CharacterStyleConfig`, `CharacterShadowConfig` and `CharacterRagdollFallConfig` with the root. Interfaces: 4 (`Deformable`/`Deformation`, generic `Ragdoll`), 6 (the held `Item`'s stances, `strike` and `art.draw_weapon`; the `Outfit`'s `look(style)` and `revision`), 11 (`PhysicsLayers.SOLID`, `FillBatch`), 12 (`PixelArt`, `PixelCanvas`, `ArtCanvasConfig`, `ArtSurfaceConfig`), 15 (`GameLog`).

**Side files:**
- Pose, stances, the rig, the run's legs and arms, the stunned look → `body-pose.md`.
- Hands, punch, kick, legs against walls → `body-melee.md`.
- Ragdoll fall and the fallen body's drawing → `body-fall.md`.
- Pixel-art painters and the resolved look (style, outfit, plates) → `body-art.md`.

## Files

Animate: `character_pose.gd` (+ parts `pose_springs`, `pose_arms`, `pose_gait`, `pose_drive`), `character_rig.gd`, `character_legs.gd`, `character_hands.gd`, `character_kick.gd`, `character_ragdoll.gd`; `body_sweep.gd` is the collide-and-slide the hands and legs share, `body_strike.gd` (+ `melee_blow.gd`) the melee strike the punch and kick share. Draw: `character_visuals.gd` (+ parts `body_dress`, `body_limbs`, `body_shadow`, `body_laid_out`, `body_trunk`, `body_meshes`), `character_style.gd` (the look a style resolves to) and the painters in `body/art/`. `character_base.tscn` attaches `character_hands.gd` and `character_visuals.gd` as the `Hands` / `Visuals` children; the rest are `RefCounted` helpers the anatomy owns.

## The anatomy (`character_anatomy.gd`)

`CharacterAnatomy`, built once with the character as `anatomy`, holds the animate parts (`pose`, `hands`, `legs`, `kick`, `footsteps`, `ragdoll` (null while alive), `deformable`, `wound`, `occluder`) and steps them. `attach()` (from `_ready`) finds the hands and adds the node parts. On foot `step(delta)` runs the kick, the pose (its `PoseDrive` refilled by `_pose_drive`), the legs and the footsteps; dead, it steps the ragdoll and bleeds out once settled. `hold(...)` is the frame a ride or interaction holds the body. `die()` / `fall(momentum, direction)` are death (`SKILL.md`).

**Order matters:** the pose is stepped right after `move_and_slide()` and before the `Hands` child's `_physics_process`, so hands build on this frame's pose. Pose, aim, shots and punches stay on `facing`; the pose is visual only.

## Drawing: gotchas

`character_visuals.gd` draws the living body back to front: shadow, legs and shoes, arms, `item.art.draw_weapon()` (under the torso, so a long gun's butt tucks behind the shoulder), shoulders, fists, head last (so it covers gun and fists), then a hand at or above `HAND_ON_FACE_HEIGHT` (only the blinded `eyes` stance) over the face, then wound marks. A hand hanging relaxed (below `HAND_OVER_HEIGHT`) is drawn *before* the shoulders so they cover it. A crouch (`_draw_lying`) keeps the order, drawing the head after the fists. `draw_order` records what the last draw painted, for `tests/character/head_over_gun_test.gd` and `hanging_hands_under_shoulders_test.gd`.

- **A standing body repaints only when its drawing changes.** `_process` solves the rig once (shared by the body's and the shadow's draw) and rounds its inputs to a key (`_redraw_key_of`): joints and feet, the shoulder / head / fist / weapon angles, the weapon raise, the held slot, the wound count and damage, and the resolved look's hash (`dress.look_key`), rounded to `REDRAW_POS_STEP` and `REDRAW_ANGLE_STEP`, below a visible pixel. `queue_redraw()` runs only when the key changes (an idle body repaints about once in 15 frames). **A new input to the standing draw must join `_redraw_key_of`, or the body will not repaint when it changes.** `redraws` counts draws for tests; `drawn_art()` (a test read) returns the textures the last draw painted, because pixel art is cached by what it depicts. Tests: `standing_body_repaints_on_change_test.gd`, `outfit_art_test.gd`; the idle crowd's cost: `tests/perf/crowd_deaths_stress_test.gd`.
- **A body the camera cannot see is not drawn at all.** `_in_view()` takes the viewport's visible rect into world space, grown by `CULL_MARGIN` (the longest reach of a limb or gun past the centre). Outside it nothing is solved or repainted, and the redraw key is cleared so the first frame back repaints. A test that reads the drawing of a body its camera does not frame keeps it in view with `camera_follow` (`fallen_draw_order_stable_test.gd`). Test: `offscreen_body_is_not_drawn_test.gd`.
- **A body wholly outside the player's view is not repainted** (`docs/player-vision.md` V11). While `SeenArea.reaches(position, SEEN_REACH)` (General, interface 11; 60 px, a hand with a gun) is false, `_process` takes the same path as an off-screen body (the redraw key is cleared, `_unpainted`), so the frame any of its reach enters the cone it repaints before it is drawn. Only the repaint is skipped: pose, hands, aim, footsteps and the ragdoll run in the animate layer, which gameplay reads, so a bullet hits the same zone with or without a view. A settled corpse keeps its last drawing (it is remembered); with no view `reaches` is always true. Visibility bits: a living character is on `ViewLayers.MOVING` (`character.gd` `_ready`), a dead one is on `MOVING` until its ragdoll is settled and on `WORLD` after (`CharacterAnatomy.step`). Test: `vision/unseen_not_repainted`.
- **Kept meshes.** Windowed, each polygon command builds and frees GPU buffers every frame, so single-polygon parts are drawn as meshes the body keeps (`BodyMeshes`: a pool on the body, `meshes`, and one on its shadow): limb segments, shoulders, fists, head (`_draw_round`), the shoulders' floor shadow, a folded or fallen trunk's faces, and the standing floor shadow's plain black shapes (`BodyMeshes.Solid`, gathered as `FillBatch` does). A mesh is cut by `Geometry2D.triangulate_polygon` (a fan differs at a few edge pixels), drawn in the polygon's colour as its modulate, written in place and rebuilt only when its corner count or cut changes; `begin()` at each draw hands the pool's meshes out again from the first. Bounds are set with `mesh_set_custom_aabb`; they decide which lights reach it, so where more than 15 lights reach a body (`docs/lighting.md` L4) a mesh can keep a light the single polygon dropped, the only case its pixels differ. Headless (`BodyMeshes.enabled` false) every part is the polygon command it was. Test: `body_mesh_matches_polygons_test.gd` renders frozen bodies with meshes off, on and off, every pixel the same.
- **Shared limb chains.** The body's and the shadow's draws share one set of chains (`limbs.chains`, `body_limbs.gd`, dropped by `limbs.drop()` each `_process`). A limb capsule whose ends' radii differ by less than `BodyLimbs.CAP_CONVEX` of its length is convex and goes into a batch as a fan, uncut. Test: `body_geometry_matches_plain_test.gd`.
- **Wounds turn with the shoulders.** `take_hit` records impacts relative to `pose.torso_angle` (`Deformable.record(hit, torso_angle)`) and the visuals turn them back, so a dent stays on the shoulder or hand that was hit. A hand or head is dented by shots whose path crossed it before the body, re-seated where the shot entered, depth scaled by part size / body radius. A hand mid-punch has left its wounds behind. Limbs are not dented (shots hit the body circle).
