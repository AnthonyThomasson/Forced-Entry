# Grenades

Spec: `docs/grenades.md` G1 to G6 and G10 (carrying, throwing, flight, fuse). The burst itself is `Explosion` (`domain-projectile`). Tests: `tests/items/grenade_*`.

## Files

- `grenade.gd` (`GrenadeItem`) + `stun_grenade.gd` / `frag_grenade.gd` (each only names its row: `super(KIND)`).
- `thrown_grenade.gd` (`ThrownGrenade`): the live world node, group `live_grenades`. `grenade_throw.gd` (`GrenadeThrow`: `kind`, `from`, `release` (the hand's height, `BodyHeights.release`), `to`, `thrower`, `style`, `apex` (-1: the planner chooses)) is what `ThrownGrenade.spawn(throw, parent)` takes.
- `grenade_arc.gd` (`GrenadeArc`): **the shared arc planner**, a published entry of interface 6 (the human's throw plans with it, the AI judges a lob with it). `grenade_course.gd` (`GrenadeCourse`: `from`, `release`, `to`, `row`, `space`, `passed`, `mask`, `margin`, `apex`, `verify`) is one question to it and `grenade_launch.gd` (`GrenadeLaunch`: `ok`, `direction`, `speed`, `rise`, `apex`, `time`, `touchdown`, `rest`) its answer. `general/small_flight.gd` (`SmallFlight`, General) is the 3D flight stepper the node, the planner's verify and `DroppedItem` all use.
- `grenade_spoon.gd` (`GrenadeSpoon`, an `Item` carried by a Despawner-tracked `DroppedItem`, popped at release); art in `weapon-looks.md`.
- `grenade_config.gd` (`GrenadeConfig`): one row per kind (fuse, windup, `max_range`, `throw_speed`, `restitution`, `friction`, the effect numbers `Explosion` reads) beside the flight numbers every kind shares (`gravity`, `max_apex` (the open sky's cap), `radius`, `min_hop_speed`). The stun's effect on a body is `CharacterStunConfig`.

## Contract

- A grenade is a **count** (`uses_left()`; every other item answers −1 and `is_throwable()` is false). `Character` empties a slot whose count reached 0 and takes up the gun, else the fists. Loadout counts `stuns` / `frags` are 0..3.
- **`throw_stats()` is the one read the AI throws by** (interfaces 6 and 7): `{ kind, max_range, danger_radius, fuse, flash_cover_reach, max_blind }`, made by `GrenadeConfig.stats_of(kind, row)` (`flash_cover_reach` is INF for a frag). `Character.inventory.throw_stats(slot)` forwards it and a live `ThrownGrenade.throw_stats()` answers from the row it was thrown with. Tests: `items/grenade_throw_stats_test`, `ai/grenade/grenade_range_follows_the_item`.
- `secondary` starts the throw: the `throw` stance for `windup`, counted in `tick`; a death or a slot switch during it throws nothing. `_release` spends one, builds the `GrenadeThrow` from the hand's position, `user.weapon.aim_target()` and the item's weapon style, pops the spoon and posts `&"throw"`.

## Flight

`ThrownGrenade` flies a real 3D flight in the twin space (`TwinSpace.space_of(node)`, interface 29) with `SmallFlight`; spec G3.

- **The planner** (`GrenadeArc.plan(course)`, called by `spawn` with `verify = true`) picks the launch: the lowest arc, enumerated by apex from the release height up to the course's top (`_top`: `GrenadeConfig.max_apex` in the open, the roof's underside less the grenade's size over the hand or the aim under a roof: only a real ceiling caps a lob, D11a) in `APEX_STEP` steps (`HIGH_STEP` above the walls' height), that clears what the 3D cast of a cylinder (`radius + margin` wide) meets in the twin space and lands near the aim. Flight time follows from the apex, the reach is what `throw_speed` gives over that time (capped at `max_range`), and where it first touches down is solved analytically so the bounces and the skid end at the aim. A throw that is made also flies each clear arc to rest (the node's own stepper) and keeps the first resting within `FIT_NEAR` of the aim, longer lobs (`FIT_STEP` further out, up to `LOB_BEYOND` past the aim) over a sofa on the skid's way; a rest short of the aim counts `SHORT_COST` times. Budgets: `SWEEPS` swept arcs, `FIT_TRIES` flown ones. `GrenadeArc.work` counts the planner's work (an arc swept, a cast or a ray) so the AI's throw-spot scan is priced by it (`ThrowSpots.block_work`). With no clear arc it returns the highest, `ok = false`. A plan works each arc out and sweeps each lob once (`GrenadeCourse.made`, `Arc.struck`, `Arc.times` / `heights`), yet `work` counts every sweep asked, answered from the plan's memory or not: it is the AI's price, not a clock. Test: `items/grenade_arc_matches_reference` (a frozen pre-cut copy, `tests/_items/grenade_arc_reference.gd`, as the oracle).
- **The AI's question** is `GrenadeArc.clear(course)` (no verify): `AgentThrow.lob_clear` builds the course (`mask` world + vehicle, `margin` `LOB_MARGIN`) from the NPC's posture's release height. Never a separate rule: the AI and the throw are the one planner (test `ai/grenade/throw_planner_agrees_with_flight`).
- **The flight** (`SmallFlight.step`): gravity, a sweep through the twin space (`cast_motion` + `get_rest_info` for the normal) and the floor plane (the floor is a plane, not swept: slab tops are 0.1 px up) and, where a roof stands over the hand or the aim, a ceiling plane at its underside (`SmallFlight.ceiling`, `GrenadeCourse.roof`; none in the open; a dropped item has none and strikes the roof's slab), up to three contacts a frame. A hard strike bounces (reflect and scale by the surface's `restitution`), a soft one settles and, on an upward-facing surface (floor, a piece's top), rolls under `friction` to rest. So it lands on furniture, passes under a doorway's lintel (the wall twin's header, `ObjectTwins`) and bounces off it when too high. Tests: `items/grenade_bounces_in_3d`, `items/grenade_lands_on_furniture`, `items/grenade_flies_under_the_lintel`, `items/grenade_high_lob_bounces_off_the_lintel`, `items/grenade_lobbed_over_cover`, `items/grenade_lobs_over_furniture`.
- **The thrower's own body is passed for the first `THROWER_CLEAR` px**, in the sweep and in the plan alike (`GrenadeCourse.passed` from `GrenadeArc.passed_by`, the thrower's twin, in the query's `exclude`): it leaves the hand about 16 px from the body centre, so a pixel used to decide whether a throw struck its thrower. Test: `items/grenade_passes_its_thrower`.
- It posts `&"grenade"` (`bounce` / `rest`) and, when the fuse ends, calls `Explosion.frag` / `Explosion.stun` (`(at, world, source, row)`). `altitude()` is its underside's height over the floor (or the piece it lies on).
