29. **Main → General, Objects, Vehicles, Character: `TwinSpace`, the twin 3D space** (`general/twin_space.gd`, `general/twin_shapes.gd`; `MatchBuilder.stage` builds it; ADR 0014).
    A 3D physics space beside the 2D simulation holding a 3D twin of each solid 2D body, kept in step with it each physics frame. The 2D simulation is the truth, except for furniture, which a free 3D body leads (below); the real-cover jobs read the twins rule by rule: a bullet's 3D ray and a hit's owner (`Projectile`, `Pointer.aim_hit`), a fragment, a throw, a sight line, and (furn3d) the nav's holes, the sit/lie refusal and a vehicle's shove of a led piece, through the 2D footprint a led piece keeps; the rest follow (a bullet's 3D ray, a fragment, a throw, a sight line), then the bodies flip to 3D one class at a time and the direction reverses (ADR 0014).
    - **The space:** `TwinSpace` (`class_name`, a never-drawn `SubViewport` named `Twins` with a 3D world of its own, child of the match's world; Main's `MatchBuilder.stage` makes one per world, so a match and a test world have one, headless too, and each a fresh Jolt space: Jolt's results follow the slots its bodies hold, so a shared space made a world depend on what ran before). `space() -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D`, and
      the static `TwinSpace.space_of(node) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` (any node of the sim in the tree, found through its 2D space) is what a reader casts into. The 3D engine is Jolt (`physics/3d/physics_engine`, `project.godot`; `max_linear_velocity` 5000 px/s caps every dynamic body: vehicles3d, ragdoll3d).
    - **Reading a hit:** `TwinSpace.owner_of(collider: Object) -> Node2D` maps a ray or shape result's `collider` (a twin) to the 2D body it stands for (null for anything else), so a bullet then calls that body's
      `get_surface()` (3); `twin_of(body) -> Node3D` the other way (null for a body with none); `TwinSpace.twin_for(body) -> Node3D` the same for a reader holding no `TwinSpace` (a meta on the body),
      `TwinSpace.space_for(space2d: PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` the 3D space beside a 2D direct space (the bridge a reader that still holds the 2D space casts through, `SightRay`
      for the AI: null when no twin space stands beside it), `TwinShapes.top_of(twin) -> float` the top of a twin's shapes in px (kept on the twin). Layers: `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_WORLD` (walls, floors, furniture, doors), `TWIN_VEHICLE`, `TWIN_CHARACTER`, `TWIN_ALL` (11);
      a twin is on its layer only while its body's `collision_layer` is not 0 (a dead character, a knocked-down door) and a seated character's cylinder is disabled. Heights are px above the floor on
      `RenderConfig.unit` (`PlanSpace`, 11): walls 96 (with a lintel, a header from 84 to 96, over each doorway: `ObjectTwins._lintels`, which a grenade's flight and a ray at that height meet; a roofed `Floor` also a roof body on the walls' top, 96 to 98, on its own layer `TWIN_ROOF` (out of `TWIN_ALL`: a ray from the sky, the aim, reaches what is under it), none over the open ground, the street or a roofless shell: `Floor.roofed`), a door's leaf 84, a floor slab's top at the ground (a road a kerb below), furniture its `PieceHeights` parts (28), a vehicle its body's belt, a character its posture's head top (`BodyHeights`).
    - **The hook:** `TwinSpace.builders: Array[Callable]`, each `Callable(node: Node2D) -> Node3D`, set before `watch`. For each physics body (and each member of a group `track_group(group, kind)` names, `kind` the script its members are of: the floors have no
      body) the first builder that answers a node wins, null passes on. The node is a `CollisionObject3D` with its shapes built in code in the frame of the 2D body's origin (`TwinShapes.lift_body(body, twin, base, top)`
      lifts a body's own 2D shapes, `TwinShapes.box(rect, local, base, top)` / `lift(shape, local, base, top)` one footprint) on a `collision_layer` of `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_*`, `collision_mask` 0. Any `CollisionObject3D` is written whenever its body moves (Objects' twins are `StaticBody3D`s, which keeps Jolt's step free of always-active bodies; a vehicle's and a character's are
      `AnimatableBody3D`s, `sync_to_physics` off); one whose body never moves is written once. A node with `twin_update(body: Node2D)` has it run each frame it follows (a character's cylinder follows its posture). Each domain registers its own
      (`register(space)`) and Main wires them in `MatchBuilder.stage`: Objects' `ObjectTwins` (walls, doors, floors, solid furniture), Vehicles' `VehicleTwins`, Character's `CharacterTwin`.
    - **The update rule** (`TwinSpace.update()`, once a physics frame at `PRIORITY` -100, before every body's own script): each followed body's `global_transform` is read and its twin written **only when it changed**; a sleeping
      `RigidBody2D` is read once more as it falls asleep, then parked until it wakes, with a slow look (`PARK_SPREAD` frames round) for a script that moves it asleep; a body that never moves (frozen at twin time: never followed, even if unfrozen later) is written once, when twinned; one that joins the tree is twinned on the next update; one that leaves frees its twin. A reader finds
      a rigid body's twin exactly where the 2D step left it; a door or a character, which a script moves, is one frame (1/60 s) behind (the sync runs before the script that moves it; a write reaches Jolt at once), a teleported sleeper up to `PARK_SPREAD` frames. Read-outs: `count`, `followed`, `written`. A dent does not reshape a twin.
    - **Furniture leads (D13/D14, #194 furn3d; ADR 0014):** a free piece (not anchored, on the floor) is a `PieceBody` (`objects/piece_body.gd`, a `RigidBody3D`, config `PieceBodyConfig`), the truth of the piece: it slides, spins and tips under Jolt from
      its real parts and catalogue mass, and its 2D `RigidBody2D` is the frozen footprint written from it each frame. `TwinSpace.leader_of(body) -> RigidBody3D` names the 3D body a footprint follows (null for any other body),
      so a mover shoves a led piece through `Knockback.push` (5); a script that moves a footprint is adopted (the 3D body is put there). A tipped piece (`PieceBody.tipped()`, more than `PieceBodyConfig.tipped_degrees` off upright) has the hull of its
      projected shapes for a footprint collider (`footprint_stamp` tells the nav), and `EnvironmentObject.get_interactions` drops every `move_to` action (sit, lie) from it. A world nested under the twin space's root in a `SubViewport` of its own
      is twinned by its own space (`node.get_viewport()`), not by the outer one. A piece, character or wall that spawns overlapping a piece is settled clear (`PieceBody.settle_out`).
      Readers reach a led piece's `PieceBody` duck-typed (`plan_velocity`, `plan_spin`, `centre_height`, `inverse_yaw_inertia`; the footprint's `footprint_stamp`: `dependency-notes.md`).
    - **Sharing space:** `TwinSpace.share_space(a, b, shared)` mirrors a character-and-piece 2D collision exception onto their twins (a sit or lie begins it, `CharacterUse.restore_cleared` ends it). `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_GROUND` is the plane under the twins.
