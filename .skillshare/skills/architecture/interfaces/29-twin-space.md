29. **Main → General, Objects, Vehicles, Character: `TwinSpace`, the twin 3D space** (`general/twin_space.gd`, `general/twin_shapes.gd`; `MatchBuilder.stage` builds it; ADR 0014).
    A 3D physics space (Jolt) holding a 3D twin of each solid body, in step with its plan handle each physics frame. **No 2D physics is left** (ADR 0014, del2d 17c): every sim root (wall, floor, piece, door, vehicle, character) is a plain `Node2D` handle with no 2D body, shape or layer; a free piece, a character and a vehicle are led from 3D, a door's leaf is driven, a wall is written once. `World2D` stays only for `NavigationServer2D` (nav3d replaces it). Readers: a bullet's 3D ray and a hit's owner (`Projectile`, `Pointer.aim_hit`), a fragment, a throw, a sight line, the nav's walls and holes, the sit/lie refusal.
    - **The space:** `TwinSpace` (`class_name`, a never-drawn `SubViewport` named `Twins` with a 3D world of its own; `MatchBuilder.stage` makes one per world, each a fresh Jolt space). `space() -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D`, and
      the static `TwinSpace.space_of(node) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` (any sim node in the tree) is what a reader casts into; `TwinSpace.of_world(world)` its world's own: `View3D` draws it. The 3D engine is Jolt (`physics/3d/physics_engine`; `max_linear_velocity` 5000 px/s caps every dynamic body).
    - **Reading a hit:** `TwinSpace.owner_of(collider: Object) -> Node2D` maps a ray or shape result's `collider` (a twin) to the 2D body it stands for (null for anything else), so a bullet then calls that body's
      `get_surface()` (3); `twin_of(body) -> Node3D` the other way (null for a body with none); `TwinSpace.twin_for(body)` the same for a reader holding no `TwinSpace`,
      `TwinSpace.space_for(space2d: PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` the 3D space beside a 2D direct space (for a reader holding the 2D space, `SightRay`, the AI: null with none), `TwinSpace.space_in(world: World2D)` likewise, `TwinSpace.intersecting(space, query, page := 32) -> Array[Dictionary]` every hit of a shape query, paged; `TwinShapes.top_of(twin) -> float` the top of a twin's shapes in px (kept on the twin), `TwinShapes.footprint_of(shape, at)` a shape's floor polygon. Layers: `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_WORLD` (walls, floors, furniture, doors), `TWIN_VEHICLE`, `TWIN_CHARACTER`, `TWIN_ALL` (11);
      a twin is on its layer only while its body's `collision_layer` is not 0 (a dead character) and a riding character's cylinder is disabled (nothing reads its twin then). Heights are px above the floor on
      `RenderConfig.unit` (`PlanSpace`, 11): walls 96 (with a lintel, a header from 84 to 96, over each doorway: `ObjectTwins._lintels`, which a grenade's flight and a ray at that height meet; a roofed `Floor` also a roof body, 96 to 98, on `TWIN_ROOF` (out of `TWIN_ALL`: the aim's ray from the sky reaches what is under it; none over open ground, the street or a roofless shell: `Floor.roofed`), a door's leaf 84, a floor slab's top at the ground (a road a kerb below), furniture its `PieceHeights` parts (28), a vehicle its body's belt, a character its posture's head top (`BodyHeights`).
    - **The hook:** `TwinSpace.builders: Array[Callable]`, each `Callable(node: Node2D) -> Node3D`, set before `watch`. For each physics body (and each member of `TwinSpace.BODIES` (a wall: a plain `Node2D` plan handle, also in `WALLS`, `HARD`) and of a group `track_group(group, kind)` names: the floors have no
      body) the first builder that answers a node wins, null passes on. The node is a `CollisionObject3D` with its shapes built in code in the frame of the handle's origin (`TwinShapes.box(rect, local, base, top)`, `lift(shape, local, base, top)`) on a `collision_layer` of `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_*`, `collision_mask` 0. Any `CollisionObject3D` is written whenever its body moves (Objects' are `StaticBody3D`s, keeping Jolt's step free of always-active bodies, but a free piece, a door and what leads; a vehicle's is an
      `AnimatableBody3D`, `sync_to_physics` off; a led body (below) writes itself); one whose body never moves is written once. A node with `twin_update(body: Node2D)` has it run each frame it follows. Each domain registers its own
      (`register(space)`) and Main wires them in `MatchBuilder.stage`: Objects' `ObjectTwins` (walls, doors, floors, solid furniture), Vehicles' `VehicleTwins`, Character's `CharacterTwin`.
    - **The update rule** (`TwinSpace.update()`, once a physics frame at `PRIORITY` -100, before every body's own script): each followed body's `global_transform` is read and its twin written **only when it changed**; a sleeping
      rigid body is read once more as it falls asleep, then parked until it wakes, with a slow look (`PARK_SPREAD` frames round) for a script that moves it asleep; a body that never moves is written once, when twinned (an anchored or hung piece, looked at slowly too); one that joins the tree is twinned on the next update; one that leaves frees its twin. A reader finds
      a rigid body's twin exactly where the 2D step left it; a character, which a script moves, is one frame (1/60 s) behind (the sync runs before the script that moves it; a write reaches Jolt at once; a house door is the exception: its `DoorBody` leader writes the 2D body in its own step, and `TwinSpace.retwin(body)` gives a door that falls a fresh twin, a free `DoorSlab`, D18), a teleported sleeper up to `PARK_SPREAD`.  A dent reshapes a wall's twin (`WallTwin`, cut again from the dents: `Wall.segment_rebuilt`), not a
      piece's, a door's or a vehicle's. A wall two rooms share is built once (`WallFactory.seams_for`, 2). Twins are made in tree order (bodies and pieces), then the groups.
    - **Vehicles lead too:** a car or van is a `VehicleBody` (a `RigidBody3D` locked upright), its 2D body the footprint.
    - **Furniture leads (D13/D14, #194 furn3d; ADR 0014):** a free piece (not anchored, on the floor) is a `PieceBody` (`objects/piece_body.gd`, a `RigidBody3D`, config `PieceBodyConfig`), the truth of the piece: it slides, spins and tips under Jolt from
      its real parts and catalogue mass, and its plan handle (a `Node2D` of `BODIES` and `PIECES`, no 2D shape) is the footprint written from it each frame. `TwinSpace.leader_of(body) -> RigidBody3D` names the 3D body a footprint follows (null for any other body),
      so a mover shoves it through `Knockback.push` (5); a script that moves a footprint is adopted. A tipped piece (`PieceBody.tipped()`, more than `PieceBodyConfig.tipped_degrees` off upright) has the hull of its
      projected shapes for a footprint (the nav reads its twin), and `EnvironmentObject.get_interactions` drops every `move_to` action (sit, lie) from it. A world nested under the twin space's root in a `SubViewport` of its own
      is twinned by its own space (`node.get_viewport()`), not by the outer one. What spawns overlapping a piece is settled clear (`PieceBody.settle_out`).
      Readers reach a led piece's `PieceBody` duck-typed (`dependency-notes.md`).
    - **Small things fly in it** (`SmallFlight`, `general/small_flight.gd`, a `RefCounted`; the grenade, a dropped item, a casing, a chip, `GrenadeArc`): `new(radius)`, `place(plan, centre)`, set `velocity`, `rise`, `mask`; `step(space, delta, passed) -> NONE|LANDED|STRUCK|RESTED` (a sphere swept through the twins, one look-ahead cast for up to `HORIZON` frames over open floor), `at`, `height`, `altitude()`, `resting`; `settle(space)`, `release()`, `embedded(space)`, `unsupported(space)`, static `speed_for(distance, up, drop, pull)`.
    - **Sharing space:** `TwinSpace.share_space(a, b, shared)` mirrors a character-and-piece 2D collision exception onto their twins (a sit or lie begins it, `CharacterUse.restore_cleared` ends it).
    - **Characters lead (D13, #194 chars3d-a):** a character's twin is a `CharacterTwin` (a `CharacterBody3D` cylinder), the truth of where it stands; its 2D body is the footprint. `TwinSpace.walker_of(body)` names it, `TwinSpace.real_velocity_of(body)`
      is the plan velocity a body covered (never `get_real_velocity()`). The rest: the `domain-character` skill's movement side file.
