29. **Main → General, Objects, Vehicles, Character: `TwinSpace`, the twin 3D space** (`general/twin_space.gd`, `general/twin_shapes.gd`; `MatchBuilder.stage` builds it; ADR 0014).
    A 3D physics space beside the 2D simulation holding a 3D twin of each solid 2D body, kept in step with it each physics frame. The 2D simulation is the truth, except for furniture and characters, which a 3D body leads (below); the real-cover jobs read the twins rule by rule: a bullet's 3D ray and a hit's owner (`Projectile`, `Pointer.aim_hit`), a fragment, a throw, a sight line, and (furn3d) the nav's holes, the sit/lie refusal and a vehicle's shove of a led piece, through the 2D footprint a led piece keeps; the rest follow (a bullet's 3D ray, a fragment, a throw, a sight line), then the bodies flip to 3D one class at a time and the direction reverses (ADR 0014).
    - **The space:** `TwinSpace` (`class_name`, a never-drawn `SubViewport` named `Twins` with a 3D world of its own; `MatchBuilder.stage` makes one per world, headless too, each a fresh Jolt space: a shared one made a world depend on what ran before). `space() -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D`, and
      the static `TwinSpace.space_of(node) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` (any node of the sim in the tree, found through its 2D space) is what a reader casts into. The 3D engine is Jolt (`physics/3d/physics_engine`, `project.godot`; `max_linear_velocity` 5000 px/s caps every dynamic body: vehicles3d, ragdoll3d).
    - **Reading a hit:** `TwinSpace.owner_of(collider: Object) -> Node2D` maps a ray or shape result's `collider` (a twin) to the 2D body it stands for (null for anything else), so a bullet then calls that body's
      `get_surface()` (3); `twin_of(body) -> Node3D` the other way (null for a body with none); `TwinSpace.twin_for(body) -> Node3D` the same for a reader holding no `TwinSpace` (a meta on the body),
      `TwinSpace.space_for(space2d: PhysicsDirectSpaceState2D) -> PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` the 3D space beside a 2D direct space (the bridge for a reader that holds the 2D space, `SightRay`, the AI: null with no twin space), `TwinSpace.intersecting(space, query, page := 32) -> Array[Dictionary]` every hit of a shape query, paged; `TwinShapes.top_of(twin) -> float` the top of a twin's shapes in px (kept on the twin). Layers: `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_WORLD` (walls, floors, furniture, doors), `TWIN_VEHICLE`, `TWIN_CHARACTER`, `TWIN_ALL` (11);
      a twin is on its layer only while its body's `collision_layer` is not 0 (a dead character) and a seated character's cylinder is disabled. Heights are px above the floor on
      `RenderConfig.unit` (`PlanSpace`, 11): walls 96 (with a lintel, a header from 84 to 96, over each doorway: `ObjectTwins._lintels`, which a grenade's flight and a ray at that height meet; a roofed `Floor` also a roof body, 96 to 98, on `TWIN_ROOF` (out of `TWIN_ALL`: the aim's ray from the sky reaches what is under it; none over open ground, the street or a roofless shell: `Floor.roofed`), a door's leaf 84, a floor slab's top at the ground (a road a kerb below), furniture its `PieceHeights` parts (28), a vehicle its body's belt, a character its posture's head top (`BodyHeights`).
    - **The hook:** `TwinSpace.builders: Array[Callable]`, each `Callable(node: Node2D) -> Node3D`, set before `watch`. For each physics body (and each member of a group `track_group(group, kind)` names, `kind` the script its members are of: the floors have no
      body) the first builder that answers a node wins, null passes on. The node is a `CollisionObject3D` with its shapes built in code in the frame of the 2D body's origin (`TwinShapes.lift_body(body, twin, base, top)`
      lifts a body's own 2D shapes, `TwinShapes.box(rect, local, base, top)` / `lift(shape, local, base, top)` one footprint) on a `collision_layer` of `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_*`, `collision_mask` 0. Any `CollisionObject3D` is written whenever its body moves (Objects' are `StaticBody3D`s, keeping Jolt's step free of always-active bodies, but a free piece, a door and what leads; a vehicle's is an
      `AnimatableBody3D`, `sync_to_physics` off; a led body (below) writes itself); one whose body never moves is written once. A node with `twin_update(body: Node2D)` has it run each frame it follows (a character's cylinder follows its posture). Each domain registers its own
      (`register(space)`) and Main wires them in `MatchBuilder.stage`: Objects' `ObjectTwins` (walls, doors, floors, solid furniture), Vehicles' `VehicleTwins`, Character's `CharacterTwin`.
    - **The update rule** (`TwinSpace.update()`, once a physics frame at `PRIORITY` -100, before every body's own script): each followed body's `global_transform` is read and its twin written **only when it changed**; a sleeping
      `RigidBody2D` is read once more as it falls asleep, then parked until it wakes, with a slow look (`PARK_SPREAD` frames round) for a script that moves it asleep; a body that never moves (frozen at twin time: never followed, even if unfrozen later) is written once, when twinned; one that joins the tree is twinned on the next update; one that leaves frees its twin. A reader finds
      a rigid body's twin exactly where the 2D step left it; a character, which a script moves, is one frame (1/60 s) behind (the sync runs before the script that moves it; a write reaches Jolt at once; a house door is the exception: its `DoorBody` leader writes the 2D body in its own step, and `TwinSpace.retwin(body)` gives a door that falls a fresh twin, a free `DoorSlab`, D18), a teleported sleeper up to `PARK_SPREAD` frames. Read-outs: `count`, `followed`, `written`. A dent reshapes a wall's twin (`WallTwin`: a wall's 3D body is the truth for the 3D reads, a static body built from its segments and cut again from the same dents as the 2D collider, `Wall.segment_rebuilt`; the dent state stays in the 2D `Wall` until the 2D space is deleted), not a
      piece's, a door's or a vehicle's. A wall two rooms share is built once (`WallFactory.seams_for`, 2): one 3D wall and one 2D collider per segment, so a ray through the seam meets one wall whichever side it comes from.
    - **Vehicles lead too:** a car or van is a `VehicleBody` (a `RigidBody3D` locked upright), its 2D body the footprint.
    - **Furniture leads (D13/D14, #194 furn3d; ADR 0014):** a free piece (not anchored, on the floor) is a `PieceBody` (`objects/piece_body.gd`, a `RigidBody3D`, config `PieceBodyConfig`), the truth of the piece: it slides, spins and tips under Jolt from
      its real parts and catalogue mass, and its 2D `RigidBody2D` is the frozen footprint written from it each frame. `TwinSpace.leader_of(body) -> RigidBody3D` names the 3D body a footprint follows (null for any other body),
      so a mover shoves it through `Knockback.push` (5); a script that moves a footprint is adopted. A tipped piece (`PieceBody.tipped()`, more than `PieceBodyConfig.tipped_degrees` off upright) has the hull of its
      projected shapes for a footprint collider (`footprint_stamp` tells the nav), and `EnvironmentObject.get_interactions` drops every `move_to` action (sit, lie) from it. A world nested under the twin space's root in a `SubViewport` of its own
      is twinned by its own space (`node.get_viewport()`), not by the outer one. What spawns overlapping a piece is settled clear (`PieceBody.settle_out`).
      Readers reach a led piece's `PieceBody` duck-typed (`plan_velocity`, `plan_spin`, `centre_height`, `inverse_yaw_inertia`; the footprint's `footprint_stamp`: `dependency-notes.md`).
    - **Sharing space:** `TwinSpace.share_space(a, b, shared)` mirrors a character-and-piece 2D collision exception onto their twins (a sit or lie begins it, `CharacterUse.restore_cleared` ends it). `PhysicsLayers.TWIN_GROUND` is the plane under the twins.
    - **Characters lead (D13, #194 chars3d-a):** a character's twin is a `CharacterTwin` (a `CharacterBody3D` cylinder), the truth of where it stands; its 2D body is the footprint. `TwinSpace.walker_of(body)` names it, `TwinSpace.real_velocity_of(body)`
      is the plan velocity a body covered (never `get_real_velocity()`). The rest: the `domain-character` skill's movement side file.
