27. **Main → Art: `MeshCarrier`, `MeshFollower`, and General's `CameraRig`: the 3D render** (`art/mirror/`, `general/camera_rig.gd`; built by Main's `main_view3d.gd`; ADR 0015, which supersedes ADR 0013). The 3D render is the only render: no 2D physics is left (every sim root is a plan handle, 29) and the view draws the twin space's 3D world through an orthographic camera.
    - **The view:** `Main.start_match` hides the `World`, builds the 3D view (`View3D`, `main_view3d.gd`, a `CanvasLayer` on `RenderConfig.canvas_layer` below the HUD: a `TextureRect` showing a `SubViewport` with the rig, the dusk's lights, the carrier and the follower) before the match. `mirror_world(world)` then points the viewport at `TwinSpace.of_world(world).find_world_3d()`: the meshes stand in the world the bodies' twins are in. `MatchBuilder.stage` builds no 2D lights (20), Vision no 2D picture (25); the overlays draw flat on the ground (below). A test world is shown by it in a window (13); a headless one has no rig (26).
    - **`MeshCarrier`** (`art/mirror/mesh_carrier.gd`, `class_name`, a `Node3D`; Art imports only General's `RenderConfig`, `TwinSpace`, `PlanSpace`; it reads bodies duck-typed: the groups `characters`, `vehicles`, `doors`, `floors`, `wall_thickness`, `look`, `art`):
      - `carry(root: Node) -> void` takes the twin space of `root`'s world and gives each body it twins (`TwinSpace.twinned(body, twin)`, bodies already twinned at once) the mesh its builder makes: **a child of the twin, no transform of its own**, so it stands where the twin does and nothing writes it (a character's holds its 2D turn, written only when it changes). `TwinSpace.retwinned` moves a body's mesh to its new twin in the same call. 2D `(x, y)` is 3D `(x * unit, 0, y * unit)`, `unit` = `RenderConfig.unit`, **the one scale**.
      - **Shown rules**, each frame (`update()` by hand): a mesh hides while its body's `visible` is off, its body is a character riding (`use.is_riding()`), or the player's view does not reach it (`MirrorSight`, V5/V6/V12); a body that dies is hidden unless its mesh stays; a body's `reshaped` builds its mesh again (a wall's mesh replaces its changed segment itself); a body that leaves the tree loses it. Read-outs: `count`, `followed`, `written`, `proxies_of(body)`, `mesh_of(body)`; `leave_out(subtree)` drops a subtree's meshes and carries nothing under it later. `built(body, mesh)` says each mesh built.
      - **Fallbacks:** a body no builder gives a mesh has **none** (no stand-in box: no body holds a 2D shape to read one off, and every kind that ships has a builder). A body the space gave no twin (`TwinSpace.untwinned`: a non-solid piece, a rug, which never moves) has its mesh placed where it stands, once, a child of the carrier.
      - **The mesh hook** (full rules `domain-art/mesh-hook.md`): `builders: Dictionary`, a key -> `Callable(node: Node2D) -> Node3D` in **3D units**, set before `carry()` (Main's `_register_meshes`). Keys: a kind (`MeshKinds.WALL`, `DOOR`, `CHARACTER`, `VEHICLE`, `FURNITURE`, `FLOOR`; `MeshKinds.kind_of`) or a finer key that wins (`MeshKinds.furniture_key(art)`, `floor_key(art)`); `builder_for(body)` says which. A mesh node's own hooks (`mirror_update`, `on_body_died`, `remirror`): `render-3d-meshes.md`.
    - **`MeshFollower`** (`art/mirror/mesh_follower.gd`, a `Node3D`): shows the drawn things that are no body (floor stencils, lying items) with `track(node, builder)` and `track_group(group, builder)`, `follow(root)`; each mesh stands on its node's place, written only when it changed, with the shown rules, `mirror_update` and parking while the node is out of the tree. Same read-outs; `domain-art/follower.md`.
    - **`CameraRig`** (`general/camera_rig.gd`, `class_name`, a `Node3D` that owns an orthographic `Camera3D` as `camera`): `bind(source: Camera2D,
      viewport: SubViewport, rect: Control = null)` follows the sim's `Camera2D`, which stays the focus, zoom and shake source (Sound, the cull and
      the tests read it): each frame the camera frames `source.get_screen_center_position()` (focus plus shake offset) at `source.zoom` (ortho size: the screen's height in 2D px over the zoom, times
      `unit`), at the pitch `RenderConfig.pitch_degrees` (80, near top-down) and `yaw` (degrees, `RenderConfig.yaw_degrees` 0 to start: north up). **Q / E turn it a quarter:** a press of a camera-turn key
      (`Keybinds.camera_turns_pressed()`) steps `yaw` `CameraTurnConfig.step_degrees` (90), eased, ending exactly on N, E, S or W (`turning()`).
      It binds `Pointer` (26) to its camera and the picture's rect (larger than the window), sizes the `SubViewport` so a picture pixel is whole screen
      pixels (`texel_match`: `round(zoom)`, at most `max_lines` rows; the window plus 1 px, times `oversample`). In 3D the controller's zoom is a whole step of `Zoom3dConfig.steps` and the focus snaps to
      that grid along the camera's right and up (`rect` shifted back by the remainder), so following does not shimmer. `texel() -> float` is the world units a picture
      pixel covers; `internal_size() -> Vector2i` the picture's size in pixels.
    - **The pixel post** (Art `art/post/`, `PixelPostConfig`): the view's meshes share one toon `ShaderMaterial` (`ToonMaterial.make()`; a colour per instance) whose light is the dusk's (20, `domain-art/textured-toon.md`: the ambient plus each light stepped into bands, capped); `PixelPost.attach(camera, oversample)` adds a full-screen
      outline quad under the camera (a dark line where the depth steps, `silhouette_px` wide, or the normal creases, `crease_px`; `PixelPost.refresh(node, oversample)`). `View3D` shows the picture
      through a nearest-filtered `TextureRect`; it can render `supersample` (at rest) or `turn_supersample` (turning) times larger and average down through a filtered stage (`View3D.shown`), both 1: 2 flickers more on textured art.
    - **The flat overlays** (`GroundLayer`, `general/ground_layer.gd`; `domain-general/camera3d.md`): the 3D view
      is up `ViewLayers.overlay` moves every debug overlay under a `CanvasLayer` on
      `Pointer.screen_transform`: they lie flat on the 3D floor at any yaw, over the picture and under the HUD (`OverlayRegistry.overlays_of`).
    - **Wall fade** (rule 9, `domain-art/wall-fade.md`): Art's `WallFade` (Main's `View3D` makes it, `listen(carrier)`) fades the walls, doors and tall furniture
      between the camera and the player (spectating: every character). It reads, duck-typed: `MeshCarrier.built(body, mesh)`, General's `CameraController.focus_targets() -> Array[Node2D]` (read only) and Objects' `WallMesh.segments() -> Dictionary` (index -> instance) with its `segment_replaced(index, part)`. It writes General's `FadedTwins.hold`/`release` (Pointer, 26, reads `holds`).
    - **Tuning:** `RenderConfig` (General: the camera, `texel_match`, `max_lines`, `canvas_layer`, `overlay_layer`; `CameraTurnConfig`, `Zoom3dConfig`),
      `RenderLightConfig` (General, the backdrop; the lights are Lighting's, 20), `PixelPostConfig` and `ToonLightConfig` (Art).
    - **The dusk's globals** (`ToonLight`, set by Lighting): `set_ambient`, `set_sky`, `set_rooms` (each an image and its rect), `clear`.
    - **The vision hook** (the player's view in 3D, `docs/player-vision.md` V12; `domain-art/vision-hook.md`): Vision sets global shader uniforms through `ToonMask.set_mask(texture, world_rect, unseen_brightness)` (the cone),
      `set_memory(texture, world_rect, look: Vector4, tint)` (ever seen), `clear()`; default everything visible. `MeshNodes.moving(node, on)` marks what moves (drawn in the cone only); the carrier and follower hide a body the cone misses.
    - **The 2D art on the meshes** (ADR 0013 addendum): a mesh wears its painter's unlit render (`PixelArt.render_flat`, `domain-art/flat-paint.md`) through `MeshNodes.textured` (`domain-art/textured-toon.md`). The 2D painters are the texture source.
    - **Which domain has meshes** and who plugs each in (`ObjectMeshes` ... `PhysicsMeshes`): `render-3d-meshes.md`.
