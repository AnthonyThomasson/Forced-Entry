# Body: hands, punch, kick, legs

Specs: `docs/cover-and-stance.md` P5 (legs touch the world). Tests: `tests/character/` (`punch_turns_furniture_test`, `kick_*_test`, `legs_rest_on_walls_test`, `leg_press_turns_furniture_test`).

## Hands (`character_hands.gd`, a child `Node2D`, `anatomy.hands`)

Owns hand geometry, the punch tween and punch hit detection. Exposes `hand_position(i)` (local), `world(i)`, `punch(i)`, `punch_amount(i)`, `is_attacking()` and the `punched` signal. Two circles rest at a pose built in each hand's frame (`hand_angles[hand]` + its perpendicular): the stance's `rest_forward` / `rest_lateral` plus `pose.arms.hand_offset(hand, along)` for walk swing and recoil, collision-resolved so they rest on walls and objects (the rig's arm just bends more). A punch swings the fist to `punch_gap + reach` beyond the body edge, so the stance does not change how far it lands. `hand_radius` and `punch_gap` are exports.

## Punch hit detection

A swing strikes through `body_strike.gd` (`strike(character, blow, struck, first_only, on_hit)`; `blow` a `MeleeBlow`, shared with the kick): a circle query on `PhysicsLayers.SOLID` at the fist's *raw* punch position (it extends into what is hit, unlike the drawn hand that rests on the surface), deduped per swing (`struck`). Each new hit shoves the body through the pushable contract (`apply_impulse(impulse, at)`, `at` the fist's offset, or the butt's for a long gun, so a piece struck near an end turns) and deals damage, both scaled by the swing's range of motion: through `take_blow(blow.kind, damage, impulse)` where the body has it (a house door; the punch's `MeleeBlow.kind` is the default `&"punch"`), else `take_damage`. It emits `punched(hand, body, damage)` before the damage; the character re-emits `hit_landed`. A punch pushes but never deforms; walls take damage without moving. Tuning: `CharacterMeleeConfig.punch_*`.

**A long gun's butt strike** (`domain-items`): the hand's extension is shortened by `strike.forward()` + `strike.short` and the hit is queried at the butt (`strike.end`) once it is ahead of the fist. The other hand is placed rigidly on the barrel (`_raw_hand_position`: the weapon hand's place plus `strike.direction × strike.support_gap`, gripping from the first frame), so both arms follow the turning gun. The swing takes `strike.tempo` times a fist's time (the damage's range of motion is normalised by it), and the redraw key carries both hands' punch amounts, since the gun turns with them.

## The leg kick (`character_kick.gd`, `anatomy.kick`)

Independent of the held item, always the right leg (side 0), stepped after `move_and_slide` and before `pose.update`. `anatomy.leg_kick()` starts it (false when dead, crouched, in an interaction or already kicking); a strike then a recover phase run; `amount()` is the foot's 0..1 extension, read by the rig through `anatomy.leg_kick_amount(i)`. The rig lifts the kicking ankle to `kick_foot_height` and throws it out along `facing`. During the strike a circle of `kick_radius` at `kick_reach × amount()` is queried through `body_strike.gd` (at most one body a frame); each body is hit once per kick, shoved by `kick_impulse` at the foot (`blow.kind = &"kick"`, which `body_strike.gd` hands a door's `take_blow`, interface 3), reported as `kicked(body, damage)` and dealt `kick_damage`. The character posts `hit_landed(body, damage, 2)` and the `&"hit"` event with outcome `punch`; like a punch it never deforms. Tuning: `CharacterMeleeConfig.kick_*`.

## Legs (`character_legs.gd`, stepped after the pose)

Each ankle is swept from the body centre to where the unobstructed rig would put it (a `leg_radius` circle through `body_sweep.gd`, as the hands are) and rests at the first wall, solid piece or character it meets, sliding the leftover. A knee that would pass through something moves the foot back (`KNEE_TRIES`); the rig reads the result through `anatomy.legs.ankle(i)` (null = unobstructed) and re-solves the knee (`CharacterRig.knee_at`). A foot or knee swinging into a pushable (`apply_impulse` + `get_mass`, never another character) shoves it by `leg_push_impulse` through `CharacterPush.shove`, so a piece pressed near an end turns and its contact point is carried at no more than walking pace; a foot at rest pushes nothing. Anatomy refreshes one conservative near-solid query shared by both legs and hands; when clear, ankle casts are skipped and hands use their raw positions. The hand radius covers its item reach and punch/support offsets, and disabling the gate restores the plain path (`body_gate_matches_plain_test.gd`). Cleared while busy in an interaction and on death.
