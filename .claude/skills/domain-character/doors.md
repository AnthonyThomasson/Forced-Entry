# Doors: the Use key and the scroll (interfaces 7 and 24, `docs/doors.md` DR6, DR7)

`use.doors` (`CharacterDoors`, a `RefCounted` held by `use`, not by the body, which is at its 15 variables, ADR 0004) is the character's use of an Objects `Door`, found duck-typed by the group `doors`: `door_in_reach()` (the standing door nearest `movement.aim_point` whose leaf, a segment from `leaf_pose(angle())` and `leaf_size()`, has its nearest point within `CharacterDoorConfig.reach` of the body and ahead of it; none when dead), `distance_to(door)`, `use()`, `open_fully()` and `turn(steps)`, each taking an optional held door (acted on while `reaches(door)`) and false with none in reach. It reads no input event: the puppet's `use()` / `turn_door()` and the AI's locomotion (`open_fully`) call it as the player's controller does.

`use.use_target()` is `&"vehicle"` seated, `&"door"` when a door in reach is nearer than every boardable handle (`CharacterVehicleUse.nearest_door_distance()`), else `&"vehicle"` or `&""`. `use.try_use()` acts on it at once. `player_controller.gd` is the input side: on the press of `Keybinds.USE` it reads `use_target()`; a vehicle is acted on at once, a door is remembered (`_held_door`). Its `_input` takes a scroll (`Keybinds.scroll_notches(event)`) while Use is held on a door, turns that door by it (`doors.turn(notches, _held_door)`; the camera, which reads `_unhandled_input`, never sees the event) and marks it scrolled (`_use_scrolled`); with no door held the event goes on to the camera. On the release an unscrolled hold toggles the held door. Tests: `tests/character/door_use_key_test.gd`, `door_scroll_open_test.gd`, `tests/general/use_vehicle_key_test.gd`.

A door in the way is also a pushable for `_apply_pushes` (`SKILL.md`, "Walking into furniture"): the slow-down by `get_mass` is the door's `weight`, and a walking push (a shove far under a kick's) never counts against a latch. A punch and a kick strike it through `body_strike.gd`'s `apply_impulse` then `take_blow(kind, amount, impulse)`, the strike naming its kind (`MeleeBlow.kind`; `docs/doors.md` DR10).
