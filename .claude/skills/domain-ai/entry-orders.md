# AI entry: ordered stacks, joins and holds

An entry under a leader's order (`docs/leadership.md`: LD9 the stack, LD13 held for the go, LD14 a frag, LD17 the human leader waited for, LD24 / LD25 the join, D38 the human leader's rally). Code: `entry/entry_orders.gd` (`orders`), `entry_readiness.gd`, `behavior_entries.gd`, `behavior_orders.gd`. The squad that issues these orders is `team.md`. Back to `entry.md`.

## An ordered stack (LD9)

- An `entry` leaf from the ORDER node carries `order_id`, `bang` / `mode`, `stack_at` or `split`, `wait_go`, `go`. `begin` keeps them (`orders.id`, `orders.side`, `orders.hold`, ...) and `radio.say` adds `order_id`, `order_bang`, `order_side`, `order_mode`, `order_hold`, `order_go`, `order_frame` to every call (an unordered member's calls carry `StackClaim.DEFAULTS`). The one builder of a claim's order fields is `orders.terms(entry)` / `StackClaim.order_terms`; `StackClaim.encode` / `decode` carry them to every reader's `ClaimLedger`.
- A stack is ordered when this member's entry is, or a teammate's claim carries an `order_id` (`orders.claim`, `orders.stack_ordered`). Then the side is the order's for every member (`orders.ordered_side` through `claim.ask_side` and `claim.keep_slot`, D6 on a split: `DoorStack.split_side`), and the mode is the order's (an unordered member follows it, `orders.follow_order`, D10) with no suspect-room bang, no first claimer's `_go_quiet`, no `listen.maybe_upgrade` and no listen. Only a `bang_off` from a claim under the same order vetoes (`grenade.vetoed`). `DoorStack.mode` (`grenade.stack_bangs`) is the stack's mode.
- **D7: the barrier.** `readiness.stack_ready` waits for every other living recipient of the order (`orders.recipients`: the live order's `to` from `AgentPerception.orders.current()`, `Squad.alive` via `Entry.world.squad`) to have radioed past `claim`; for the human leader while it holds a slot (`orders.waits_for`, `orders.leads`, LD17); and for an unordered member `ready_timeout` from `readiness.all_in_at`. All of it is capped at `tuning.entry.order_ready_cap` from the first ordered `ready` (`orders.ready_at`, `orders.capped`); `members_ahead` drops a recipient still at `claim` only then. `solo` is false while another recipient is coming.
- The order ends when the room is cleared (`AgentPerception.entry.mark_cleared`, `done`).
- **The approach (D4, D11).** Before the claim goes out `Behavior.entries._begin_pending` sets `state.held_back` and `entries._detour_first` reads the whole way (`Entry.approach_points()`, leg by leg on `AgentPerception.views.walk_route`; it stands up to `ROUTE_WAIT` while that map is `pending`, which also covers slots not laid yet, `Entry.laid`): a threshold into a room not cleared, and (unless another recipient is coming, `entries._recipients_coming`) a doorway view it passes, is a detour first (`_entry.reset()`, `entries._begin_detour`), with no claim made; otherwise the entry `begin`s afresh from the claims heard meanwhile and claims. On the walk `entries._guard_approach` catches what comes up (`entry-guard.md`). `orders.ordered_stack()` sets `AgentPerception.views.ordered` each tick, which keeps its walks out of the views, counts the room its stack holds as clear (`views._stack_room`, D20) and, for an NPC with `tuning.entry.enabled` off, a room seen empty (`_seen_empty`, D11).
- `Behavior.set_leaf` leaves a running ordered entry (or the detour in front of it) alone when the same order is decided again (`orders.same`). Tests: `tests/ai/entry/order_stack_*` (`order_stack_waits_for_leader`, `order_stack_joiner_grace`), `stack_dead_end_refused`, `stack_reach_*`.

## A join (LD24, LD25)

The squad's rally: the leader's own claim gives each follower holding no order a `stack` order with mode `join`, whose option is an `entry` with `join: true` (`StackClaim.is_join(params)`, the one test of it for order, leaf params and claim alike). `begin` leaves the order's id out (`orders.id` ""), so the entry is the member's own unordered one joining the leader's stack: the team's side, the first claimer's mode (D10), E17's suspect-room bang and `maybe_upgrade`, the listen, the joiner's `ready_timeout`, and `orders.bang` false. `orders.join` marks every call `join: true`, so its squad never takes a join's claim for its holder's own (D30). `Behavior` keeps the `order_id` in the leaf and leaves a join out of the ordered stack's guarded way (`Orders.ordered_entry`). A join whose `begin` fails (no slot at a dead end) is ended `refused`. It ends `done` at the room cleared, `cancel`led when the leader radios `abort`, or `refused`; in `approach` / `stack` its end forces a decision at once (`orders.join_over`, "join over"), from the pie on it runs to its end. Tests: `ai/entry/join_*`, `ai/stack/human_leader_rallies`, `human_rally_spares_ordered`.

## Held for the leader's go (LD13)

A stack with `wait_go` (`orders.hold`) is `orders.held_for_go()` (`DoorStack.held(claim.me(entry))`) until its go latches (`readiness.released`). Held, each member radios `ready` and holds its slot (`readiness.holding`, read as `holding_for_go()`): no pie, no listen, no bang (`grenade.due` calls none), no crossing. `orders.note_go()`, each step, latches the go the first time any of these holds:
- the live order has `go: true` (the leader's `go`, D13, taken by `orders.process` whenever it came);
- the stack's claims say it came (`DoorStack.released(order_id, into, since)`): a claim under the order saying `order_go`, or a member of the stack (under the order, or the human) crossing or through into that room (D14). The human leader stepping in is its go, but only on a crossing begun since the order was issued (`crossed_frame` at or after `since`; -1 releases nothing). A claim left from an earlier entry through the doorway, under another order or into the other room, releases nothing. The squad's `view()` reads the same rule;
- the leader that gave the order is gone or dead (D12; never for a rally).

It then re-radios its latest call with `order_go`; released, it goes on the ready barrier as any ordered stack. `entries.apply` radios `in_position` while `holding_for_go()`. Tests: `ai/entry/order_stack_hold_for_go`, `order_stack_go_latched`, `order_stack_hold_human_go`, `order_stack_hold_leader_dies`, `order_stack_hold_ignores_earlier_entry`; the rule itself `tests/ai/door_stack_suite.gd`.

## Held for the human leader (LD24, D38)

A join of the human leader's rally (`wait_go`, `orders.hold`) is held the same way, with no cap: `DoorStack.hold` gives `{ order_id: "", into, since }` (`since` the join's `order_frame`, from its own claim or the lowest slot's join that still stacks), and `DoorStack.released("", into, since)` (`_releases_rally`) is a joiner of that rally saying `order_go` or crossing, or the human crossing since. `orders.rallied()` (a hold with no order id) makes `orders.mode_set()` true as `stack_ordered()` does: no suspect-room turn, no first-claimer mode, no `maybe_upgrade`, no listen; `grenade.vetoed` is the mode's own veto (a rally member gave its grenade up), never a `bang_off` from outside the rally. `note_go()` takes the go's mode (`orders.mode` from the order's `go_mode`; `state.bang` from `DoorStack.mode`, `_rally_mode`). Once released, `members_ahead` drops the human unless it is crossing, so the first AI is the point.

A held member fights only on the stack's side: `AgentPerception.orders.held_room()` is the confinement's `keep_out`, which the planner's COMBAT filter, the fight and `Combat`'s spots ask; its walks go round that room (`Locomotion` `doors.keep_out_of`, `behavior-locomotion.md`) and the entry guard never takes it in. Tests: `tests/ai/stack/rally_*`, `ai/entry/order_stack_hold_*`; probes `tests/_ai/rally_probe.gd`, `tests/_ai/held_fight_probe.gd`.
