# AI locomotion: pathing, bodies and doorway views

The pathing layer of the behaviour sub-domain: `scenes/ai/behavior/locomotion.gd` (`Locomotion`, a `Node` child of `Behavior` that owns the `NavigationAgent2D`) and its parts in `locomotion/`, each built once with it: `crowd` (`loco_crowd.gd`: the known bodies a walk accounts for, the holds), `give_way` (`loco_give_way.gd`), `stall` (`loco_stall.gd`: sampling for the stuck overlay) and `doors` (`loco_doors.gd`: the walk round the doorway views). Entered from `behavior.md`. The constants below live in those files; read them there.

## Pathing

- All movement goes through `move_to(dest, within := 0)`. Steering aims at `get_next_path_position()`, a navmesh waypoint, so it is wall-safe; for an unreachable target it steps toward the closest reachable point. There is no RVO and no straight-at-the-destination fallback (with no agent it steers straight and arrives within `tuning.pace.arrive_dist`).
- Furniture on the route (a piece shoved off its baked hole) is bulldozed by the character's own push physics. Navigation bakes furniture in as holes and rings the house with a walkable strip (`domain-navigation`).
- `reachable(p)` snaps a point onto the navmesh (every leaf destination goes through it); `reached(point, within)` reports arrival at the free spot it took, never while held; `path_ahead()` returns the walk just given (`{ path, index }`), which the entry guard and `Watch.travel_point` read. `reachable` and `on_navmesh` use the plain map.
- Stack slots arrive within `StackClaim.SLOT_REACH`, tighter than the agent's own distance, because a member a stride short of its slot stands on the next one.
- Which room a point lies in is `perception.confinement.room_at`; `Locomotion.rooms` is set by `Behavior`.

## Bodies (issue #53)

A walk accounts for the characters the NPC KNOWS of, and never walks into one. `Behavior.errand.set_up_walk` hands `crowd.bodies` the perception's contacts each tick (seen, or seen within `tuning.memory.contact_ttl`). A body it has not seen does not exist for the walk; one out of sight whose last spot it can now see empty has moved on (`errand._walk_bodies`). `crowd.ignore_id` leaves out the one a `melee` closes on. A body slower than `REST_SPEED` is at rest. Each walk is in one of four ways, checked once a physics frame (`_check`):

- **plain:** the world's own map. A known body at rest closer than `PASS_CLEARANCE` to the path within `LOOKAHEAD`, or one on the goal, ends it.
- **doors:** the Navigation avoid map (`NavBuilder.avoid_map`: the planning bake with the doorway views carved out, E18), for a walk `Behavior` marks `doors.avoid_doors` (`entries.keeps_out_of_views`, set by `errand.set_up_walk`); an `entry` primitive also sets `doors.avoid_entering = "<opening>><into>"` so its approach keeps out of the room it enters and its own doorway's view. `doors.update` asks perception's views part (`doors.views`, handed `perception.views` by `Behavior.setup`) for `walk_route(goal, avoid_entering)` each frame until decided (plain while the avoid map bakes, `pending`), then every `RECHECK` while round the views. It takes the avoid map when the plain route comes into a view and the way round is within `door_avoid_ratio` x plain + `door_avoid_slack`, else plain (and `passing_ahead` secures what it crosses, E15). A body at rest on a doors walk sends it to the steering map (round) or holds it; the steering map has no views carved, so E15 secures what that crosses. The last stretch short of a goal off the avoid map is walked plain (`_last_stretch`).
  - **A held stack's member (D38)** has `doors.keep_out_of(opening, room)` given the held stack each tick (`confinement.keep_out`; fight walks and order walks alike, not an evade). A walk whose plain way goes into that room is routed round it and its doorway's view however long (`views.walk_enters`, `walk_route(goal, _held_entry)`, a `held` walk), and stands where it is while that route bakes (`doors.waiting()`), because walking the plain way meanwhile would take it in. It never goes round a body by a way into the room (`doors.enters_kept_out`).
- **round:** the Navigation steering map with every character at rest carved out, when its route reaches the goal (or ends within `LAST_LEG` of it with the plain way passing no known body at rest: a slot between two taken ones lies inside their carved margins) and passes no body at rest (`crowd.goes_round`). It follows whatever detour that takes, out of the room included. A round walk done short of a goal no body stands on walks the rest plain; a held one looks again every `RECHECK` for a route that cannot flip between two holders.
- **held:** no way round (the body seals the only way, or just stopped and the 0.75 s re-baked map has not carved it yet), or the body stands on the goal. The NPC walks on until `STOP_GAP` from the body and stands, looks every `RECHECK` for a way round (avoid map first, then steering map: `crowd.check_held`) and moves on the moment nothing known is in the way. After `crowd.WAIT`, then every `REPLAN_EVERY`, it re-plans once the steering map shows the body it is held by (`CARVED_GAP`) or `CARVE_WAIT` past that time whatever the map shows. A hold that begins again within `HOLD_GAP` of game time (`GameClock`: a pause is no gap) and `HOLD_MOVED` of the last one is the same hold and keeps its clocks (`crowd.begin_hold`). A body on the goal sends the walk to the nearest free spot (`give_way.free_spot`); any other hold raises `crowd.take_blocked()`, on which `Behavior._way_blocked` re-plans whatever walks: `move` / `engage` / `melee` / `defend` / `ambush` / `interact` (and a `throw` still closing) end with `task.resolve_reason` "way blocked" and the controller re-decides; an `entry` re-plans its own walk (`Entry.way_blocked`, `entry.md`). An evade holds the best spot it has until the burst.

The steering map is the one `NavBuilder.steering_map` names when `setup` runs. A match places its NPCs only once both maps are `NavBuilder.ready` (`domain-main`); `crowd.goes_round` waits for both too.

## Giving way and keeping off

- `give_way.apply`: of two walkers in view whose courses come within `GIVE_CLEARANCE` in the next `GIVE_HORIZON` (or the time the NPC's own walk still takes, if shorter, so a follower closing on its spot never yields to a leader walking on, LD21), the one with the right of way walks on (`_has_way`: the other sees it, and its instance id is the lower; one that cannot see it cannot give way). The other steps off the walker's line, else waits, at most `GIVE_MAX`. It stops for a walker closer than `GIVE_IMMINENT` even with the right of way.
- `give_way.keep_off`, last of all (and after the combat back-off): drops the part of a step heading into a known body closer than `KEEP_OFF`, so the NPC slides past (the agent cuts corners by its `path_desired_distance`). With nothing left it stands.
- `crowd.holding()` is true whenever the walk stands or steps aside for a body (held, giving way, kept off, at the free spot beside a taken destination); the entry's crossing then does not push on straight (E4). `crowd.held_by()`, `held_at()` and `held_time()` are what an entry asks before giving its slot up for a hold; `seals(p)` is what it asks of each waypoint left (`entry.md`, Bodies on the way).
- `debug_state()["loco"]` adds `way`, `held_for`, `giving_way`, `last_leg`.
