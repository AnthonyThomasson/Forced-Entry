# AI squad: leader, orders, following, rally, ambush rule

`Squad` (`scenes/ai/team/squad.gd`, interface 21; `docs/leadership.md` LD3 to LD7, LD19 to LD26, LD29, D31 to D36) and its parts. Back to `team.md`.

## Shape

One per side, a `Node` under the match's `World` (or a test world), built after the characters are armed: `MatchBuilder.build_squads` calls `setup(roster: SquadRoster, openings, rooms)` (the roster, `squad_roster.gd`: `faction`, `members`, `human`, `claim_ttl`, `ambush_attacks`) and `bind_squad` hands each NPC its `squad`. Parts built with it, each holding it: `stacks` (`squad_stacks.gd`), `ambush` (`squad_ambush.gd`), `_follow` (`squad_follow.gd`). It reads members only through the Character API and the bus. An NPC member (one that follows) has a child in the group `npc_controllers` at `setup` (`_npcs`).

- **Leader:** the highest `wardrobe.rank`, the first spawned among equals (the human when it leads). When the leader dies the next leads within the frame and `&"leader"` `{ faction, leader, previous }` is posted (only tests listen).
- **`issue(request)`** turns a leader's plain request into an `&"order"` and posts it (`_post_order` stamps it with the `GameClock.frame()` as `frame`, as it does a `go`). Kinds: `defend_room`, `stack`, `ambush`, `cancel`. A click means the room key or the doorway nearest `pos` (`_door_for`, `_door_of`); a stack's `into` is the room on the far side of the wall plane; `stack_at` or `split`; `mode` quiet / stun / frag (`MODES` is the stack seam's `StackClaim.MODES`); `wait_go` (LD13). An ambush names a doorway by click or by id (`_ambush_door`), with `room` on the click's side and `beyond` the other (LD15, D17). Orders carry the target point `pos` (drawn by the UI's order markers). No `to` means every living member but the leader.
- **A `go` request** (`_go`, LD13) posts a `go` order to every living member holding a held stack at the doorway nearest `pos` that no go has released, whatever its `to` (D13), and replaces nothing. When the leader that gave held stacks dies the squad posts their go itself (`auto: true`, D12).
- **`resolve(request)`** is the same resolution, read-only, without posting: what the command menu names its items from.
- **`view()`** is `{ leader, members: [{ node, order, step, held, follow_spot }], stacks, trail, leash }`. Steps are read off the members' `order_step` callouts; a released held stack carries `go: true`; an order radioed `done` is no longer held (`order` {}, `step` `done`), a `refused` one still is. `held` comes from `_waits_for_go`, the one place that rule is worked out (the UI reads it). A go is latched by `_latch_go` on each claim heard at that doorway (`DoorStack.released`, same rule as `entry-orders.md`) and kept in `_released`.
- **`stacks`** (LD18) is every stack the side is forming, laid by `stacks.now()` and again after `stacks.invalidate()` (a claim heard, a member's death): from the side's entry claims in its own `ClaimLedger` (`stacks.ledger`: unlimited range, the latest per speaker, current for `claim_ttl`, the NPCs' `tuning.radio.callout_ttl` read by `MatchBuilder.callout_ttl_of`; the human's `PlayerStack` among them), at each doorway `ClaimLedger.openings_stacking()` names, with `DoorStack.layout()` slots (`there`, `mine`), laid on the nav map and physics space of the squad's own world (the UI's stack markers draw them).
- A callout of its own side goes to `_hear_side`: it redraws the stacks, latches a go, rallies on the leader's own claim, and, while an AI leads, hands the callout to the ambush rule with the members it may order (`_free()`). The squad posts orders itself only for the rally's joins, the cancels and the ambush rule.

## Following (LD19 to LD23)

`follows(member)`: alive, the squad has a leader, not the leader, an NPC. Tunables are `SquadFollowConfig`'s (the same for every squad). `SquadFollow` lays the spots: `track` (in `_physics_process`) drops a trail point each `TRAIL_STEP` the leader moves, trimmed to what the file needs, remembering the rooms visited (`_visited`); a new leader starts it afresh. `follow_spot(member)` is `SquadFollow.spot` (`Vector2.INF` for a non-follower), laid at most once a physics frame and only when the leader moved, turned or stopped, or the followers changed (`_lay_spots`, in roster order):

- **moving:** on the trail at `gap + step * i` (`_trail_point`, snapped with `NavBuilder.snap`);
- **still** (after `still_time`): a slot from `_ring_slots` behind `_heading()`, on the map, in the leader's sight (`AgentVision.wall_hit`) and out of doorways;
- `_clear_of_way` then moves a follower out of the lane ahead of the leader's `facing` (own side first, else further ahead, only into visited rooms), kept in `_aside` until the leader has moved a ring or turned back.

**The leash (LD23).** Each tick the controller's reflexes refresh the NPC's confinement (`perception.md`) with `SquadFollowConfig.leash` round the leader's live position (`leash_center`). The planner's COMBAT filter (on the snapshot's frozen copy), the behaviour's fight, `Combat`'s spots and the leash break (`leash_break`) read it. The leash binds only a follower holding no order: `follows` stays true for a member carrying an order (its follow spot, its file place and the rally's choice of followers still count it), but a member defending a room, ambushing or in a stack (held or a join) fights where its order takes it, however far its leader is. The `follow` primitive is `behavior-policy.md`.

## Rally, abort cancel, takeover (LD24 to LD26)

`_on_event` passes an entry callout of the current leader's (same faction, any `entry_step`, not a grenade call, no `order_id`, no `join`: a follower that took over the lead holding a join rallies nobody, D30) to `_rally`; from the human only a claim marked `own` (`_own_claim`). On `claim`, once per `"<leader id>:<opening>"` (`_rallied`), one `stack` order with `mode` `JOIN` (not in `MODES`; `StackClaim.is_join(order)` tells it) goes to every follower whose `_orders` entry is empty (D24): `opening` / `into` the claim's, `pos` the doorway's centre, `wait_go` the claim's `human` (the human leader's rally is held for its go, D38; an AI leader's is not). On the leader's `abort` the key is erased and one `cancel` goes to the living members holding a join of that leader at that opening (D25). `_on_died`, when the dead member led and was the human, calls `_take_over(previous)`: one `cancel` to members holding a `defend_room`, an `ambush` or a join of `previous`'s rally (D38: no automatic go for a rally); held stacks it ordered are released with an `auto` go (D12).

## The defenders' ambush rule (LD29, D31 to D36)

`SquadAmbush` (`ai/team/squad_ambush.gd`; enabled by `roster.ambush_attacks`, set by `MatchBuilder.build_squads` for the defenders only, so the invaders' squad never lists its free members). `hear(data, free)` reads the threat point (`_threat_point`: a COMBAT callout's `target_pos`, an INVESTIGATE callout's `lead_pos`; INF for an entry step, an order's progress or a grenade call), the leader's room (`HouseRooms.key_at`) and the attack doorway (`AttackDoor.doorway(point, leader_room, rooms, openings)`, static, preloaded: "" for a point in the leader's room, at INF, a leader in no room, or no way through; else a Dijkstra over the doorways from the point's room, outside counting as a room its exterior doors join). `free` is `Squad._free()`: living NPC members holding no order, or one they radioed `refused` (D36), in spawn order, the leader included; `_recipients` drops the last non-leader when there are three or more (the rear guard, D34). It issues `{ kind: "ambush", to, opening, room }` through `squad.issue` when the doorway is new, and for a re-order only after `SquadAmbushConfig.reorder_gap` and with at least `min_recipients` (D35). `reset()` (a leader change) starts afresh; the dead leader's ambushes stand, and LD26's takeover cancel stays human-only.

Tests: see `team.md`; the ambush rule's are `tests/general/squad_ambush_rule*`, `tests/ai/attack_door_suite.gd` and `ai/combat/crew_ambush_attack_door`.
