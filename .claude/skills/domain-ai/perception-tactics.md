# AI · Perception · Tactics geometry (`agent_tactics.gd`, `throw_aim.gd`)

Pure queries over known contacts, the physics space (via the vision rays) and the nav map (`NavBuilder.snap` / `route` / `path_length`, interface 9). The flank behaviour they must deliver and its tests: `docs/flanking-behavior.md`. Tunables: `tuning.tactics`, `tuning.combat`.

- Whether the NPC is hidden from a hostile where it stands is worked out once per snapshot (`ctx.covered_from`), not per spot. Hostile fronts (`ctx.fronts`) are built once per `sense()` and serve both the flank frames and the watch test.
- **Flank sides** are measured in the target's own frame (its last-seen facing, else the side facing this NPC) and snapped to the navmesh. Exposure counts route samples the target can see; for a flank only samples inside its view cone (`watch_arc` round its front) count, so a route behind its back reads hidden. Each spot carries `crosses`, `held_by`, `exposed_to`, `heading_toward`, `ally_lane`; the snapshot turns them into the boolean tags the `flank` node's `drop_tags` drop (`decision.md`).
- **Route safety.** `_route_crosses` names a hostile within `route_clearance` of the route *and* nearer than the route's start (walking away from someone close is not passing them). `_watched_in_open` names a hostile that would catch the NPC in the open: looking toward the point with a clear line and no cover within a step, or, while the NPC is now hidden, looking along a mostly exposed route there. Fire spots and the ambush spot must pass both.
- **Fire spots** are sampled on rings round the NPC and round the target; the best per 8-way sector round the target is kept. `here` is never screened.
- **Retreat spots** (`retreat_positions`) come from known rooms, the cover spots on the ring round the NPC and a spot beside each ally (`_beside`). One is kept when it takes the NPC out of sight of every threat while it stands in sight of one, else when it lies a `STRIDE` farther from the nearest threat; never one a known character stands on (`TAKEN`) nor one the NPC already stands at. The snapshot's `retreat_group` then drops, for a held stack's member (the knowledge's `keep_out`, D38), every spot whose plain way back (`plain_enters`) to `_held_back_point` (`HELD_BACK` 48 px out of its order's doorway, away from the held room) runs through that room (test `ai/entry/order_stack_hold_retreat_stays_stack_side`).
- **`where_it_stands(ctx, p)`** (the walk to `p`'s navmesh point is under a `STRIDE`) screens the retreat spots and the snapshot's LOCATE and "where the shots came from" lead. Reason (issue #51 R12, `docs/tactical-entry.md` E9): a move that ends the tick it begins is offered and taken again every other frame. Tests: `ai/combat/retreat_never_where_it_stands`, `locate_never_where_it_stands`, `retreat_out_of_sight_goes_farther`.
- A character is never cover. A blocking object is named by its `object_name`, a character by its name, anything else "wall". `combat_spots` (ring round the NPC: fire vs cover) also serves the behaviour's peek-and-cover.
- A combat spot's `route_len` stays on the plain route; the snapshot's option route bands are the *walked* route (`perception-views.md`).

## Throw spots (`throw_spot(ctx, aim: ThrowAim, ex)`, issue #51 R13 / R15, `docs/grenades.md` G17)

Where to throw onto `aim.spot` from when the lob from where the NPC stands fails (out of range or not clear). `ThrowAim` carries the spot, the reach, `keep_out` (the room the hostile holds, or the shots' room), the `lob` test and the `route` callable.

- Candidates lie on rings round the NPC and round the spot; cheap tests run first (straight distance, snapped within `SNAP_TOLERANCE`, within reach, more than a `STRIDE` away, outside `keep_out`, the `lob` callable), then they sort hidden-from-the-spot, then covered, then nearest.
- Routes are measured only for the first `THROW_ROUTES`, through the `route` callable (`{ path, len, detour }`: the snapshot's `_throw_walk`, the walked route and whether the entry guard would stop it). A spot qualifies if the route reaches it, is at most `tuning.grenades.throw_spot_walk` and passes no known hostile. The pick is the first with no `detour`, then hidden, then covered, then shortest.
- Cost is ~150 snaps and lob tests plus up to ten routes, spent only on a throw the NPC cannot make where it stands.
