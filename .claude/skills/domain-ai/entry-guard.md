# AI entry: the guard, doorway views and held walks

The entry guard turns a walk into an entry, and the views keep walks out of a doorway's line of sight. Spec: `docs/tactical-entry.md` E2, E9, E15, E18 to E22. Code: `behavior_entries.gd` (`Behavior.entries`), `entry/entry_claim.gd`, `entry_approach.gd`, `entry_readiness.gd`, perception's `perception_views.gd`. Back to `entry.md`.

## The guard (E9)

`Behavior.entries.guard()` runs every tick after the primitive steered (a `move` not arrived, an `engage` and its repositions, an `interact` walk) and reads the walk the nav agent was just given (`Locomotion.path_ahead()`), not the order's destination. When `views.threshold_ahead()` finds a doorway on it into a room not cleared, within `tuning.entry_guard.reach` of walking, it starts a **detour**: the order is stashed whole in `entries.resume`, `task.primitive` becomes `entry` for that doorway, and `entries.finish()` puts the order back (refilling its commit timer) when the entry ends.

- **Exempt:** `tuning.entry_guard.enabled` or `tuning.entry.enabled` off; a leaf with `entry_guard: false` (RETREAT); an arrived `move`; a doorway whose entry was given up less than `rearm` ago (`entries._guard_off`); a doorway into the room a held stack's member keeps out of (D38, `entry-orders.md`); an NPC under fire, except for a doorway into the room the shots came from (`fight.into_the_shots` over `hearing.shots_from_room`, E9), which stops there as an entry (a bang one when the stack carries a stun, the room being suspect). Under fire it secures no view it passes.
- A new decision while a detour runs (`set_leaf`) replaces the paused order and leaves the entry running, unless the guard does not apply to it (`entries.guards(leaf, into)`: not a walk, a retreat, or under fire and not into the shots): then the detour is dropped and the entry abandoned (`abort`). Coming under fire before the crossing cancels the detour the same way (`entries._cancel_detour`); a crossing under way is finished. A decision in flight when the NPC became committed to its own ENTER crossing is ignored (`set_leaf` returns); `fallback()` abandons any entry.
- A member standing in a doorway of the room it keeps out of is not in it yet (`threshold_ahead` reads that room from the doorway's outer side; test `ai/entry/order_stack_hold_from_doorway`).
- Tests: `tests/ai/entry/entry_guard_*`, `entry_fallback_aborts`, `entry_decision_in_flight_during_cross`.

## Doorway views a walk crosses (E15, E18)

- The guard also looks for a doorway view the walk goes INTO without entering the room (`views.passing_ahead`: a doorway into a room not clear for passing, inside the house or out, exterior doors included), nearer than any doorway it crosses (`entries._passing_gate`), skipping one a teammate is working or that was just secured or given up. It meets only what `Locomotion` could not walk round.
- The detour is an `entry` with `secure: true`: no team, nothing radioed; it stacks beside the jamb, slices one side of the pie (the watch stage fires, E14 holds it on a hostile in view) and ends `secured` without crossing. `entries.finish` then leaves the doorway alone for `tuning.entry_guard.secure_time`.
- An ENTER order's own approach walk is guarded the same way (`entries._guard_approach`: abandon, secure, restart from `approach`), but not for a member heading for a stack others have claimed (`entry.stack(opening).team` not empty): the team goes to its door as one.
- **E18, keeping out:** `entries.keeps_out_of_views()` (set on `doors.avoid_doors` each tick by `errand.set_up_walk`) is true for a `move`, `engage` or `interact` without `entry_guard: false`, and for an entry's `approach` (which also keeps out of the room it enters and its own doorway's view, cleared or not), while both `enabled` switches hold (or it is an ordered stack's walk) and the NPC is not under fire or evading. The rest of an entry walks plain. `Locomotion` does the routing (`behavior-locomotion.md`).
- Tests: `tests/ai/entry/entry_guard_*`, `entry_corridor_doors_not_passed`, `entry_secure_exterior_door_then_cross`, `entry_pass_far_out_secured`.

## Stack mechanics

- **The stack moves up (E2).** `claim.move_up_now(delta)`, first in `readiness.tick_stack` every `MOVE_UP_EVERY` while the member stands within `StackClaim.SLOT_REACH` of its slot, has not begun the pie and is not mid move-up. The rule is the stack's (`DoorStack.move_up_vacancies(claim.me())`). The walk is the entry's: the nearest vacancy not in `claim._moved_for`, within `MOVE_UP_REACH`, and not while the holder's way to its slot runs through the step (`claim._latecomer_crosses`: the crossing, not the distance alone, so a holder following on the same lane is let in). It keeps its `claim_frame`, sets `member.move_up` (radioed until `say_ready` clears it); `DoorStack.slot_for` gives the latecomer the freed slot, and every reader's `DoorStack` counts the claim at the old slot meanwhile. Tests: `stack_move_up_waits_for_near_latecomer`, `stack_move_up_lets_follower_in`.
- **Split stack (E19).** A member whose way to the team's side crosses the doorway's view stacks on its own side; `claim.keep_slot` weighs the side again only when the team's side changes (`DoorStack.team_side`). Its place in its file is its slot less the claims ahead on the other side (`StackLayout.place`). The other side's front man (`readiness.slices_own_half`) slices its own half from `stack`, radios `ready` only then, and crosses after the point's `in`. Rear spots count from the point's side (`claim.lead_side`).
- **Tail and dead end (E20).** A tail member (`claim.tail()`: `StackLayout.is_tail`) says ready, holds for a held stack's go, then runs `readiness._tick_security`: it never crosses. A slot no dead end holds is never claimed.
- **Approach points.** An open-floor or tail slot is walked plain; a wall slot via a point `APPROACH_LEAD` past it along the wall, shortened by `LEAD_STEP` until valid and short of the next slot a teammate claims, so the walk never aims at a teammate's body. The approach is given up (`abort`) past `approach.cap`.

## Bodies on the way

Every phase walks through `Locomotion.move_to`, so a member goes round a teammate at rest, stops short of one on its slot and takes a free spot beside it, and gives way to a walking one. The crossing never stands in the funnel except while `crowd.holding()`. A walk a body seals and stays in re-plans (`Entry.way_blocked`, called by `Behavior._way_blocked` on `crowd.take_blocked`):

- The approach drops the waypoint it was walking to and each next one the hold seals too (`approach.drop_sealed`, `Locomotion.seals`), then walks into the slot unless it is sealed by a body between it and the slot (`approach._slot_sealed`): a holder within `PASS_CLEARANCE` of the slot is Locomotion's body on the goal, no seal (the walk goes to a free spot beside it); else `AgentPerception.entry.slot_reach(slot, bodies)` is 0 against `approach._standing_bodies()`. A map not baked yet answers -1, no verdict: the member keeps its slot and asks again at the next re-plan. A member walks round by the hall rather than give its slot up.
- With the slot sealed, a member the stack keeps from its slot takes the back of the stack (`claim.to_back`, `DoorStack.back_slot`, radioed as a new claim), and with none to take gives the claim up (`abort`; `Behavior` re-decides with "way blocked", or a detour hands the walk back to its order).
- A member held by a teammate of the same stack still on its way (`approach.held_by_forming`, under `HELD_BY_FORMING_CAP` of standing held) keeps its slot and waits (`forming_wait`). Two members holding each other wait out the cap.
- A pie step is skipped; the thrower gives the bang up; a crossing waits out the first signal, then gives the entry up if still outside the wall plane. E13's capacity counts a member's place in the stack, not its slot number, since a member that took the back leaves its old slot empty.
- Tests: `entry_held_*`, `ai/stack/stack_ai_goes_round_player_in_slot`, `ai/movement/npc_dest_taken_stops_nearby`.
