# AI · Perception · Grenades (`agent_grenades.gd`, `agent_throw.gd`, `perception_grenades.gd`)

What the NPC knows of live grenades and the plan to get clear of them. Spec: `docs/grenades.md` G15, G16. The behaviour half (running the plan, the throw) is `behavior.md` (the evade reflex) and `combat.md`; the GRENADE and STUN ENTRY options are `snapshot-wording.md`. Tunables: `tuning.grenades` (`enabled`, `hear_grenade`, `evade_margin`, `blind_fire_chance`, `throw_spot_walk`).

## What it knows (`grenades.known()`)

A live grenade is a node of the group `live_grenades` (Items: `kind`, `fuse_left()`, `throw_stats()`, `thrower`). The NPC knows one by **sight** (`observe`, only while the group is non-empty: view cone and clear sight line), by **hearing** it come down (`hear`, a `&"grenade"` bounce or rest within `hear_grenade`, not deaf), by an ally's **callout** (`called`), or because it threw it (**own**: always known, blind or not). A grenade keeps the source it was first known by. It is forgotten at the burst (`&"explosion"`, or the node leaving the group). `process_explosion` keeps `grenades.last_burst` (`{ kind, position, frame, team }`: a bang entry waits for its stun, and an entry goes in on its own team's burst, E17) and marks the hostiles a stun caught as `flashed` (memory topic `flashed`), which the contact view reads as `blind`.

Its own grenade (`_note_own`) is known at once; until a bounce its spot is where it was aimed (`Character.weapon.aim_target()`, clamped to the kind's range), not the hand it left.

## The zone and the safe spot (`agent_throw.gd`)

- The cover rule is the blast's own, asked of the Projectile System so the AI keeps no copy: callers use `Explosion.shields` and `Explosion.first_stop` (interface 3). A wall always stops the line; furniture stops it when its `coverage` exceeds the body's height (`movement.posture_coverage`, `docs/cover-and-stance.md`); characters stop nothing. For a stun, furniture shields only a body within `flash_cover_reach` of it.
- `escape(space, map, threat: GrenadeThreat, runner: GrenadeRunner, exclude)` returns `{ spot, crouch, route }`. Candidates: the NPC's own position first (already shielded: no move), the circle just outside the zone, the shadows behind what rays from the burst meet, rings round the NPC, and a probe against the far face of each stopper. Each is snapped to the navmesh, judged (outside, shielded standing, or shielded only crouched = `crouch`), **every** survivor routed (`NavBuilder.route`: a straight line says nothing, a spot behind a wall can be a house away), and the shortest route within `(fuse_left - margin) × run speed` wins; `spot` is `Vector2.INF` when none is in time. A route ending short of its spot (a ring past a closed arena's wall) drops it (`evade_stun_turns_away_test.gd`). Run speed accounts for stamina, a stun and a heavy outfit (`throw_timing_counts_slowed_walk_test.gd`).
- **Pruned routes.** `_nearest_in_time` routes the safe spots nearest first by straight line (candidate order among equals) and stops past both the best route and the distance it may run in time (a route is never shorter than its straight line): the answer is the one routing every spot gives, ties included. `escape_routes_measured` counts the routes (`escape_pruned_matches_full_test.gd`).
- `has_escape(...)` is the cornered / `no_safe_spot` question only: it runs the same candidates, snapping, safety verdicts, route endpoint and time rules as `escape`, deduplicates routes by snapped point and returns at the first reachable in-time safe spot. Its boolean exactly equals whether the full `escape` found a spot; the evade plan still uses full `escape` to choose and run the shortest route. `escape_route_queries` observes the early exit (`escape_spot_matches_plain_test.gd`).
- **Shared on a physics frame.** What depends on the burst alone is the same for every NPC fleeing it, and an evade wave is 4 to 8 NPCs asking on one frame, so `escape` shares the candidates round the burst, each spot's verdict and each candidate's navmesh spot in static dictionaries keyed by space, burst, kind, margins, heights and the excluded bodies, cleared when a new physics frame begins. Outside a physics frame (`Engine.is_in_physics_frame()` false) nothing is kept. Only `[from]`, the ring round it and the routes are the NPC's own. Pins: `escape_spot_matches_plain_test.gd`, `evade_wave_spots_match_own_plan_test.gd`; the plain search lives in `tests/_ai/grenade_ai_probe.gd`.
- `can_lob` is the throw test of the GRENADE tactic, the throw's re-check and the bang: within range and `throw_clear`, a lob from where the grenade leaves the hand (`Character.inventory.throw_release_offset`, learned with the carried grenades) with a band either side of the line clear of anything taller than the release height; a centre line that grazes a jamb is a grenade that strikes it. `throw_clear_on_arrival` tests the spot a thrower will walk to, with the walk's arrival slack. `reach_of(kind)` is the range less a slack: how far from its spot a thrower lets go.
- **What it knows of a grenade it carries.** No copy of the tuning: `learn(stats)` takes `throw_stats` from `Character.inventory.throw_stats(slot)` of every carried grenade (each `observe`) and from each live grenade (interface 6/7), keeps it by kind so it outlives the last thrown, and answers `range_of`, `radius_of`, `fuse_of`, `max_blind_of`, `cover_reach` (0 or INF for a kind never met). `PerceptionGrenades.carried(character)` lists the kinds with a count, a frag first (`ItemRegistry.FRAG_SLOT` / `STUN_SLOT`).

## The plan (`evade_plan()`)

For the known grenade that goes off first among those the NPC is in the zone of: `{ id, point, kind, radius, spot, crouch, fuse_left, landed, lies }`. Made on first entering a zone, again when the grenade moved (in flight, a bounce) or any distance that leaves the spot in its line (`_still_safe`, test `evade_replans_when_spot_in_line`), at most every `REPLAN_EVERY` seconds, and kept while the grenade lives, so the NPC holds the spot until the burst. Replanning counts `GameClock` time, not wall-clock (a pause does not run it on). A frag entry's stack reads `point` and `lies` (`Entry.expects_burst`, LD14). The callout waits until the grenade has come down (`announcements()`, the spot it landed at) and repeats once if it rests far from there; an ally's call or the NPC's own throw is not radioed again.

**The per-frame cap.** At most `AgentGrenades.ESCAPE_CAP` (3) `escape` searches run on one physics frame across every NPC (`_escape_allowed`: statics, reset each physics frame), so which NPC waits is the order they ask in. An NPC with no plan yet over the cap gets none (`plan()` returns {}) and asks again next frame: ten caught by one frag plan over four frames (G16). Fresh plans go first (`plan()` asks for those without a plan first): a replan takes at most `ESCAPE_CAP - 1` of a frame's searches, none on a frame a fresh plan was refused on or the one before, and keeps its old plan meanwhile (one asked for earlier than the refused fresh plan has already run that frame). `has_escape` is not capped; a stack member whose slot is its cover (`Entry.expects_burst`) still plans and takes a search. Pin: `escape_cap_spreads_plans_test.gd`.

## Wrappers for the behaviour (`perception_grenades.gd`)

`can_lob(at, to, kind, walked := false)`, `throw_clear`, `reach`, `fuse`, `in_zone(kind, burst, p)` (every character's body excluded: the frag entry's slot check, `docs/leadership.md` D16), `evade_plan`, `known`, `announcements`. `AgentThrow` is also the geometry `SnapshotThrows.grenade_group` uses. Tests: `tests/ai/grenade/`.

## Stunned and deaf (G15)

Blind: no new sighting and no new decision (it re-decides the tick sight returns), its contacts kept. One roll as it goes blind against `tuning.grenades.blind_fire_chance` decides whether it fires at the last-known spot (aim sway applies) or holds fire (`Combat.holds_fire`); the roll comes from the reflexes' own RNG, seeded at bind from the character's name and spawn spot (a re-stun while blind does not reroll). Deaf: the controller drops what it would have heard (`perception.md`).
