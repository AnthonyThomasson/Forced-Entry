# AI · Decision (`scenes/ai/decision/`)

THINK: the decision policy as data, and the only place the AI talks to the outside model. Von is a stateless single-shot ranker that scores every question independently, so a drill-down is **sequential requests, one per level**, each conditioned on the path chosen so far. The knowledge a walk decides over is built by `snapshot.md`; the chosen leaf is run by `behavior.md`.

## Files

- `decision_tree.gd` — `const NODES`, data only: the node fields, the shape, each node's `desc` / `question` / `context`.
- `decision_planner.gd` — the walk (a `Node`): gating, entry rules, per-level state, Von calls. Its leaf `decision_walk.gd` (`DecisionWalk`) holds the walk in flight.
- `decision_budget.gd` (`DecisionBudget`); the Von files are `decision-von.md`'s.

## The tree

Broad mode → tactic → option → primitive: ORDER, COMBAT (engage, flank, grenade, advance, melee, reload, retreat, locate), FOLLOW, RELOAD_QUIET, INVESTIGATE (+ STAY), SEARCH (+ ENTER, STUN ENTRY), IDLE (use, go, wait). Adding a mode or tactic is adding a node, plus a snapshot option group (`snapshot.md`) when it needs new places.

- `options` names snapshot option groups, looked up under the bound target first, then globally. A node whose groups end up empty (every flank side dropped) has no leaf and is not offered. `drop_tags` drops options carrying those tags. `requires` gates on facts (`!` negates; a bound name counts as true): `engage` and `flank` require `gun_loaded` (an empty gun is never "a clear shot"); `advance`, `melee` and `retreat` stay so an empty NPC can still close in, punch or fall back.
- `params` merge under each option's own. `run: true` lets the behaviour sprint (Von never sees it); `run_through_fire: true` on retreat lets it sprint with a shot available (every other walk drops to a walk to take it, `docs/gaze-and-fire.md` W6); `entry_guard: false` on retreat exempts it from the entry guard that sends every other walk into an uncleared room through a tactical entry first.
- A level with one option is taken without asking Von (ORDER, FOLLOW and the `reload` nodes rely on it), except INVESTIGATE (`ask`): whether a lead is worth going to is Von's call.
- **Staying in the house is Von's call, guided by the prompt, not a rule.** A territorial goal ("hold your ground inside the house; do not chase anyone outside") is in every state, the state and leads say where the gunfire or hostile is, and INVESTIGATE's `stay` child (offered while a lead lies outside and the NPC is inside: fact `lead_outside`) is Von's way to decline. Its desc must state the situation in the state's own words and echo the goal's sentence: a desc that names no situation loses to the lead under fire and makes invaders hold too. Nothing in the tree or code knows who the NPC is. Reword the desc, then re-run `tools/von_probe.py` (`investigate_outside_lead*`) and the Von tests in `tests/ai/investigate/` together. `tuning.decision.defend_territory` confines COMBAT only.
- **ENTER** offers the doorways into rooms unseen or unsearched (`entries`, `tuning.entry.enabled`); the question asks for "the shortest route" (a looser phrase lets "unexplored" outweigh a short route). Von chooses only whether and which doorway; the rest is the behaviour's `entry` primitive (`entry.md`), which asks Von nothing on the cadence until it is over. A forced decision (a hit, a belief change) still starts and replaces the entry, or with Von down aborts it.
- **GRENADE** (`docs/grenades.md` G17): requires `grenade_ready` only, so the unbound level offers it too (the shots-origin group, beside RETREAT). Its desc is "{situation} and you can throw a grenade onto their spot ({summary})"; the planner's `_fill` takes `{situation}` from the summary group. Wording: `snapshot-wording.md`. Runs the `throw` primitive (`combat.md`).
- **STUN ENTRY** (G18): requires `stack_has_stun`; the same `entries` group and `entry` primitive as ENTER with `bang: true`. Its desc and the suspect-room phrases are measured: a desc naming two reasons ties ENTER, one naming every reason bangs plain rooms.
- **How a `desc` must read.** It states the SITUATION in which the branch is right, in the phrases the snapshot writes into the state, plus specifics in parentheses (ENGAGE: "You have a clear shot at {target}, and you are not badly wounded ({summary})"). No action labels (they pull toward the goal's verbs), no negated attributes, no option repeating a dominated choice (drop it). Template fields: `{gun}`, `{summary}`, `{situation}`, `{blinded}`.

## The planner (`decision_planner.gd`)

- Config pushed in by the controller: `disabled_nodes`, `entry_rules` (ordered fact → start node), `skip_single_option`; all re-pushed each decision except `node_overrides`, which is baked in at `setup()`. `pursue_hostiles` maps to `idle` disabled + `hostile_known → combat`. The entry rule `ordered → order` sits after `under_fire` / `engaged` and before `hostile_known`. A follower disables `investigate`, `search` and `idle` and adds `follows → follow` after `hostile_known` (LD20), so with no fight or order its walk starts at the FOLLOW leaf.
- `begin(snapshot, continuing)` starts a walk at the first entry rule whose fact holds and whose node has a leaf, else `root` (an empty entry node falls back to `root`; an empty root fails). When `continuing` (the behaviour's last choice is still running) the option continuing it reads "(your current plan)": never a finished one, or Von re-picks a completed tactic. A bind level with nothing to bind (under fire, shooter unseen) falls through to the node's unbound children (RETREAT / LOCATE). The state is the goal + the node's `context` sections (the bound target's own first) + "Decided so far: COMBAT → Intruder → FLANK."
- **The confinement filter.** Where the NPC may act is no config of the planner's: it reads `snapshot["confinement"]` (`_confinement()`), perception's confinement frozen as the snapshot was built (`perception.md`), so a walk decides over one state and a hand-made snapshot drives it. Inside a `confine` subtree (COMBAT) `_allowed` drops every option `allows_option` refuses, judged where it acts (`params.from`, else `params.point`), so a territorial defender may throw from inside onto a spot outside. The planner reads no `tags.inside` and adds no confinement to the leaf's params; behaviour asks the live one. `summary_inside` fills `{summary}` while `inside_only` is on in a confined subtree. An order refused as the walk begins (LD6) leaves a copy with no room: the controller refreshes the confinement and replaces the snapshot's copy before the walk starts. Test: `tests/ai/decision/combat_options_within_confinement_test.gd` (hand-made snapshot with `DecisionStub`).
- **ORDER** (LD6): its one option is the snapshot's `order` group, taken unasked; the option names its own `primitive` (`_finish` takes an option's `primitive` over the node's). The `order` section shows after the goal at every level.
- `signal decided(leaf)`: `{ path, labels, primitive, params (node params ⊕ option params + target_id) }`. `signal failed()`: transport failure or nothing to choose; the controller wires it to the behaviour's `fallback()`.
- `entry_for(facts)` / `current_entry()` let the controller interrupt a walk when the situation now calls for a different start. `debug_state()` carries per level enough to replay offline (state, question, offered, Von's probabilities and pick) plus the `confinement` view the walk decided over.

## The budget (`decision_budget.gd`)

A snapshot is the costliest thing an NPC does in a frame, and NPCs that spawned or came under fire together want to decide on the same frame. The controller claims `DecisionBudget.claim()` before `_start_decision` and before restarting a walk: `per_frame` (1) snapshots start per physics frame. A refused NPC keeps its wish and asks again next frame. The limit is a count, not a time, so when an NPC decides never depends on the machine, and two NPCs that would claim the same flank side decide on different frames, each hearing the other's radio first. Test: `tests/ai/decision/decisions_spread_over_frames_test.gd`.
